  <p>Rigzone is hosting a Bakken Oil &amp; Gas Career Fair from 11 a.m. to 3 p.m. on Wednesday, June 26, 2013 at the Grand International Hotel in Minot, North Dakota.</p><p>Oil and gas professionals will be able to speak with recruiting representatives from industry leading companies.</p><p>Pre-registration is recommended at <a href="http://www.targetedjobfairs.com" target="_blank">www.dicecareerfairs.com</a> where attendees will be able to find a list of participating companies in the weeks leading up to the event.</p><h3>Admission is free and the Grand International Hotel is located at 1505 North Broadway in Minot, North Dakota.</h3><h2><strong>About Rigzone </strong></h2><p>Rigzone, a Dice Holdings Inc. service, is a leading online resource for the oil and gas industry that delivers content, data, advertising and career services. Dedicated to bringing upstream oil and gas news and data, including in-depth information on exploration, drilling and production markets to organizations tackling the energy challenge, its online community of highly-skilled and experienced energy professionals is unmatched.</p><p>The article above was published through BakkenShale.com`s press release distribution service. </p>
