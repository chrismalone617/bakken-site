  <p>The Williston Spring Job Fair that will be held on March 28, 2013, doesn't have room for more employers, but job seekers should hit the road to attend the event. More than 100 companies will be on hand taking applications and meeting prospective employees.</p><p>The job fair starts at 9 am and will run until 7 pm at the:</p><p>Airport International Inn 3602 2nd Ave. W. Williston, ND</p><p>A Veterans preference is from 9 am to 10 am and all candidates should be prepared to interview. Bring I-9 id, CDL &amp; MVR, and copies of your resume.</p><p>A wide range of companies will be represented:</p><ul><li>ACME</li><li>Aflac</li><li>Bakken Staffing</li><li>BNSF Railway</li><li>Cummin NPower, LLC</li><li>DIRECTV</li><li>Ferrellgas</li><li>Hertz Equipment Rental</li><li>Key Energy</li><li>Nabors Drilling</li><li>Precision Drilling</li><li>Schlumberger</li><li>United Rentals</li><li>And Many More</li></ul>
