<div data-sqsp-text-block-content="">
  <p>It's well documented that North Dakota has the lowest unemployment rate in the country. A new report by the ND Department of Commerce provides details on why.</p><p>ND has added more than 100,000 jobs since 2000 or, put another way, has grown employment from 327,700 to 429,800 workers (30+%). There's no doubt, <a href="http://jobs.bakkenshale.com/">Bakken job</a> growth has played a significant role.</p>
</div>
