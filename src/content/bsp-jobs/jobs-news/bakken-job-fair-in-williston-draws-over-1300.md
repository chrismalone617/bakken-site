  <p>The <a href="http://bakkenshale.com/bsp-jobs/jobs-news/williston-spring-job-fair-employer-spots-sold-out-march-28-201">Bakken job fair</a> held yesterday in Williston was sold out to employers and the number of job applicants that came didn't disappoint. Local officials only expected 600-700 people, but more than 1,300 came through the doors while the show was open.</p><p>Some of the job seekers even spent the night in their car to make sure they were one of the first in the door.</p><p>With the success of this event, I'm going out on a limb and going to say I bet we see more job fairs in the near future.</p><p>If you're looking now, visit our <a href="http://jobs.bakkenshale.com/">Bakken Jobs Board</a>.</p>
