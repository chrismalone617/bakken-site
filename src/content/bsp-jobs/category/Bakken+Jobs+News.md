  <p><strong>Reliable, Low Cost Flare Gas Metering for Emissions Reporting</strong>
Pennsylvania-based Thermal Instrument Company, a 55 year old designer and manufacturer&nbsp;of industrial precision made thermal mass flow meters, has recently launched its flare gas flow measurement system&nbsp;for use in the upstream oil &amp; gas exploration areas. This flare gas flow metering package brings companies into&nbsp;mandated monitoring compliance on flared emissions as stated by state and federal regulatory agencies. This&nbsp;Greenhouse Gas (GHG) Reporting Rule, 40 CFR Part 98, Subpart W is pertinent to reporting requirements and data&nbsp;collection for the upstream oil &amp; gas exploration industry.</p>
<p>In March, 2014, the U.S. EPA published some proposed revisions to this 40 CFR Part 98, Subpart W to include&nbsp;general applicability, revised calculation methods and reporting requirements for specified emission sources within the&nbsp;source category, and some other changes to monitoring standards. Additionally, a Subpart OOOO (Quad O) has&nbsp;become another EPA standard for upstream &amp; midstream gas exploration emissions compliance that must be met.</p>
<p>There are a small handful of oil &amp; gas exploration companies taking the proactive approach early on to engineer&nbsp;the flow monitoring instrumentation onto the well pad site for monitoring the mass flow exiting the flare. At this point,&nbsp;the EPA has defined January 1, 2015 as a technically feasible date when the proposed revisions will be effective for&nbsp;this mandated monitoring and recording of flow exiting the flares at oil &amp; gas exploration well sites.</p>
<p>In cooperation with a major gas exploration company in the Texas area of the Eagle Ford Shale formation, Thermal&nbsp;Instrument Company enhanced and completed their total flow monitoring system. Even with changes in gas&nbsp;composition, the flow monitoring system from Thermal Instrument Company was very repeatable and accurate and&nbsp;the customer was amazed at the less than five minute start-up time. The system can vary from just the flow&nbsp;measurement design to the whole package including solar panels, totalization module, and battery management&nbsp;hardware.</p>
<p>For more information on this flare gas flow system, contact Thermal Instrument Company at (215) 355-8400.</p>
