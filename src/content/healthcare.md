  <p class="text-xl text-slate-600 mb-6">
    The Bakken oil & gas boom has brought a flood of industry personnel to the ND/MT area, increasing the need for healthcare services in response to illness or injury. Oilfield injuries range from major trucking accidents requiring emergency medical care to minor incidents often covered by workers' compensation.
  </p>
  <p class="mb-8">
    Bakken area healthcare providers include the hospitals and urgent care clinics listed below. These facilities serve oil & gas industry personnel whether for work or non-work related injuries.
  </p>
