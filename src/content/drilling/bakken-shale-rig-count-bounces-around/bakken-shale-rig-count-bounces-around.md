<div data-sqsp-text-block-content="">
  <p>The <a href="http://bakkenshale.com/drilling">Bakken-Three Forks rig count</a>&nbsp;continues to bounce around and landed lower this week. Baker Hughes reporting 42 rigs running across our coverage area by midday Friday.</p><p>In recent Bakken Shale news,&nbsp;President Donald Trump formally resurrected the Keystone XL Pipeline by granting a permit for construction to continue and offering help to speed the process. This marks a reversal from the previous administration, when Barak Obama worked to block the controversial pipeline.</p><p>Read more:&nbsp;<a href="https://bakkenshale.com/bsp-news/lawmakers-side-with-trump-on-keystone">ND Lawmakers Side with Trump on Keystone</a></p><p><em>A total of <strong>824</strong>&nbsp;oil and gas rigs were running across the United States this week, an increase of 15 over than last week.&nbsp;<strong>160</strong>&nbsp;rigs targeted natural gas (five more than the previous week) and <strong>662</strong>&nbsp;were targeting oil in the U.S. (10 more than the previous week). The remainder were drilling service wells (e.g. disposal wells, injection wells, etc.)</em>&nbsp;&nbsp;<em><strong>42</strong>&nbsp;of the rigs active in the U.S. were running in North Dakota.</em></p><h2>Bakken Oil &amp; Gas Rigs</h2><p>Bakken oil rigs were at 42 this week with Bloomberg reporting WTI oil prices rising to $50.68 on Friday afternoon.&nbsp;The Bakken has zero natural active gas rig in the area this week with futures trading at $3.19/mmbtu by midday.</p><p>McKenzie County leads production in the region with 18 rigs running this week. View the full list below under the <em>Bakken Drilling by County</em>&nbsp;section.</p><p>Activity continues to be dominated by horizontal drilling:</p><ul><li>41 rigs are drilling horizontal wells</li><li>0 rigs are drilling directional wells</li><li>1 rigs are drilling vertical wells</li></ul><h2>Rig Count by County</h2>
