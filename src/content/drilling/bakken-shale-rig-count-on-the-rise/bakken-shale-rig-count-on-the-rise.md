<div data-sqsp-text-block-content="">
  <p>The <a href="http://bakkenshale.com/drilling">Bakken-Three Forks rig count</a>&nbsp;continued to rise this week, with Baker Hughes reporting 52 rigs running through our coverage area by Friday midday.&nbsp;&nbsp;</p><p><em>A total of <strong>941</strong>&nbsp;oil and gas rigs were running across the United States this week, an increase of eight over last week.&nbsp;<strong>183</strong>&nbsp;rigs targeted natural gas (three less than the previous week) and <strong>758</strong>&nbsp;were targeting oil in the U.S. (11 more than the previous week). The remainder were drilling service wells (e.g. disposal wells, injection wells, etc.) <strong>52</strong> of the rigs active in the U.S. were running in North Dakota.</em></p><h2>Bakken Oil &amp; Gas Rigs</h2><p>Bakken oil rigs were at 52 this week with Bloomberg reporting WTI oil prices at $43.01 on Friday afternoon.&nbsp;The Bakken has zero natural active gas rig in the area this week with futures trading at $2.93/mmbtu by midday.</p><p>McKenzie County leads production in the region with 21 rigs running this week. View the full list below under the <em>Bakken Drilling by County</em>&nbsp;section.</p><p>Activity continues to be dominated by horizontal drilling:</p><ul><li>51 rigs are drilling horizontal wells</li><li>1 rigs are drilling directional wells</li><li>0 rigs are drilling vertical wells</li></ul><h2>Rig Count by County</h2>
