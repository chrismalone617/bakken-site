<div data-sqsp-text-block-content="">
  <p>The <a href="/drilling">Bakken-Three Forks rig count</a>&nbsp;saw an increase this week this week, with Baker Hughes reporting 53 rigs running through our coverage area by Friday midday.</p><p><em>A total of <strong>944</strong>&nbsp;oil and gas rigs were running across the United States this week, four more over last two weeks.&nbsp;<strong>187</strong>&nbsp;rigs targeted natural gas ( an increase of seven) and <strong>756</strong>&nbsp;were targeting oil in the U.S. (decreased by three). The remainder were drilling service wells (e.g. disposal wells, injection wells, etc.)</em><em>&nbsp;<strong>53 </strong>of the rigs active in the U.S. were running in North Dakota.</em></p><h2>Bakken Oil &amp; Gas Rigs</h2><p>53 rigs targetted oil in the Bakken this week with Bloomberg reporting WTI oil prices at $47.48 on Friday afternoon.&nbsp;The Bakken has zero natural active gas rig in the area this week with futures trading at $2.89/mmbtu by midday.</p><p>McKenzie County leads activity in the region with 23 rigs running this week. View the full list below under the <em>Bakken Drilling by County</em>&nbsp;section.</p><p>Activity continues to be dominated by horizontal drilling:</p><ul><li>52 rigs are drilling horizontal wells</li><li>1 rigs are drilling directional wells</li><li>0 rigs are drilling vertical wells</li></ul><h2>Rig Count by County</h2>

The rigs referred to in this article are for ALL drilling reported by Baker Hughes and not solely wells targeting the Bakken formation.
