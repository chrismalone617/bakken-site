  <p>The <a href="/drilling">Bakken-Three Forks rig count</a>&nbsp;fell this week, with Baker Hughes reporting 43 rigs running across our coverage area by midday Friday.</p><p><a href="https://www.facebook.com/BakkenShale/">Connect with us on Facebook</a></p><p><em>A total of <strong>877</strong>&nbsp;oil and gas rigs were running across the United States this week, an increase of seven over than last week.&nbsp;<strong>173</strong>&nbsp;rigs targeted natural gas (two more than the previous week) and 703 were targeting oil in the U.S. (six more than the previous week). The remainder were drilling service wells (e.g. disposal wells, injection wells, etc.)</em>&nbsp;<em><strong>43</strong>&nbsp;of the rigs active in the U.S. were running in North Dakota.</em></p><h2>Bakken Oil &amp; Gas Rigs</h2><p>Bakken oil rigs were at 43 this week with Bloomberg reporting WTI oil prices dipping to $46.22 on Friday afternoon.&nbsp;The Bakken has zero natural active gas rig in the area this week with futures trading at $3.27/mmbtu by midday.</p><p>McKenzie County leads production in the region with 23 rigs running this week. View the full list below under the <em>Bakken Drilling by County</em>&nbsp;section.</p><p>Activity continues to be dominated by horizontal drilling:</p><ul><li>43 rigs are drilling horizontal wells</li><li>0 rigs are drilling directional wells</li><li>0 rigs are drilling vertical wells</li></ul><h2>Rig Count by County</h2>
