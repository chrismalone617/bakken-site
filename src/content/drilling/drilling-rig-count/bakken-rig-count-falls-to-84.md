  <p>The <a href="/drilling/">Bakken-Three Forks rig count</a> dropped by five to <strong>84</strong> rigs running across our coverage area by midday Friday.</p><p>In recent Bakken news, the National Transportation Safety Board (NTSB) is urging tougher standards for oil tankers carrying Bakken crude.</p><p>Read more: <a href="/bsp-news/news/crude-by-rail-facing-tougher-standards?rq=Crude%20by%20Rail%20Facing%20Tougher%20Standards">Crude by Rail Facing Tougher Standards</a></p><p><em>The U.S. rig count fell another 34 to </em><em>954</em><em> rigs running as of today. A total of 217 rigs were targeting natural gas (a decrease of eight &nbsp;from the previous week) and </em><em>734</em><em> were targeting oil in the U.S. (26 less than the previous week). The remainder were drilling service wells (e.g. disposal wells, injection wells, etc.)</em><strong>84 </strong>rigs are running in the Williston Basin across MT, ND, and SD. <strong>83 </strong>are in ND alone.</p><h2><strong>Bakken Oil &amp; Gas Rigs</strong></h2><p>By midday Friday, the Bakken oil rigs dropped to 84 rigs. &nbsp;WTI oil prices increased $4.10 from the previous week, trading at $55.74/bbl on Friday afternoon. The WTI-Brent saw an increase of $5.58 by week’s end, landing at $63.45. There are currently no natural active gas rigs in the area and futures are trading at $2.63/mmbtu.</p><p>McKenzie County continues to lead development this week but fell to 36 running rigs. Williams (17) and Mountrail (14) counties are the next closest producers. View the full list below under the <em>Bakken Drilling by County</em> section.</p><p>Activity continues to be dominated by horizontal drilling:</p><ul><li>83 rigs are drilling horizontal wells</li><li>1 rigs are drilling directional wells</li><li>0 rigs are drilling vertical wells</li></ul><p><a href="http://jobs.bakkenshale.com/">Be sure to visit our Bakken Job Listings to search openings and come back weekly for updates or sign up for alerts - Daily or Weekly Email Alerts</a></p><h2><strong>Bakken Drilling by County</strong></h2>
