  <p>The <a href="http://bakkenshale.com/drilling/">Bakken-Three Forks rig count</a>&nbsp;fell this week to <strong>57</strong>&nbsp;rigs running across our coverage area by end of the week on Friday.</p><p>In recent Bakken news, the region's man-camps are fighting for their existence after Williston City voted to let them die.</p><p>Read more:&nbsp;<a href="http://bakkenshale.com/bsp-news/news/williston-votes-to-evict-man-camps?rq=Williston%20Votes%20to%20Evict%20Man%20Camps">Williston Votes to Evict Man Camps</a></p><p><em>A total of 709 oil and gas rigs were running across the United States this week. 183 were targeting natural gas (nine less than the previous week) and 524 were targeting oil in the U.S. (21 less than the previous week). The remainder were drilling service wells (e.g. disposal wells, injection wells, etc.)</em><strong><em> 57</em></strong>are in ND alone.</p><h2><strong>Bakken Oil &amp; Gas Rigs</strong></h2><p>The Bakken oil rigs were at 57 this week with Bloomberg reporting WTI oil prices trading at $35.62 on Friday afternoon. WTI-Brent decreased $5.07landing at $37.93 The Bakken has zero natural active gas rig in the area this week with futures decreasing to $1.99/mmbtu by midday.</p><p>McKenzie County continues to lead development with 26 rigs running this week, far outpacing other counties. View the full list below under the <em>Bakken Drilling by County</em> section.</p><p>Activity continues to be dominated by horizontal drilling:</p><ul><li>55 rigs are drilling horizontal wells</li><li>1 rigs are drilling directional wells</li><li>1 rigs are drilling vertical wells</li></ul><p><a href="http://jobs.bakkenshale.com/">Be sure to visit our Bakken Job Listings to search openings and come back weekly for updates or sign up for alerts - Daily or Weekly Email Alerts</a></p><h2><strong>Bakken Drilling by County</strong></h2>
