  <p>The <a href="/drilling/">Bakken-Three Forks rig count</a>&nbsp;fell this week with <strong>24</strong>&nbsp;rigs running across our coverage area by midday Friday.</p><p><em>A total of <strong>405</strong>&nbsp;oil and gas rigs were running across the United States this week, down nine from last week. <strong>87 </strong>were targeting natural gas (one more than the previous week) and <strong>318 </strong>were targeting oil in the U.S. (10 less than the previous week). The remainder were drilling service wells (e.g. disposal wells, injection wells, etc.)</em><strong><em>24 </em></strong><em>of the rigs active in the U.S. were running in North Dakota.</em></p><h2><strong>Bakken Oil &amp; Gas Rigs</strong></h2><p>Bakken oil rigs fell to 24 this week with Bloomberg reporting WTI oil prices rising to $46.21 on Friday afternoon. WTI-Brent also gained some, increasing this week to $47.83.&nbsp;&nbsp;The Bakken has zero natural active gas rig in the area this week with futures trading to $2.10/mmbtu by midday.</p><p>McKenzie County continues to lead development with 12 rigs running this week, far outpacing other counties. View the full list below under the <em>Bakken Drilling by County</em> section.</p><p>Activity continues to be dominated by horizontal drilling:</p><ul><li>24 rigs are drilling horizontal wells</li><li>0 rigs are drilling directional wells</li><li>0 rigs are drilling vertical wells</li></ul><p><a href="http://jobs.bakkenshale.com/">Be sure to visit our Bakken Job Listings to search openings and come back weekly for updates or sign up for alerts - Daily or Weekly Email Alerts</a></p><h2><strong>What is the Rig Count?</strong></h2><p>The Bakken Shale Rig Count is an index of the total number of oil &amp; gas drilling rigs running across Montana and North Dakota. The rigs referred to in this article are for ALL drilling reported by Baker Hughes and not solely wells targeting the Bakken formation. All land rigs and onshore rig data shown here are based upon industry estimates provided by the Baker Hughes Rig Count.</p><p>Read more at <a target="_blank" href="http://bakerhughes.com/">bakerhughes.com</a></p>
