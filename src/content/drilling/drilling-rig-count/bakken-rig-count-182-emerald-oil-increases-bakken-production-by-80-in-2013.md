<div data-sqsp-text-block-content="">
  <p>The <a href="/drilling/">Bakken-Three Forks rig count</a>&nbsp;increased by 7 to 182 rigs running over the past week. The NDIC notes around 190 rigs are active in North Dakota, but around 14 of those are in the process of moving in and rigging up.</p><p>In recent Bakken news,&nbsp;Emerald’s total production increased by 80% in 2013 over 2012 from ~935 boe/d to ~1690 boe/d. The production increase was attributable to the addition of 10.58 net operated Bakken and Three Forks wells in 2013.</p><p><strong>Read more</strong>: <a href="/bsp-news/news/emerald-oil-increases-bakken-production-by-80-in-2013?rq=Emerald%20Oil%20Increases%20Bakken%20Production%20by%2080%25%20in%202013">Emerald Oil Increases Bakken Production by 80% in 2013</a></p><p><em>The U.S. rig count decreased by 17 to 1809 rigs running over the past week. A total of 344 rigs are targeting natural gas (1 less than last week) and 1,461 are targeting oil in the U.S.&nbsp;(18 more than last week).&nbsp;The remainder are drilling service wells (e.g. disposal wells, injection wells, etc.).&nbsp;&nbsp;<strong>184 rigs are running in the Williston Basin across MT, ND, and SD, increasing by eight rigs from last week.</strong></em></p><p>Not all rigs counted in our census are drilling for the Bakken, but it's close. The NDIC estimates 95% or more of activity in this region targets the Bakken and Three Forks formations.</p><blockquote><p>Note: The NDIC reports 190 rigs are active in North Dakota. That is 6 more than Baker Hughes reports in the Bakken area. The difference is likely accounted for in the number of rigs actually working, rigs that might not be serviced by Baker Hughes AND rigs in areas outside of the Bakken fairway. On any given week, a certain number of rigs are in route to the next well location or idle waiting to drill the next well. The NDIC notes that around 14 rigs are in the process of moving in and rigging up.</p></blockquote><h2>Bakken Oil &amp; Gas Rigs</h2><p>The number of oil rigs increased by 7 rigs running over the past week. WTI oil prices decreased to ~$99/bbl by the end of the week. Williston Basin Sweet crude traded at $84.94/bbl on March 13th. The WTI-Brent opened up some from last week, settling at ~$10 by the end of the week.</p><p>The natural gas rig count in the region held flat at zero. Natural gas futures (Henry Hub) were trading around ~$4.40/mmbtu by the end of the week. A little more than 10% of the production stream from the Bakken and Three Forks is attributable to natural gas and roughly half of that is NGLs.</p><blockquote><p>McKenzie County continues to lead development with 62 rigs running. Dunn, Mountrail, and Williams counties are the only other counties with more than 25 rigs running each. View the full list below under the <em>Bakken Drilling by County</em>&nbsp;section.</p></blockquote><p>Activity is dominated by horizontal drilling:</p><ul><li>166 rigs are drilling horizontal wells</li><li>16 rigs are drilling directional wells</li><li>0 rigs are drilling vertical wells.</li></ul><p>Be sure to visit our <a href="http://jobs.bakkenshale.com/">Bakken Job Listings</a>&nbsp;to search openings and come back weekly for updates or sign up for alerts -&nbsp;Daily or Weekly Email Alerts</p><h2>Bakken Drilling by County</h2>

<div data-sqsp-text-block-content="">
  <h2>What is the Rig Count?</h2><p>The Bakken Shale Rig Count is an index of the total number of oil &amp; gas drilling rigs running across Montana and North Dakota. The rigs referred to in this article are for ALL drilling reported by Baker Hughes and not solely wells targeting the Bakken formation. All land rigs and onshore rig data shown here are based upon industry estimates provided by the Baker Hughes Rig Count and/or Smith Service Co's (Schlumberger) Smith Rig Count.</p>
