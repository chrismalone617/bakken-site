  <p>The <a href="/drilling/">Bakken-Three Forks rig count</a> decreased this week to <strong>70</strong> rigs running across our coverage area.</p><p>In recent Bakken news, Montana’s &nbsp;Board of Oil and Gas Conservation is considering a proposal that will require buffers zones around homes to protect them from oil and gas drilling.</p><p>Read more: <a href="/bsp-news/news/montana-considers-drilling-buffer?rq=Montana%20Considers%20Drilling%20Buffer">Montana Considers Drilling Buffer</a></p><p><em>The U.S. rig count increased slightly, ending with </em><strong><em>863</em></strong><em> rigs running by midday Friday. &nbsp;A total of 217 rigs were targeting natural gas (two less than the previous week) and 645 were targeting oil in the U.S. (five more than the previous week). The remainder were drilling service wells (e.g. disposal wells, injection wells, etc.)</em><strong>70 </strong>are in ND alone.</p><h2><strong>Bakken Oil &amp; Gas Rigs</strong></h2><p>The Bakken oil rigs decreased to 69 rigs this week with Bloomberg reporting WTI oil prices trading at $52.74/bbl on Friday afternoon, a $3.98 decrease from the previous week. WTI-Brent decreased $3.12 landing to $58.73. The Bakken has zero natural active gas rig in the area this week with futures increasing to $2.77/mmbtu by midday.</p><p>McKenzie County continues to lead development and is running 29 running this week, while Williams dropped to 15 and Mountrail increased 12. View the full list below under the <em>Bakken Drilling by County</em> section.</p><p>Activity continues to be dominated by horizontal drilling:</p><ul><li>69 rigs are drilling horizontal wells</li><li>0 rigs are drilling directional wells</li><li>1 rigs are drilling vertical wells</li></ul><p><a href="http://jobs.bakkenshale.com/">Be sure to visit our Bakken Job Listings to search openings and come back weekly for updates or sign up for alerts - Daily or Weekly Email Alerts</a></p><h2><strong>Bakken Drilling by County</strong></h2>
