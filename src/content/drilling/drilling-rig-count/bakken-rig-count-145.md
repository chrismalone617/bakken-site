<div data-sqsp-text-block-content="">
  <p>The <a href="/drilling/">Bakken-Three Forks rig count</a> decreased by six t0 145 rigs running across our coverage area by the end of last week.</p><p>In recent Bakken news, companies finally caught up with plummeting oil prices and began laying off workers this month. Since November, one company after another announced massive reductions in their 2015 budgets as they have scrambled to cope with the dramatic 50% drop in crude prices since the summer.</p><p><a href="/bsp-news/news/energy-giants-announce-layoffs?rq=Energy%20Giants%20Announce%20Layoffs">Read more: Energy Giants Announce Layoffs</a></p><p><em>The U.S. rig count fell by 90 to </em><strong><em>1543</em></strong><em> rigs running by the end of last week. A total of 316 rigs were targeting natural gas (up 6 from the previous week) and 1,317 were targeting oil in the U.S. (ten less than the previous week). The remainder were drilling service wells (e.g. disposal wells, injection wells, etc.).</em><strong>145 </strong>rigs are running in the Williston Basin across MT, ND, and SD. <strong>138 </strong>are in ND alone.</p><h2><strong>Bakken Oil &amp; Gas Rigs</strong></h2><p>The number of oil rigs decreased by six to 145 rigs running by the end of the week . WTI oil prices increased by $3.10 from the previous week, trading at $48.24/bbl on Friday afternoon. The WTI-Brent also saw an increase of close to $5 by week’s end, landing at $52.00. Natural gas rigs stayed flat with futures (Henry Hub) trading at $2.69/mmbtu by the end of last week.</p><p>McKenzie County continues to lead development with 52 rigs running. Mountrail, Williams and Dunn counties are the only other counties with more than 2o rigs running each. View the full list below under the <em>Bakken Drilling by County</em> section.</p><p>Activity is dominated by horizontal drilling:</p><ul><li>139 rigs are drilling horizontal wells</li><li>4 rigs are drilling directional wells</li><li>2 rigs are drilling vertical wells</li></ul><h2><strong>Bakken Oil &amp; Gas News</strong></h2><ul><li><strong><a href="/bsp-news/news/keystone-hits-snag?rq=Keystone%20Bill%20Hits%20Another%20Snag">Keystone Bill Hits Another Snag</a></strong></li></ul><ul><li><a href="/bsp-news/news/supply-chain-initiative-bakken-industries?rq=Supply%20Chain%20Initiative%20Launched%20for%20Bakken"><strong>Supply Chain Initiative Launched for Bakken</strong></a></li></ul><ul><li><a href="/bsp-news/news/halcon-resources-reduces-2015-budget"><strong>Halcón Resources Reduces 2015 Budget</strong></a></li></ul><p><a href="http://jobs.bakkenshale.com/">Be sure to visit our Bakken Job Listings to search openings and come back weekly for updates or sign up for alerts - Daily or Weekly Email Alerts</a></p><h2><strong>Bakken Drilling by County</strong></h2>

The rigs referred to in this article are for ALL drilling reported by Baker Hughes and not solely wells targeting the Bakken formation.
