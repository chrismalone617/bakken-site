<div data-sqsp-text-block-content="">
  <p>The <a href="http://bakkenshale.com/drilling/">Bakken-Three Forks rig count</a> stayed flat this week with <strong>62</strong> rigs running across our coverage area by end of the week on Friday.</p><p>In recent Bakken news, the EIA is reporting that oil production data for the Bakken region shows a year-over-year decline for the first time in more than a decade.</p><p>Read more:<a href="http://bakkenshale.com/bsp-news/news/bakken-production-down-4-6-over-2014?rq=Bakken%20Production%20Down%204.6%25%20Over%202014">Bakken Production Down 4.6% Over 2014</a></p><p><em>A total of <strong>757</strong> oil and gas rigs were running across the United States this week. <strong>193</strong> were targeting natural gas (the same as the previous week) and <strong>564</strong> were targeting oil in the U.S. (10 less than the previous week). The remainder were drilling service wells (e.g. disposal wells, injection wells, etc.)</em><strong><em>63</em></strong>are in ND alone.</p><h2><strong>Bakken Oil &amp; Gas Rigs</strong></h2><p>The Bakken oil rigs were at 62 this week with Bloomberg reporting WTI oil prices trading at $42.11 on Friday afternoon. WTI-Brent increased $.76landing at $45.22. The Bakken has zero natural active gas rig in the area this week with futures increasing slightly to $2.17/mmbtu by midday.</p><p>McKenzie County continues to lead development with 26 rigs running this week, far outpacing other counties. View the full list below under the <em>Bakken Drilling by County</em> section.</p><p>Activity continues to be dominated by horizontal drilling:</p><ul><li>58 rigs are drilling horizontal wells</li><li>3 rigs are drilling directional wells</li><li>1 rigs are drilling vertical wells</li></ul><p><a href="http://jobs.bakkenshale.com/">Be sure to visit our Bakken Job Listings to search openings and come back weekly for updates or sign up for alerts - Daily or Weekly Email Alerts</a></p><h2><strong>Bakken Drilling by County</strong></h2>
