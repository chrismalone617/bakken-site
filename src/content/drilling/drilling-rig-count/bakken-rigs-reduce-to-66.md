<div data-sqsp-text-block-content="">
  <p>The <a href="/drilling/">Bakken-Three Forks rig count</a> dropped to <strong>66</strong>&nbsp;rigs running across our coverage area by end of the week on Friday.</p><p>In recent Bakken news, production in the Bakken Shale play decreased over the summer according to the Energy Information Administration’ s latest Drilling Productivity Report.</p><p>Read more:<a href="/bsp-news/news/eia-bakken-production-declines?rq=EIA%3A%20Bakken%20Production%20Declines">EIA: Bakken Production Declines</a></p><p><em>The U.S. rig count fell by four this week, ending with </em><em>838</em><em>&nbsp;rigs running by midday Friday. &nbsp;A total of 194 rigs were targeting natural gas (down four from previous week) and 640 were targeting oil in the U.S. (one less than the previous week). The remainder were drilling service wells (e.g. disposal wells, injection wells, etc.)</em><strong>70 </strong>are in ND alone.</p><h2><strong>Bakken Oil &amp; Gas Rigs</strong></h2><p>The Bakken oil rigs were at 66 this week with Bloomberg reporting WTI oil prices trading at $45.7/bbl on Friday afternoon.&nbsp;WTI-Brent decreased $.06 landing to $48.60. The Bakken has zero natural active gas rig in the area this week with futures increasing slightly to $2.56/mmbtu by midday.</p><p>McKenzie County continues to lead development at 25 rigs this week, far outpacing other counties. View the full list below under the <em>Bakken Drilling by County</em> section.</p><p>Activity continues to be dominated by horizontal drilling:</p><ul><li>62 rigs are drilling horizontal wells</li><li>1 rigs are drilling directional wells</li><li>3 rigs are drilling vertical wells</li></ul><p><a href="http://jobs.bakkenshale.com/">Be sure to visit our Bakken Job Listings to search openings and come back weekly for updates or sign up for alerts - Daily or Weekly Email Alerts</a></p><h2><strong>Bakken Drilling by County</strong></h2>
