<div data-sqsp-text-block-content="">
  <p>The <a href="http://bakkenshale.com/drilling/">Bakken-Three Forks rig count</a> decreased by five t0 <strong>121</strong> rigs running across our coverage area by the end of last week.</p><p>In recent Bakken news, Kinder Morgan announced last week that it finalized the acquisition of Hiland Partners, a midstream firm founded by Harold Hamm, CEO of Continental Resources. The deal, reported at $3 billion, includes assuming almost $1 billion in debt.</p><p>Read more: <a href="http://bakkenshale.com/bsp-news/news/kinder-morgan-acquires-hiland?rq=Kinder%20Morgan%20Acquires%20Hiland">Kinder Morgan Acquires Hiland</a></p><p><em>The U.S. rig count fell another 48 to 1310 rigs running by the end of last week. A total of 289 rigs were targeting natural gas (down 11 from the previous week) and 1019 were targeting oil in the U.S. (37 less than the previous week). The remainder were drilling service wells (e.g. disposal wells, injection wells, etc.)</em><strong>123 </strong>rigs are running in the Williston Basin across MT, ND, and SD. <strong>119 </strong>are in ND alone.</p><h2><strong>Bakken Oil &amp; Gas Rigs</strong></h2><p>The number of oil rigs decreased by five t0 <strong>121</strong> rigs running by the end of the week . WTI oil prices decreased by $2.14 from the previous week, trading at $50.50/bbl on Friday afternoon. The WTI-Brent saw an increase decrease of $3.34 by week’s end, landing at $60.39. Natural gas rigs remained flat with futures trading at $2.94/mmbtu by the end of last week.</p><p>McKenzie County continues to lead development with 45 rigs running. Williams (26), Mountrail (21) and Dunn (20) counties are the only other counties with more than 2o rigs running each. View the full list below under the <em>Bakken Drilling by County</em> section.</p><p>Activity is dominated by horizontal drilling:</p><ul><li>119 rigs are drilling horizontal wells</li><li>1 rigs are drilling directional wells</li><li>1 rigs are drilling vertical wells</li></ul><p><a href="http://jobs.bakkenshale.com/">Be sure to visit our Bakken Job Listings to search openings and come back weekly for updates or sign up for alerts - Daily or Weekly Email Alerts</a></p><h2><strong>Bakken Drilling by County</strong></h2>
