  <p>The <a href="http://bakkenshale.com/drilling/">Bakken-Three Forks rig count</a> increased this week to <strong>76</strong> rigs running across our coverage area.</p><p>In recent Bakken news, Montana’s &nbsp;Board of Oil and Gas Conservation is considering a proposal that will require buffers zones around homes to protect them from oil and gas drilling.</p><p>Read more:<a href="http://bakkenshale.com/bsp-news/news/public-concern-over-dakota-access-pipeline?rq=Dakota%20Access%20Pipeline%20Concerns">Dakota Access Pipeline Concerns</a></p><p><em>The U.S. rig count increased slightly, ending with </em><strong><em>862</em></strong><em> rigs running by midday Thursday. &nbsp;A total of 219 rigs were targeting natural gas (nine less than the previous week) and 640 were targeting oil in the U.S. (12 more than the previous week). The remainder were drilling service wells (e.g. disposal wells, injection wells, etc.)</em><strong>76 </strong>are in ND alone.</p><h2><strong>Bakken Oil &amp; Gas Rigs</strong></h2><p>The Bakken oil rigs increased to 75 rigs this week with Bloomberg reporting WTI oil prices trading at $56.72/bbl on Friday morning, a $2.91 decrease from the previous week. WTI-Brent decreased $1.41 landing to $61.85. The Bakken has zero natural active gas rig in the area this week with futures dropping to $2.71/mmbtu.</p><p>McKenzie County continues to lead development and is running 3o running this week, while Williams increased to 18 and Mountrail stayed flat at 11. View the full list below under the <em>Bakken Drilling by County</em> section.</p><p>Activity continues to be dominated by horizontal drilling:</p><ul><li>73 rigs are drilling horizontal wells</li><li>2 rigs are drilling directional wells</li><li>1 rigs are drilling vertical wells</li></ul><p><a href="http://jobs.bakkenshale.com/">Be sure to visit our Bakken Job Listings to search openings and come back weekly for updates or sign up for alerts - Daily or Weekly Email Alerts</a></p><h2><strong>Bakken Drilling by County</strong></h2>
