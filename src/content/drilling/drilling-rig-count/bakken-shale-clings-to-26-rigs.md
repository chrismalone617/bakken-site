<div data-sqsp-text-block-content="">
  <p>The <a href="/drilling/">Bakken-Three Forks rig count</a>&nbsp;remained flat this week with <strong>26</strong>&nbsp;rigs running across our coverage area by midday Friday.</p><p><em>A total of <strong>419 </strong>oil and gas rigs were running across the United States this week, down 12 from last week. <strong>87 </strong>were targeting natural gas (one less than the previous week) and <strong>332</strong></em><em>&nbsp;were targeting oil in the U.S. (11 less than the previous week). The remainder were drilling service wells (e.g. disposal wells, injection wells, etc.)</em><strong>26 </strong>of the rigs active in the U.S. were running in North Dakota.</p><h2><strong>Bakken Oil &amp; Gas Rigs</strong></h2><p>The Bakken oil rigs hels steady at 26 this week with Bloomberg reporting WTI oil prices climbing to $45.92 on Friday afternoon. WTI-Brent also increased this week to $47.37.&nbsp;&nbsp;The Bakken has zero natural active gas rig in the area this week with futures increasing &nbsp;to $2.18/mmbtu by midday.</p><p>McKenzie County continues to lead development with 13 rigs running this week, far outpacing other counties. View the full list below under the <em>Bakken Drilling by County</em> section.</p><p>Activity continues to be dominated by horizontal drilling:</p><ul><li>26 rigs are drilling horizontal wells</li><li>0 rigs are drilling directional wells</li><li>0 rigs are drilling vertical wells</li></ul><p><a href="http://jobs.bakkenshale.com/">Be sure to visit our Bakken Job Listings to search openings and come back weekly for updates or sign up for alerts - Daily or Weekly Email Alerts</a></p><h2><strong>Bakken Drilling by County</strong></h2>
