  <p>The <a href="/drilling/">Bakken-Three Forks rig count</a> declined by one to end the week at <strong>75</strong> rigs running across our coverage area.</p><p>In recent Bakken news, North Dakota’s oil trigger was expected to take place June 1st, but rising crude prices mean it won’t be implemented and producers will miss out on an estimated $480 million over the next six months.</p><p>Read more: <a href="/bsp-news/news/rising-crude-derails-bakken-tax-trigger">Rising Crude Prices Derails Bakken Tax Trigger</a></p><p><em>The U.S. rig count fell by seven, ending with </em><strong><em>868</em></strong><em> rigs running by midday Friday. &nbsp;A total of 222 rigs were targeting natural gas (down three from the previous week) and 642 were targeting oil in the U.S. (four less than the previous week). The remainder were drilling service wells (e.g. disposal wells, injection wells, etc.)</em><strong>76 </strong>rigs are running in the Williston Basin across MT, ND, and SD. <strong>76 </strong>are in ND alone.</p><h2><strong>Bakken Oil &amp; Gas Rigs</strong></h2><p>The Bakken oil rigs stayed at 75 rigs this week with Bloomberg reporting WTI oil prices trading at $59.13/bbl on Friday afternoon, a $1.08 decrease from the previous week. WTI-Brent decreased $2.08 landing at $63.31. The Bakken has zero natural active gas rig in the area this week with futures trading dipping to $2.59/mmbtu by midday.</p><p>McKenzie County continues to lead development but fell to 3o running rigs this week. Williams (18) and Mountrail (13) counties are the next closest producers. View the full list below under the <em>Bakken Drilling by County</em> section.</p><p>Activity continues to be dominated by horizontal drilling:</p><ul><li>74 rigs are drilling horizontal wells</li><li>0 rigs are drilling directional wells</li><li>1 rigs are drilling vertical wells</li></ul><p><a href="http://jobs.bakkenshale.com/">Be sure to visit our Bakken Job Listings to search openings and come back weekly for updates or sign up for alerts - Daily or Weekly Email Alerts</a></p><h2><strong>Bakken Drilling by County</strong></h2>
