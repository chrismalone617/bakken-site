  <p>The <a href="http://bakkenshale.com/drilling/">Bakken-Three Forks rig count</a>&nbsp;fell this week with <strong>59</strong>&nbsp;rigs running across our coverage area by end of the week on Friday.</p><p>In recent Bakken news, it's been one year since OPEC annoucned it would not decrease production and while the year has been brutal, shale is hardly dead.</p><p>Read more:&nbsp;<a href="http://bakkenshale.com/bsp-news/news/opecs-plan-to-kill-shale-one-year-later">OPEC’s Plan to Kill Shale: One Year Later</a></p><p><em>A total of <strong>737</strong>&nbsp;oil and gas rigs were running across the United States this week.<strong>192</strong>&nbsp;were targeting natural gas (three more than the previous week) and <strong>545</strong>&nbsp;were targeting oil in the U.S. (10 less than the previous week). The remainder were drilling service wells (e.g. disposal wells, injection wells, etc.)</em><strong><em>59</em></strong>are in ND alone.</p><h2><strong>Bakken Oil &amp; Gas Rigs</strong></h2><p>The Bakken oil rigs were at 59 this week with Bloomberg reporting WTI oil prices trading at $39.97 on Friday afternoon. WTI-Brent decreased $1.86landing at $43.00 The Bakken has zero natural active gas rig in the area this week with futures decreasing to $2.19/mmbtu by midday.</p><p>McKenzie County continues to lead development with 28 rigs running this week, far outpacing other counties. View the full list below under the <em>Bakken Drilling by County</em> section.</p><p>Activity continues to be dominated by horizontal drilling:</p><ul><li>57 rigs are drilling horizontal wells</li><li>1 rigs are drilling directional wells</li><li>1 rigs are drilling vertical wells</li></ul><p><a href="http://jobs.bakkenshale.com/">Be sure to visit our Bakken Job Listings to search openings and come back weekly for updates or sign up for alerts - Daily or Weekly Email Alerts</a></p><h2><strong>Bakken Drilling by County</strong></h2>
