<div data-sqsp-text-block-content="">
  <p>The <a href="http://bakkenshale.com/drilling/">Bakken-Three Forks rig count</a> remained flat this week with <strong>75</strong> rigs running across our coverage area.</p><p>In recent Bakken news, North Dakota experienced its wettest May on record and all the rain is causing trouble for some oil and gas producers. Flood waters threaten operators near the confluence of the Missouri and Yellowstone rivers.</p><p>Read more: <a href="http://bakkenshale.com/bsp-news/news/floods-threaten-bakken-producers?rq=Floods%20Threaten%20Bakken%20Producers">Floods Threaten Bakken Producers</a></p><p><em>The U.S. rig count fell by nine, ending with </em><strong><em>859</em></strong><em> rigs running by midday Friday. &nbsp;A total of 221 rigs were targeting natural gas (down one from the previous week) and 635 were targeting oil in the U.S. (seven less than the previous week). The remainder were drilling service wells (e.g. disposal wells, injection wells, etc.)</em><strong>76 </strong>rigs are running in the Williston Basin across MT, ND, and SD. <strong>76 </strong>are in ND alone.</p><h2><strong>Bakken Oil &amp; Gas Rigs</strong></h2><p>The Bakken oil rigs stayed at 75 rigs this week with Bloomberg reporting WTI oil prices trading at $59.98/bbl on Friday afternoon, an $.85 increase from the previous week. WTI-Brent increased $.38 landing at $63.691. The Bakken has zero natural active gas rig in the area this week with futures trading dipping to $2.76/mmbtu by midday.</p><p>McKenzie County continues to lead development and is running 3o running this week. Williams (18) and Mountrail (13) counties are the next closest producers. View the full list below under the <em>Bakken Drilling by County</em> section.</p><p>Activity continues to be dominated by horizontal drilling:</p><ul><li>73 rigs are drilling horizontal wells</li><li>1 rigs are drilling directional wells</li><li>1 rigs are drilling vertical wells</li></ul><p><a href="http://jobs.bakkenshale.com/">Be sure to visit our Bakken Job Listings to search openings and come back weekly for updates or sign up for alerts - Daily or Weekly Email Alerts</a></p><h2><strong>Bakken Drilling by County</strong></h2>
