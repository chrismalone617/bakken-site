  <p>The <a href="/drilling/">Bakken-Three Forks rig count</a> increased to <strong>71</strong> rigs running across our coverage area by end of the week on Friday. In recent Bakken news, crude oil has fallen to a six year low in recent weeks, but even at the current price of around $43, analysts speculate the Bakken will remain strong. &nbsp;</p><p>Read more: <a href="/bsp-news/news/can-bakken-break-even-at-30?rq=Can%20Bakken%20Break%20Even%20at%20%2430%3F">Can Bakken Break Even at $30?</a></p><p><em>The U.S. rig count increased by one, ending with </em><strong><em>885</em></strong><em> rigs running by midday Friday. &nbsp;A total of 211 rigs were targeting natural gas (the same as the previous week) and 674 were targeting oil in the U.S. (two more than the previous week). The remainder were drilling service wells (e.g. disposal wells, injection wells, etc.)</em> &nbsp;<strong>72 </strong>are in ND alone.</p><h2><strong>Bakken Oil &amp; Gas Rigs</strong></h2><p>The Bakken oil rigs were at 71 rigs this week with Bloomberg reporting WTI oil prices trading at $40.45/bbl on Friday afternoon, a $1.68 decrease from the previous week. WTI-Brent decreased $3.57 landing to $45.46. The Bakken has zero natural active gas rig in the area this week with futures decreasing to $2.68/mmbtu by midday.</p><p>McKenzie County continues to lead development and is running 26 running this week, while Williams (15) and Mountrail (12) followed behind. View the full list below under the <em>Bakken Drilling by County</em> section.</p><p>Activity continues to be dominated by horizontal drilling:</p><ul><li>61 rigs are drilling horizontal wells</li><li>1 rigs are drilling directional wells</li><li>2 rigs are drilling vertical wells</li></ul><p><a href="http://jobs.bakkenshale.com/">Be sure to visit our Bakken Job Listings to search openings and come back weekly for updates or sign up for alerts - Daily or Weekly Email Alerts</a></p><h2><strong>Bakken Drilling by County</strong></h2>
