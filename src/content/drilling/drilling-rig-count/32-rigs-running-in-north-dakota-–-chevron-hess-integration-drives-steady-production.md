The Bakken [rig count](https://ycharts.com/indicators/bakken_region_active_rigs) currently sits at approximately 28 to 32 active rigs, holding relatively steady over the past month. While this is significantly lower than the decade-highs of 2012, modern efficiency means these rigs are far more productive; operators now maintain stable production levels with a fraction of the hardware once required.

Following [Chevron’s $53 billion acquisition of Hess](https://eastdaley.com/daley-note/bakken-on-the-block-close-of-hess-sale-could-put-assets-in-play), the Bakken remains a "flywheel" for the portfolio. Hess Midstream recently projected flat to slightly growing throughput for 2026, with plans to operate a consistent [three-rig program](https://hessmidstream.gcs-web.com/news-releases/news-release-details/hess-midstream-lp-announces-2026-guidance-extends-return-capital/) in the region. Production from these assets is expected to average roughly 200,000 barrels of oil equivalent per day (boe/d).

*The total U.S. rig count stands at 550, with 407 rigs targeting oil and 134 targeting natural gas as of the latest Baker Hughes report.*

## Bakken Oil & Gas Market

* **WTI Oil Price:** West Texas Intermediate (WTI) surged today, trading near $73.14/bbl amid [escalating Middle East tensions.](https://www.livemint.com/market/commodities/crude-oil-prices-live-us-israel-iran-war-brent-oil-price-wti-crude-oil-price-strait-of-hormuz-ali-khamenei-dubai-opec-11772416774770.html)
* **Williston Basin Sweet**: Local crude prices are seeing a similar lift, with recent [North Dakota first purchase prices averaging](https://www.eia.gov/dnav/pet/hist/LeafHandler.ashx?n=PET&s=F002038__3&f=M) in the $61–$63 range prior to today's spike.
* **Natural Gas:** Henry Hub futures are trading near $3.00/mmbtu. In the Bakken, gas capture remains a primary focus, with gas gathering volumes expected to average 450–460 million cubic feet per day this year.

## Regional Drilling Breakdown

Development continues to be concentrated in the "Big Four" counties:

* **McKenzie County** remains the heart of activity, recently seeing a [Supreme Court ruling](https://www.newsfromthestates.com/article/hell-lot-royalties-supreme-court-ruling-likely-trigger-litigation-oil-companies) regarding section line royalty allocations that could impact future lease litigation.
* **Williams, Dunn, and Mountrail** counties follow as the primary hubs for [ongoing horizontal drilling](https://northdakotarigsmap.com/current-rigs/).

## Key Bakken News

* **Chevron-Hess Outlook:** Chevron is evaluating its long-term Bakken strategy, with analysts speculating on [potential asset divestments ](https://eastdaley.com/daley-note/bakken-on-the-block-close-of-hess-sale-could-put-assets-in-play)as the major focuses on Guyana and the Gulf of Mexico.
* **Efficiency Gains:** Capital expenditures for Bakken midstream infrastructure are [projected to fall](https://hessmidstream.gcs-web.com/news-releases/news-release-details/hess-midstream-lp-announces-2026-guidance-extends-return-capital/) to approximately $150 million in 2026 as the region shifts from build-out to maintenance and optimization.
