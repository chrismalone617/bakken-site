<div data-sqsp-text-block-content="">
  <p>The <a href="http://bakkenshale.com/drilling/">Bakken-Three Forks rig count</a> decreased this week to <strong>73</strong> rigs running across our coverage area.</p><p>In recent Bakken news, Energy Transfer Pipeline is moving forward with plans to build a massive 1.134 mile pipeline that will transport crude from the Bakken through South Dakota and into Patoka, Illinois amid public concerns.</p><p>Read more:<a href="http://bakkenshale.com/bsp-news/news/public-concern-over-dakota-access-pipeline?rq=Dakota%20Access%20Pipeline%20Concerns">Dakota Access Pipeline Concerns</a></p><p><em>The U.S. rig count increased slightly, ending with </em><strong><em>859</em></strong><em> rigs running by midday Friday. &nbsp;A total of 228 rigs were targeting natural gas (five more the previous week) and 628 were targeting oil in the U.S. (three less than the previous week). The remainder were drilling service wells (e.g. disposal wells, injection wells, etc.)</em><strong>74 </strong>are in ND alone.</p><h2><strong>Bakken Oil &amp; Gas Rigs</strong></h2><p>The Bakken oil rigs fell to 73 rigs this week with Bloomberg reporting WTI oil prices trading at $59.63/bbl on Friday afternoon, a $.02 decrease from the previous week. WTI-Brent increased $.24 landing to $63.26. The Bakken has zero natural active gas rig in the area this week with futures dropping to $2.77/mmbtu by midday.</p><p>McKenzie County continues to lead development and is running 3o running this week. Williams stays at 18, but Mountrail loses two rigs to 11. View the full list below under the <em>Bakken Drilling by County</em> section.</p><p>Activity continues to be dominated by horizontal drilling:</p><ul><li>72 rigs are drilling horizontal wells</li><li>1 rigs are drilling directional wells</li><li>0 rigs are drilling vertical wells</li></ul><p><a href="http://jobs.bakkenshale.com/">Be sure to visit our Bakken Job Listings to search openings and come back weekly for updates or sign up for alerts - Daily or Weekly Email Alerts</a></p><h2><strong>Bakken Oil &amp; Gas News</strong></h2><p><a href="http://bakkenshale.com/bsp-news/news/two-saltwater-leaks-in-north-dakota?rq=Two%20Saltwater%20Leaks%20in%20North%20Dakota">Two Saltwater Leaks in North Dakota</a></p><p><a href="http://bakkenshale.com/bsp-news/news/turning-drilling-waste-into-roads?rq=Turning%20Drilling%20Waste%20into%20Roads">Turning Drilling Waste into Roads</a></p><h2><strong>What is the Rig Count?</strong></h2><p>The Bakken Shale Rig Count is an index of the total number of oil &amp; gas drilling rigs running across Montana and North Dakota. The rigs referred to in this article are for ALL drilling reported by Baker Hughes and not solely wells targeting the Bakken formation. All land rigs and onshore rig data shown here are based upon industry estimates provided by the Baker Hughes Rig Count.</p><p>Read more at <a target="_blank" href="http://phx.corporate-ir.net/phoenix.zhtml?c=79687&amp;p=irol-reportsother%20">bakerhughes.com</a></p>
