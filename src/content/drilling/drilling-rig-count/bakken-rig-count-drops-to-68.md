  <p>The <a href="/drilling/">Bakken-Three Forks rig count</a> decreased this week to <strong>68</strong> rigs running across our coverage area.</p><p>In recent Bakken news, U.S. District Court Judge Scott W. Skavdahl delayed the new federal fracking rule, which was to go into effect last week. The permitting of oil and gas wells on federal land will proceed under current regulations until July 22nd.</p><p>Read more: <a href="/bsp-news/news/north-dakota-fights-fracking-rule-2?rq=North%20Dakota%20Fights%20Fracking%20Rule">North Dakota Fights Fracking Rule</a></p><p><em>The U.S. rig count decreased by six, ending with </em><strong><em>857</em></strong><em> rigs running by midday Friday. &nbsp;A total of 218 rigs were targeting natural gas (one more than the previous week) and 638 were targeting oil in the U.S. (seven less than the previous week). The remainder were drilling service wells (e.g. disposal wells, injection wells, etc.)</em><strong>68 </strong>are in ND alone.</p><h2><strong>Bakken Oil &amp; Gas Rigs</strong></h2><p>The Bakken oil rigs decreased to 68 rigs this week with Bloomberg reporting WTI oil prices trading at $50.85/bbl on Friday afternoon, a $1.89 decrease from the previous week. WTI-Brent decreased $1.62 landing to $57.11. The Bakken has zero natural active gas rig in the area this week with futures increasing to $2.88/mmbtu by midday.</p><p>McKenzie County continues to lead development and is running 27 running this week, while Williams dropped to 14 and Mountrail stayed flat at 12. View the full list below under the <em>Bakken Drilling by County</em> section.</p><p>Activity continues to be dominated by horizontal drilling:</p><ul><li>68 rigs are drilling horizontal wells</li><li>0 rigs are drilling directional wells</li><li>0 rigs are drilling vertical wells</li></ul><p><a href="http://jobs.bakkenshale.com/">Be sure to visit our Bakken Job Listings to search openings and come back weekly for updates or sign up for alerts - Daily or Weekly Email Alerts</a></p><h2><strong>Bakken Drilling by County</strong></h2>
