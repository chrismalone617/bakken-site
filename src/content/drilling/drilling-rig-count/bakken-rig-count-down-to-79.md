<div data-sqsp-text-block-content="">
  <p>The <a href="http://bakkenshale.com/drilling/">Bakken-Three Forks rig count</a> fell this week to <strong>79</strong> rigs running across our coverage area by midday Friday.</p><p>In recent Bakken news, the SEC froze the assets of North Dakota Developments, LLC (“NDD”) and its principles, claiming they defrauded over 980 investors from 66 different countries of $62 million dollars intended to go towards Bakken man camps.</p><p>Read more: <a href="http://bakkenshale.com/bsp-news/news/bakken-man-camps-center-of-ponzi-type-scheme?rq=Bakken%20Man%20Camps%20Center%20of%20Ponzi-Type%20Scheme">Bakken Man Camps Center of Ponzi-Type Scheme</a></p><p><em>The U.S. rig count fell by six, ending with </em><strong><em>888</em></strong><em> rigs running by midday Friday. &nbsp;A total of 223 rigs were targeting natural gas (an increase of two from the previous week) and 660 were targeting oil in the U.S. (eight less than the previous week). The remainder were drilling service wells (e.g. disposal wells, injection wells, etc.)</em><strong>79 </strong>rigs are running in the Williston Basin across MT, ND, and SD. <strong>79 </strong>are in ND alone.</p><h2><strong>Bakken Oil &amp; Gas Rigs</strong></h2><p>The Bakken oil rigs stayed at 79 rigs this week and &nbsp;WTI oil prices continued to climb trading at $59.51/bbl on Friday afternoon, a $.12 increase from the previous week. The WTI-Brent saw an increase of $1.31, landing at $66.70. The Bakken has zero natural active gas rig in the area this week with futures trading increasing to $3.02/mmbtu by midday.</p><p>McKenzie County continues to lead development this week at 34 running rigs. Williams (19) and Mountrail (13) counties are the next closest producers. View the full list below under the <em>Bakken Drilling by County</em> section.</p><p>Activity continues to be dominated by horizontal drilling:</p><ul><li>79 rigs are drilling horizontal wells</li><li>0 rigs are drilling directional wells</li><li>0 rigs are drilling vertical wells</li></ul><p><a href="http://jobs.bakkenshale.com/">Be sure to visit our Bakken Job Listings to search openings and come back weekly for updates or sign up for alerts - Daily or Weekly Email Alerts</a></p><h2><strong>Bakken Drilling by County</strong></h2>
