<div data-sqsp-text-block-content="">
  <p>The <a href="/drilling/">Bakken-Three Forks rig count</a>&nbsp;fell by one this week with <strong>62</strong>&nbsp;rigs running across our coverage area by end of the week on Friday.</p><p>In recent Bakken news, a well owned by Oasis Petroleum blew last week leaking more than 67,000 gallons of oil and causing law enforcement to close several roads.</p><p>Read more:<a href="/bsp-news/news/7725?rq=North%20Dakota%20Well%20Under%20Control">North Dakota Well Under Control</a></p><p><em>A total of <strong>775</strong>&nbsp;oil and gas rigs were running across the United States this week. <strong>197</strong> were targeting natural gas (four more than the previous week) and <strong>578</strong>were targeting oil in the U.S. (16 less than the previous week). The remainder were drilling service wells (e.g. disposal wells, injection wells, etc.)</em><strong><em>62</em></strong>are in ND alone.</p><h2><strong>Bakken Oil &amp; Gas Rigs</strong></h2><p>The Bakken oil rigs were at 62 this week with Bloomberg reporting WTI oil prices trading at $46.35/bbl on Friday afternoon.&nbsp;WTI-Brent increased $1.41landing at $49.40. The Bakken has zero natural active gas rig in the area this week with futures increasing slightly to $2.33/mmbtu by midday.</p><p>McKenzie County continues to lead development with 26 rigs running this week, far outpacing other counties. View the full list below under the <em>Bakken Drilling by County</em> section.</p><p>Activity continues to be dominated by horizontal drilling:</p><ul><li>58 rigs are drilling horizontal wells</li><li>3 rigs are drilling directional wells</li><li>1 rigs are drilling vertical wells</li></ul><p><a href="http://jobs.bakkenshale.com/">Be sure to visit our Bakken Job Listings to search openings and come back weekly for updates or sign up for alerts - Daily or Weekly Email Alerts</a></p><h2><strong>Bakken Drilling by County</strong></h2>
