  <p>The <a href="http://bakkenshale.com/drilling/">Bakken-Three Forks rig count</a> decreased by four t0 <strong>102</strong> rigs running across our coverage area by the end of last week.</p><p>In recent Bakken news, two separate accidents across the Williston last week have added to the growing concern about Bakken oil safety and is fueling the intense debate about the way oil is produced, contained and transported in the United States.</p><p>Read more: <a href="http://bakkenshale.com/bsp-news/news/bakken-oil-safety?rq=Bakken%20Oil%20Safety">Bakken Oil Safety</a></p><p><em>The U.S. rig count fell another 67 to 1125 rigs running as of today. A total of 257 rigs were targeting natural gas (down 11 from the previous week) and 866 were targeting oil in the U.S. (56 less than the previous week). The remainder were drilling service wells (e.g. disposal wells, injection wells, etc.)</em><strong>101 </strong>rigs are running in the Williston Basin across MT, ND, and SD. <strong>104 </strong>are in ND alone.</p><h2><strong>Bakken Oil &amp; Gas Rigs</strong></h2><p>The number of oil rigs decreased by four t0 <strong>102</strong> rigs running by midday. WTI oil prices decreased by $4.37 from the previous week, trading at $44.81/bbl on Friday afternoon. The WTI-Brent saw a decrease of $4.75 by week’s end, landing at $54.67. Natural gas rigs decreased by four with futures trading at $2.73/mmbtu by midday.</p><p>McKenzie County continues to lead development and increased to 44 rigs running. Williams (19) and Mountrail (18) counties are the next closest producers. View the full list below under the <em>Bakken Drilling by County</em> section.</p><p>Activity continues to be dominated by horizontal drilling:</p><ul><li>102 rigs are drilling horizontal wells</li><li>0 rigs are drilling directional wells</li><li>0 rigs are drilling vertical wells</li></ul><p><a href="http://jobs.bakkenshale.com/">Be sure to visit our Bakken Job Listings to search openings and come back weekly for updates or sign up for alerts - Daily or Weekly Email Alerts</a></p><h2><strong>Bakken Drilling by County</strong></h2>
