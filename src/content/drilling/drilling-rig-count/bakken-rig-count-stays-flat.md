  <p>The <a href="/drilling/">Bakken-Three Forks rig count</a> stayed flat this week at <strong>80</strong> rigs running across our coverage area by midday Friday.</p><p>In recent Bakken news, the first U.S. refinery built in over 30 years began operations this week at the new Dakota Prairie refinery, located on 375 acres in Stark County, four miles west of Dickinson, N.D</p><p>Read more: <a href="/bsp-news/news/new-bakken-refinery-begins-operations?rq=New%20Bakken%20Refinery%20Begins%20Operations">New Bakken Refinery Begins Operations</a></p><p><em>The U.S. rig count fell again this week by 11 ending, ending with </em><strong><em>894</em></strong><em> rigs running by midday Friday. &nbsp;A total of </em><em>221 </em><em>rigs were targeting natural gas (and decrease of one from the previous week) and </em><em>668</em><em> were targeting oil in the U.S. (11 less than the previous week). The remainder were drilling service wells (e.g. disposal wells, injection wells, etc.)</em><strong>80 </strong>rigs are running in the Williston Basin across MT, ND, and SD. <strong>80 </strong>are in ND alone.</p><h2><strong>Bakken Oil &amp; Gas Rigs</strong></h2><p>The Bakken oil rigs stayed at 80 rigs this week and &nbsp;WTI oil prices continued to climb trading at $59.39/bbl on Friday afternoon, a $.63 increase from the previous week. The WTI-Brent saw an increase of $.74, landing at $66.02. The Bakken has zero natural active gas rig in the area this week with futures trading increasing to $2.88/mmbtu by midday.</p><p>McKenzie County continues to lead development this week by climbing two to 34 running rigs. Williams (19) and Mountrail (13) counties are the next closest producers. View the full list below under the <em>Bakken Drilling by County</em> section.</p><p>Activity continues to be dominated by horizontal drilling:</p><ul><li>80 rigs are drilling horizontal wells</li><li>0 rigs are drilling directional wells</li><li>0 rigs are drilling vertical wells</li></ul><p><a href="http://jobs.bakkenshale.com/">Be sure to visit our Bakken Job Listings to search openings and come back weekly for updates or sign up for alerts - Daily or Weekly Email Alerts</a></p><h2><strong>Bakken Drilling by County</strong></h2>
