<div data-sqsp-text-block-content="">
  <p>
The Williston Basin rig count settled at 185 rigs running to end April. That's according to Baker Hughes and includes rigs active in the Montana portion of the basin.</p><p>All but approximately five rigs in the basin are drilling in areas prospective for the Bakken and Three Forks formations.</p><p>The rig count in the area hit a peak of 224 in June of 2012 and has fallen almost 80% since. Much of the decline has been the result of the use of pad drilling where one rig can drill more wells per year.</p><p>Acreage across the region is largely held by production or will be by year-end 2013. That means operators can slow down and focus on efficiency of development.</p>
