  <p>Due to the holiday, we are releasing the weekly rig count post early.</p><p>The <a href="/drilling/">Bakken-Three Forks rig count</a>&nbsp;fell by three this week landing at <strong>54</strong>&nbsp;rigs running across our coverage area by midday Thursday.</p><p><em>A total of <strong>698</strong>&nbsp;oil and gas rigs were running across the United States this week. <strong>162</strong>&nbsp;were targeting natural gas (same as the previous week) and <strong>536</strong>&nbsp;were targeting oil in the U.S. (two less than the previous week). The remainder were drilling service wells (e.g. disposal wells, injection wells, etc.)</em><strong><em> 52</em></strong>are in ND alone.</p><h2><strong>Bakken Oil &amp; Gas Rigs</strong></h2><p>The Bakken oil rigs were at 52 this week with Bloomberg reporting WTI oil prices trading at $37.47 on Friday afternoon. WTI-Brent increased $.42landing at $37.73 The Bakken has zero natural active gas rig in the area this week with futures increasing to $2.33/mmbtu by midday.</p><p>McKenzie County continues to lead development with 23 rigs running this week, far outpacing other counties. View the full list below under the <em>Bakken Drilling by County</em> section.</p><p>Activity continues to be dominated by horizontal drilling:</p><ul><li>50 rigs are drilling horizontal wells</li><li>2 rigs are drilling directional wells</li><li>0 rigs are drilling vertical wells</li></ul><p><a href="http://jobs.bakkenshale.com/">Be sure to visit our Bakken Job Listings to search openings and come back weekly for updates or sign up for alerts - Daily or Weekly Email Alerts</a></p><h2><strong>Bakken Drilling by County</strong></h2>
