  <p>The <a href="/drilling/">Bakken-Three Forks rig count</a> dropped by five to <strong>79</strong> rigs running across our coverage area by midday Friday.</p><p>In recent Bakken news, the Department of Transportation &nbsp;issued an emergency order on Friday that establishes a maximum speed of 40 miles an hour for certain trains going through high threat urban areas.</p><p>Read more: <a href="/bsp-news/news/dot-tells-oil-tankers-to-slow-down?rq=DOT%20Tells%20Oil%20Tankers%20to%20Slow%20Down">DOT Tells Oil Tankers to Slow Down</a></p><p><em>The U.S. rig count fell again this week by another 22 ending at </em><em>932</em><em> rigs running by midday Friday. &nbsp;A total of </em><em>225 </em><em>rigs were targeting natural gas (and increase of eight &nbsp;from the previous week) and </em><em>703</em><em> were targeting oil in the U.S. (31 less than the previous week). The remainder were drilling service wells (e.g. disposal wells, injection wells, etc.)</em><strong>79 </strong>rigs are running in the Williston Basin across MT, ND, and SD. <strong>78 </strong>are in ND alone.</p><h2><strong>Bakken Oil &amp; Gas Rigs</strong></h2><p>By midday Friday, the Bakken oil rigs dropped to 78 rigs. &nbsp;WTI oil prices continued to increase this week and were up $1.60 from the previous week, trading at $57.34/bbl on Friday afternoon. The WTI-Brent saw an increase of $1.83 by week’s end, landing at $65.28. There was one natural active gas rig in the area and futures are trading at $2.25/mmbtu by midday.</p><p>McKenzie County continues to lead development this week but fell to 32 running rigs. Williams (17) and Mountrail (14) counties are the next closest producers. View the full list below under the <em>Bakken Drilling by County</em> section.</p><p>Activity continues to be dominated by horizontal drilling:</p><ul><li>79 rigs are drilling horizontal wells</li><li>0 rigs are drilling directional wells</li><li>0 rigs are drilling vertical wells</li></ul><p><a href="http://jobs.bakkenshale.com/">Be sure to visit our Bakken Job Listings to search openings and come back weekly for updates or sign up for alerts - Daily or Weekly Email Alerts</a></p><h2><strong>Bakken Drilling by County</strong></h2>
