  <p>The <a href="http://bakkenshale.com/drilling/">Bakken-Three Forks rig count</a> decreased by nine t0 <strong>126</strong> rigs running across our coverage area by the end of last week.</p><p>In recent Bakken news, Occidental announced it will scale back its 2015 capital spending by 33 percent to $5.8 billion from $8.7 billion spent in 2014. In addition to the cuts, the company will also be reducing its activity in the Bakken.</p><p>Read more: <a href="http://bakkenshale.com/bsp-news/news/occidental-reduces-2015-capex-33-percent?rq=Occidental%20Petroleum%20Reduced%202015%20Capex%20by%2033%25">Occidental Petroleum Reduced 2015 Capex by 33%</a></p><p><em>The U.S. rig count fell another 98 to 1358 rigs running by the end of last week. A total of 300 rigs were targeting natural gas (down 14 from the previous week) and 1056 were targeting oil in the U.S. (84 less than the previous week). The remainder were drilling service wells (e.g. disposal wells, injection wells, etc.).</em><strong>129 </strong>rigs are running in the Williston Basin across MT, ND, and SD. <strong>123 </strong>are in ND alone.</p><h2><strong>Bakken Oil &amp; Gas Rigs</strong></h2><p>The number of oil rigs decreased by nine t0 <strong>126</strong> rigs running by the end of the week . WTI oil prices increased by $.95 from the previous week, trading at $52.64/bbl on Friday afternoon.. The WTI-Brent saw a decrease of $.75 by week’s end, landing at $57.05. Natural gas rigs decreased by one with futures trading at $2.80/mmbtu by the end of last week.</p><p>McKenzie County continues to lead development with 47 rigs running. Williams (28), Mountrail (21) and Dunn (20) counties are the only other counties with more than 2o rigs running each. View the full list below under the <em>Bakken Drilling by County</em> section.</p><p>Activity is dominated by horizontal drilling:</p><ul><li>125 rigs are drilling horizontal wells</li><li>0 rigs are drilling directional wells</li><li>1 rigs are drilling vertical wells</li></ul><p><a href="http://jobs.bakkenshale.com/">Be sure to visit our Bakken Job Listings to search openings and come back weekly for updates or sign up for alerts - Daily or Weekly Email Alerts</a></p><h2><strong>Bakken Drilling by County</strong></h2>
