  <p>The <a href="http://bakkenshale.com/drilling/">Bakken-Three Forks rig count</a> dropped to <strong>69</strong>&nbsp;rigs running across our coverage area by end of the week on Friday.</p><p>In recent Bakken news, North Dakota senator, Heidi Heitkamp, pushes back against the latest federal mandate to reduce methane emissions.</p><p>Read more:<a href="http://bakkenshale.com/bsp-news/news/heitkamp-reacts-to-methane-ruling?rq=Heitkamp%20Reacts%20to%20Methane%20Ruling">Heitkamp Reacts to Methane Ruling</a></p><p><em>The U.S. rig count fell by 16 this week, ending with </em><strong><em>848</em></strong><em>&nbsp;rigs running by midday Friday. &nbsp;A total of 196 rigs were targeting natural gas (down six from previous week) and 652 were targeting oil in the U.S. (10 less than the previous week). The remainder were drilling service wells (e.g. disposal wells, injection wells, etc.)</em><strong>70 </strong>are in ND alone.</p><h2><strong>Bakken Oil &amp; Gas Rigs</strong></h2><p>The Bakken oil rigs were at 69 this week with Bloomberg reporting WTI oil prices trading at $45.19/bbl on Friday afternoon, a $1.59 decrease from the previous week. WTI-Brent decreased $.1.69 landing to $48.66. The Bakken has zero natural active gas rig in the area this week with futures increasing slightly to $2.70/mmbtu by midday.</p><p>McKenzie County continues to lead development at 22 rigs this week, while Williams (14), Dunn (13) and Mountrail (13) followed behind. View the full list below under the <em>Bakken Drilling by County</em> section.</p><p>Activity continues to be dominated by horizontal drilling:</p><ul><li>66 rigs are drilling horizontal wells</li><li>0 rigs are drilling directional wells</li><li>3 rigs are drilling vertical wells</li></ul><p><a href="http://jobs.bakkenshale.com/">Be sure to visit our Bakken Job Listings to search openings and come back weekly for updates or sign up for alerts - Daily or Weekly Email Alerts</a></p><h2><strong>Bakken Drilling by County</strong></h2>
