  <p>The Williston Basin rig count is up five rigs to 190 rigs running since the end of April.</p><p>Activity should be strong throughout the rest of the year.</p><p>News from this week was highlighted by our time at Bentek's annual Benposium Conference. We came away with several Bakken highlights.</p><p>One of those is that rail is likely here to stay. Oil will begin moving back onto pipelines, but it will be a dog fight and pipeline operators will have to offer competitive prices.</p><p>It was also noted that oil production has grown nearly twice as fast as many analyst predicted.</p><p>Natural gas prices were up a little to more than $4 again at $4.06/mmbtu on Friday afternoon. It has been a mild May, so having a $4 price and not a $2 price is a nice gift for natural gas producers.</p><p>WTI futures were almost unchanged from the past two weeks at $96/bbl on Friday afternoon. Williston Basin sweet crude traded at $83.69/bbl on May 17th.</p><p>Other Bakken related news from the past week includes:</p><ul dir="ltr"><li><a href="/bsp-news/news/continental-resources-ops-update">Continental Resources Bakken Production Surpasses 100,000 boe/d Gross</a></li><li><a href="/bsp-jobs/jobs-news/bakken-job-fair-planned-in-minot-on-june-26-2013">Bakken Job Fair Planned In Minot in Jun</a>e</li></ul>
