<div data-sqsp-text-block-content="">
  <p>The <a href="http://bakkenshale.com/drilling/">Bakken rig count</a> increased by one rig to 177 running during Thanksgiving week.</p><p>News was highlighted by an agreement between Enbridge Partners and Marathon Petroleum which will install Marathon as the anchor shipper and partner in Enbridge's upcoming Sandpiper Pipeline.</p><p>Read more in the article: <a href="http://bakkenshale.com/pmn/pipeline-midstream-news/enbridges-sandpiper-pipeline-gains-anchor-shipper-in-marathon-petroleum-open-season-starts">Enbridge`s Sandpiper Pipeline Gains Anchor Shipper in Marathon Petroleum</a></p><p><em>The U.S. rig count increased two rigs from 1,761 to 1,763 running over Thanksgiving week. A total of 367 rigs are targeting natural gas (2 less than last week) and 1,391 (4 more than last week) are targeting oil in the U.S. The remainder are drilling service wells (e.g. disposal wells, injection wells, etc.).</em><em><strong>180 (up 1 from last week) rigs are running in the Williston Basin across MT, ND, and SD.</strong></em></p><p>Not all rigs counted in our census are drilling for the Bakken, but it's close. The NDIC estimates 95% or more of activity in this region targets the Bakken and Three Forks formations.</p><blockquote><p>Note: The NDIC reports 187 rigs are active in North Dakota. That is 25 more than Baker Hughes reports in the Bakken area. The difference is likely accounted for in the number of rigs actually working, rigs that might not be serviced by Baker Hughes AND areas outside of the Bakken fairway. On any given week, a certain number of rigs are in route to the next well location or idle waiting to drill the next well. The NDIC notes that more than 10 rigs are in the process of moving in and rigging up.</p></blockquote><h2>Bakken Oil &amp; Gas Rigs</h2><p>[ic-l]The number of oil rigs running increased by one to 177. WTI oil prices were trading near $93/bbl to end the week. Williston Basin Sweet crude traded at $74.44/bbl as of Nov 29th. The WTI-Brent spread continues to widen, reaching approximately $17.</p><p>The natural gas rig count in the region held flat at zero. Natural gas futures (Henry Hub) were trading near $3.85/mmbtu on Friday. Natural gas delivered to the Northern Border pipeline in Watford City is trading closer to $3.00/mmbtu. A little more than 10% of the production stream from the Bakken and Three Forks is attributable to natural gas and roughly half of that is NGLs.</p><blockquote><p>McKenzie County continues to lead development with 61 rigs running. Dunn, Mountrail, and Williams counties are the only other counties with more than 20 rigs running. View the full list below.</p></blockquote><p>Activity is dominated by horizontal drilling: 155 rigs are drilling horizontal wells, 17 rigs are drilling directional wells, and 5 rigs are drilling vertical wells.</p><h2>Bakken Oil &amp; Gas News</h2><ul><li><a href="http://bakkenshale.com/bsp-news/news/oil-gas-industry-focused-innovation-deloitte-oil-gas-conference">Oil &amp; Gas Industry Focused on Innovation - Deloitte Oil &amp; Gas Conference</a></li><li><a href="http://bakkenshale.com/pmn/pipeline-midstream-news/dakota-plains-facility-expansion-expected-online-december?rq=Dakota%20Plains%20Facility%20Expansion%20Expected%20Online%20in%20December%20-%20Video">Dakota Plains Facility Expansion Expected Online in December - Video</a></li><li><a href="http://bakkenshale.com/pmn/pipeline-midstream-news/enbridges-sandpiper-pipeline-gains-anchor-shipper-in-marathon-petroleum-open-season-starts">Enbridge`s Sandpiper Pipeline Gains Anchor Shipper in Marathon Petroleum - Open Season</a></li></ul><p>Be sure to visit our <a href="http://jobs.bakkenshale.com/">Bakken Job Listings</a> to search openings and come back weekly for updates or sign up for alerts - Daily or Weekly Email Alerts</p><h2>Bakken Drilling by County</h2>

  <h2>What is the Rig Count?</h2><p>The Bakken Shale Rig Count is an index of the total number of oil &amp; gas drilling rigs running across Montana and North Dakota. The rigs referred to in this article are for ALL drilling reported by Baker Hughes and not solely wells targeting the Bakken formation. All land rigs and onshore rig data shown here are based upon industry estimates provided by the Baker Hughes Rig Count and/or Smith Service Co's (Schlumberger) Smith Rig Count.</p>
