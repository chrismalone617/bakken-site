<div data-sqsp-text-block-content="">
  <p>The <a href="http://bakkenshale.com/drilling">Bakken-Three Forks rig count</a>&nbsp;declined this week with Baker Hughes reporting 49 rigs running through our coverage area by Friday midday.</p><p><em>A total of <strong>935</strong>&nbsp;oil and gas rigs were running across the United States this week, one less than last week.&nbsp;<strong>190</strong>&nbsp;rigs targeted natural gas (+ four) and <strong>744</strong>&nbsp;were targeting oil in the U.S. (- five). The remainder were drilling service wells (e.g. disposal wells, injection wells, etc.) <strong>49</strong></em><em><strong>&nbsp;</strong>of the rigs active in the U.S. were running in North Dakota.</em></p><h2>Bakken Oil &amp; Gas Rigs</h2><p>49 rigs targeted oil in the Bakken this week with Bloomberg reporting WTI oil prices at $50.66 on Friday afternoon.&nbsp;The Bakken has zero natural active gas rig in the area this week with futures trading at $2.96/mmbtu by midday.</p><p>McKenzie County leads activity in the region with 22 rigs running this week. View the full list below under the <em>Bakken Drilling by County</em>&nbsp;section.</p><p>Activity continues to be dominated by horizontal drilling:</p><ul><li>49 rigs are drilling horizontal wells</li><li>0 rigs are drilling directional wells</li><li>0 rigs are drilling vertical wells</li></ul><h2>Rig Count by County</h2>
