<div data-sqsp-text-block-content="">
  <p>Just in time for Christmas, Bakken Shale operators brought more rigs on line.</p><p>The <a href="http://bakkenshale.com/drilling">Bakken-Three Forks rig count</a>&nbsp;increased for the second week in a row this week, with Baker Hughes reporting 49 rigs running through our coverage area by Friday midday.&nbsp;</p><p>In recent Bakken Shale news, a mineral rights dispute is delaying oil and gas development around Lake Sakakawea.</p><p><a href="https://bakkenshale.com/bsp-news/mineral-rights-in-bakken-under-question">Mineral Rights in Bakken Under Question</a></p><p><em>A total of <strong>931</strong>&nbsp;oil and gas rigs were running across the United States this week, one more than last week.&nbsp;<strong>184</strong>&nbsp;rigs were targeting natural gas (+1) and 747 were targeting oil in the U.S. (+0). The remainder were drilling service wells (e.g. disposal wells, injection wells, etc.) 48<strong>&nbsp;</strong>of the rigs active in the U.S. were running in North Dakota.</em></p><h2>Bakken Oil &amp; Gas Rigs</h2><p>49 rigs targeted oil in the Bakken this week with Bloomberg reporting WTI oil prices at $58.47 on Friday afternoon.&nbsp;The Bakken has zero natural active gas rig in the area this week with commodity futures trading at $2.67/mmbtu by midday.</p><p><a href="http://www.mineralrightsforum.com/group/bakken-shale-nd-mt">Own mineral rights in the Bakken? Join the discussion here.&nbsp;</a></p><p>McKenzie County leads activity in the region with 23 rigs running this week. View the full list below under the <em>Bakken Drilling by County</em>&nbsp;section.</p><p>Activity continues to be dominated by horizontal drilling:</p><ul><li>49 rigs are drilling horizontal wells</li><li>0 rigs are drilling directional wells</li><li>0 rigs are drilling vertical wells</li></ul><h2>Rig Count by County</h2>
