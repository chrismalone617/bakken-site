<div data-sqsp-text-block-content="">
  <p>The <a href="/drilling">Bakken-Three Forks rig count</a>&nbsp;lost ground this week with Baker Hughes reporting 27 rigs running across our coverage area by midday Friday.</p><p>In recent Bakken news,&nbsp;North Dakota's Department of Mineral Resources released the latest data showing that state oil production dropped 2% in June, falling to its lowest level since 2014.</p><p><a href="/bsp-news/news/north-dakota-oil-production-at-lowest-level-since-2014">Read more: North Dakota Production Falls to Lowest in Years</a></p><p><em>A total of <strong>489</strong>&nbsp;oil and gas rigs were running across the United States this week, and increase from last week of 10.&nbsp;<strong>83</strong>&nbsp;were targeting natural gas (same more than the previous week) and <strong>406</strong>&nbsp;were targeting oil in the U.S. (10 more than the previous week). The remainder were drilling service wells (e.g. disposal wells, injection wells, etc.)</em><span>&nbsp;</span><em><strong>27</strong>&nbsp;of the rigs active in the U.S. were running in North Dakota.</em></p><h2>Bakken Oil &amp; Gas Rigs</h2><p>Bakken oil rigs were at 27 this week with Bloomberg reporting WTI oil prices up to $<span>48.52</span>&nbsp;on Friday afternoon. WTI-Brent also ended the week a bit higher with Bloomberg reporting $<span>50.88</span>.&nbsp;The Bakken has zero natural active gas rig in the area this week with futures trading at $2.57/mmbtu by midday.</p><p>McKenzie County leads development with 13 rigs running this week. View the full list below under the<em>Bakken Drilling by County</em>&nbsp;section.</p><p>Activity continues to be dominated by horizontal drilling:</p><ul><li>27 rigs are drilling horizontal wells</li><li>0 rigs are drilling directional wells</li><li>0 rigs are drilling vertical wells</li></ul><h2>Bakken Drilling by County</h2>
