  <p>The <a href="/drilling">Bakken-Three Forks rig count</a>&nbsp;remained flat this week, with Baker Hughes reporting 37 rigs running across our coverage area by midday Friday.</p><p>Rig counts across the country are slowly climbing as conditions in the oil and gas industry continues to improve. But the Bakken doesn't seem to be keeping up with the growth of other shale plays. The <a href="http://eaglefordshale.com/news/the-eagle-ford-rig-count-climbs-to-68/">Eagle Ford Shale rig count</a> is at 68 this week, a 50% increase in the last six months.</p><p><em>A total of <strong>728</strong>&nbsp;oil and gas rigs were running across the United States this week, a gain of 27 over last week.&nbsp;<strong>145</strong>&nbsp;rigs targeted natural gas (the same as the previous week) and <strong>583</strong>&nbsp;were targeting oil in the U.S. (27 more than the previous week). The remainder were drilling service wells (e.g. disposal wells, injection wells, etc.)</em><span>&nbsp;</span><em><strong>36</strong>&nbsp;of the rigs active in the U.S. were running in North Dakota.</em></p><h2>Bakken Oil &amp; Gas Rigs</h2><p>Bakken oil rigs were at 37 this week with Bloomberg reporting WTI oil prices increasing to $53.80 on Friday afternoon. Prices for WTI-Brent also increased to per barrel of $56.75.&nbsp;The Bakken has zero natural active gas rig in the area this week with futures trading at $3.06/mmbtu by midday.</p><p>McKenzie County leads production in the region with 17 rigs running this week. View the full list below under the <em>Bakken Drilling by County</em>&nbsp;section.</p><p>Activity continues to be dominated by horizontal drilling:</p><ul><li>37 rigs are drilling horizontal wells</li><li>0 rigs are drilling directional wells</li><li>0 rigs are drilling vertical wells</li></ul><h2>Rig Count by County</h2>
