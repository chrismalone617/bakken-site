  <p>The <a href="http://bakkenshale.com/drilling">Bakken-Three Forks rig count</a>&nbsp;remained flat this week with 26 rigs running across our coverage area by midday Friday.</p><p>In recent Bakken news,&nbsp;<span>Bakken Shale operators and industry officials applaud the recent federal court ruling that declared new fracking rules unlawful.</span></p><p><a href="http://bakkenshale.com/bsp-news/news/bakken-applaud-recent-ruling-on-frack-regulations">Read more:&nbsp;</a><a href="http://bakkenshale.com/bsp-news/news/bakken-applaud-recent-ruling-on-frack-regulations">Bakken Applauds Frack Decision</a></p><p><em>A total of <strong>430</strong>&nbsp;oil and gas rigs were running across the United States this week, an increase of 10 over last week.&nbsp;<strong>89</strong>&nbsp;were targeting natural gas (one less than the previous week) and <strong>341</strong>&nbsp;were targeting oil in the U.S. (11 more than the previous week). The remainder were drilling service wells (e.g. disposal wells, injection wells, etc.)</em><span>&nbsp;</span><span>&nbsp;</span><em><strong>26</strong>&nbsp;of the rigs active in the U.S. were running in North Dakota.</em></p><h2>Bakken Oil &amp; Gas Rigs</h2><p>Bakken oil rigs were at 26 this week with Bloomberg reporting WTI oil prices rose to $49.18 on Friday afternoon. WTI-Brent saw a slight increase as it climbed over the $50 mark this week to $<span>50.63</span>.&nbsp;The Bakken has zero natural active gas rig in the area this week with futures trading at $2.93/mmbtu by midday.</p><p>McKenzie County leads development with 12 rigs running this week. View the full list below under the <em>Bakken Drilling by County</em>&nbsp;section.</p><p>Activity continues to be dominated by horizontal drilling:</p><ul><li>26 rigs are drilling horizontal wells</li><li>0 rigs are drilling directional wells</li><li>0 rigs are drilling vertical wells</li></ul><p><a href="http://jobs.bakkenshale.com/">Be sure to visit our Bakken Job Listings to search openings and come back weekly for updates or sign up for alerts - Daily or Weekly Email Alerts</a></p><h2>Bakken Drilling by County</h2>
