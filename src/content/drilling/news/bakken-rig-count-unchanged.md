<div data-sqsp-text-block-content="">
  <p>The <a href="http://bakkenshale.com/drilling">Bakken-Three Forks rig count</a>&nbsp;remained flat this week, with Baker Hughes reporting 38 rigs running across our coverage area by midday Friday.</p><p>In recent Bakken news, ExxonMobil executives announced their 2017 capital spending program to be around $22 billion, an increase of 16 percent from 2016. Of that amount, they will allocate $5.5 billion for drilling in Bakken Shale and Permian basin.</p><p>Read more:&nbsp;<a href="https://bakkenshale.com/bsp-news/news/exxon-bets-on-bakken-shale">ExxonMobil Bets on U.S. Shale</a></p><p><em>A total of <strong>768</strong>&nbsp;oil and gas rigs were running across the United States this week, an increase of 12 over than last week.&nbsp;<strong>151</strong>&nbsp;rigs targeted natural gas (five more than the previous week) and <strong>617</strong>&nbsp;were targeting oil in the U.S. (eight more than the previous week). The remainder were drilling service wells (e.g. disposal wells, injection wells, etc.)</em>&nbsp;&nbsp;<em><strong>37</strong>&nbsp;of the rigs active in the U.S. were running in North Dakota.</em></p><h2>Bakken Oil &amp; Gas Rigs</h2><p>Bakken oil rigs were at 38 this week with Bloomberg reporting WTI oil prices falling to $48.49 on Friday afternoon.&nbsp;The Bakken has zero natural active gas rig in the area this week with futures trading at $3.01/mmbtu by midday.</p><p>McKenzie County leads production in the region with 18 rigs running this week. View the full list below under the <em>Bakken Drilling by County</em>&nbsp;section.</p><p>Activity continues to be dominated by horizontal drilling:</p><ul><li>38 rigs are drilling horizontal wells</li><li>0 rigs are drilling directional wells</li><li>0 rigs are drilling vertical wells</li></ul><h2>Rig Count by County</h2>
