<div data-sqsp-text-block-content="">
  <p>The <a href="http://bakkenshale.com/drilling">Bakken-Three Forks rig count</a>&nbsp;has stalled for the past three weeks, with Baker Hughes reporting 37 rigs running across our coverage area by midday Friday.</p><p><em>A total of <strong>740</strong>&nbsp;oil and gas rigs were running across the United States this week, a gain of 12 over last week.&nbsp;<strong>149</strong>&nbsp;rigs targeted natural gas (4 more than the previous week) and <strong>591</strong>&nbsp;were targeting oil in the U.S. (eight more than the previous week). The remainder were drilling service wells (e.g. disposal wells, injection wells, etc.)</em>&nbsp;<em><strong>36</strong>&nbsp;of the rigs active in the U.S. were running in North Dakota.</em></p><h2>Bakken Oil &amp; Gas Rigs</h2><p>Bakken oil rigs were at 37 this week with Bloomberg reporting WTI oil prices increasing to $53.86 on Friday afternoon. Prices for WTI-Brent dipped per barrel to $56.70.&nbsp;The Bakken has zero natural active gas rig in the area this week with futures trading at $3.03/mmbtu by midday.</p><p>McKenzie County leads production in the region with 17 rigs running this week. View the full list below under the <em>Bakken Drilling by County</em>&nbsp;section.</p><p>Activity continues to be dominated by horizontal drilling:</p><ul><li>36 rigs are drilling horizontal wells</li><li>0 rigs are drilling directional wells</li><li>1 rigs are drilling vertical wells</li></ul><h2>Rig Count by County</h2>
