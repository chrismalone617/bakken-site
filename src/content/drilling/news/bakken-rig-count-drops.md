  <p>The <a href="http://bakkenshale.com/drilling">Bakken-Three Forks rig count</a>&nbsp;dropped again this week, with Baker Hughes reporting 35 rigs running across our coverage area by midday Friday.</p><p>In recent Bakken news, Marathon Oil announced their 2016 results last week including plans to increase Bakken Shale activity.</p><p><a href="https://bakkenshale.com/bsp-news/news/marathon-announces-2017-plan-for-bakken">Read more:&nbsp;</a><a href="https://bakkenshale.com/bsp-news/news/marathon-announces-2017-plan-for-bakken">M</a><a href="https://bakkenshale.com/bsp-news/news/marathon-announces-2017-plan-for-bakken">arathon Announces 2017 Bakken Plan</a></p><p><em>A total of <strong>754</strong>&nbsp;oil and gas rigs were running across the United States this week, an increase of four over than last week.&nbsp;<strong>151</strong>&nbsp;rigs targeted natural gas (2 less than the previous week) and <strong>602</strong>&nbsp;were targeting oil in the U.S. (five more than the previous week). The remainder were drilling service wells (e.g. disposal wells, injection wells, etc.)</em>&nbsp;<em><strong>34</strong>&nbsp;of the rigs active in the U.S. were running in North Dakota.</em></p><h2>Bakken Oil &amp; Gas Rigs</h2><p>Bakken oil rigs were at 35 this week with Bloomberg reporting WTI oil prices increasing to $53.99 on Friday afternoon.&nbsp;The Bakken has zero natural active gas rig in the area this week with futures trading at $2.79/mmbtu by midday.</p><p>McKenzie County leads production in the region with 18 rigs running this week. View the full list below under the <em>Bakken Drilling by County</em>&nbsp;section.</p><p>Activity continues to be dominated by horizontal drilling:</p><ul><li>35 rigs are drilling horizontal wells</li><li>0 rigs are drilling directional wells</li><li>0 rigs are drilling vertical wells</li></ul><h2>Rig Count by County</h2>
