<div data-sqsp-text-block-content="">
  <p>For the third week in a row, the <a href="http://bakkenshale.com/drilling">Bakken-Three Forks rig count</a>&nbsp;has stalled with Baker Hughes reporting 27 rigs running across our coverage area by midday Friday.</p><p>In recent Bakken news, Australian-based, Samson Oil &amp; Gas Ltd. plans to sell its interest in the North Stockyard field in North Dakota in order to reduce its debt.&nbsp;The deal is scheduled to close on August 31st, 2016.</p><p><a href="http://bakkenshale.com/bsp-news/news/samson-sells-bakken-assets">Read more: Samson Sells Bakken Assets</a></p><p><em>A total of <strong>460</strong>&nbsp;oil and gas rigs were running across the United States this week, up one from last week.&nbsp;<strong>86</strong>&nbsp;were targeting natural gas (two less than the previous week) and <strong>374</strong>&nbsp;were targeting oil in the U.S. (three more than the previous week). The remainder were drilling service wells (e.g. disposal wells, injection wells, etc.)</em><span>&nbsp;</span><span>&nbsp;</span><em><strong>27</strong>&nbsp;of the rigs active in the U.S. were running in North Dakota.</em></p><h2>Bakken Oil &amp; Gas Rigs</h2><p>Bakken oil rigs were at 27 this week with Bloomberg reporting WTI oil prices dipping to $<span>41.60</span>&nbsp;on Friday afternoon. WTI-Brent also ended the week lower with Bloomberg reporting $43.53.&nbsp;The Bakken has zero natural active gas rig in the area this week with futures trading at $2.88/mmbtu by midday.</p><p>McKenzie County leads development with 11 rigs running this week. View the full list below under the <em>Bakken Drilling by County</em>&nbsp;section.</p><p>Activity continues to be dominated by horizontal drilling:</p><ul><li>27 rigs are drilling horizontal wells</li><li>0 rigs are drilling directional wells</li><li>0 rigs are drilling vertical wells</li></ul><h2>Bakken Drilling by County</h2>
