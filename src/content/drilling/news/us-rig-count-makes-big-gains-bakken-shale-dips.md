<div data-sqsp-text-block-content="">
  <p>The <a href="http://bakkenshale.com/drilling">Bakken-Three Forks rig count</a>&nbsp;fell back this week, with Baker Hughes reporting 36 rigs running across our coverage area by midday Friday.</p><p>In recent Bakken news, North Dakota legislators voted in favor of House Bill 1151, which eliminates a reporting requirement for oil spills under 10 barrels. The 82-11 vote was a clear victory for companies who say the measure will save them time and money.&nbsp;</p><p>Read more:&nbsp;<a href="https://bakkenshale.com/bsp-news/news/ppd6hoq2g6exdj04ax0dzf20qw9tmh">North Dakota Bill Eliminates Oil Spill Reporting</a></p><p><em>A total of </em><strong><em>740</em></strong><em>&nbsp;oil and gas rigs were running across the United States this week, a gain of 12 over last week.&nbsp;</em><strong><em>149</em></strong><em>&nbsp;rigs targeted natural gas (4 more than the previous week) and </em><strong><em>591</em></strong><em>&nbsp;were targeting oil in the U.S. (eight more than the previous week). The remainder were drilling service wells (e.g. disposal wells, injection wells, etc.)</em>&nbsp;<em><strong>35</strong>&nbsp;of the rigs active in the U.S. were running in North Dakota.</em></p><h2>Bakken Oil &amp; Gas Rigs</h2><p>Bakken oil rigs were at 36 this week with Bloomberg reporting WTI oil prices dipping to $53.40 on Friday afternoon. Prices for WTI-Brent also dropped per barrel to $55.81.&nbsp;The Bakken has zero natural active gas rig in the area this week with futures trading at $2.83/mmbtu by midday.</p><p>McKenzie County leads production in the region with 18 rigs running this week. View the full list below under the <em>Bakken Drilling by County</em>&nbsp;section.</p><p>Activity continues to be dominated by horizontal drilling:</p><ul><li>36 rigs are drilling horizontal wells</li><li>0 rigs are drilling directional wells</li><li>0 rigs are drilling vertical wells</li></ul><h2>Rig Count by County</h2>
