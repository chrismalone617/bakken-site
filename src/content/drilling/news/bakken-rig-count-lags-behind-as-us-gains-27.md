<div data-sqsp-text-block-content="">
  <p>The <a href="http://bakkenshale.com/drilling">Bakken-Three Forks rig count</a>&nbsp;inches up by one this week with Baker Hughes reporting 32 rigs running across our coverage area by midday Friday..</p><p>In recent Bakken news,&nbsp;the U.S. Army Corps of Engineers reversed its position and denied Energy Transfer Partners the permit needed to complete the Dakota Access Pipeline.&nbsp;This latest set-backs means producers will be forced to rely on rail to ship product.</p><p><a href="https://bakkenshale.com/bsp-news/news/dapl">Read more: If Pipelines Fail, Crude by Rail will Increase</a></p><p><em>A total of <strong>623</strong>&nbsp;oil and gas rigs were running across the United States this week, a gain of 27 over last week.&nbsp;<strong>125</strong>&nbsp;rigs targeted natural gas (six more than the previous week) and <strong>498</strong>&nbsp;were targeting oil in the U.S. (21 more than the previous week). The remainder were drilling service wells (e.g. disposal wells, injection wells, etc.)</em>&nbsp;<em><strong>32</strong>&nbsp;of the rigs active in the U.S. were running in North Dakota.</em></p><h2>Bakken Oil &amp; Gas Rigs</h2><p>Bakken oil rigs were at 32 this week with Bloomberg reporting WTI oil prices dropping slightly to $51.50 on Friday afternoon. Prices for WTI-Brent also dipped to a per barrel price of $54.33.&nbsp;The Bakken has zero natural active gas rig in the area this week with futures trading at $3.75/mmbtu by midday.</p><p>McKenzie County leads production in the region with 15 rigs running this week. View the full list below under the <em>Bakken Drilling by County</em>&nbsp;section.</p><p>Activity continues to be dominated by horizontal drilling:</p><ul><li>32 rigs are drilling horizontal wells</li><li>0 rigs are drilling directional wells</li><li>0 rigs are drilling vertical wells</li></ul><h2>Bakken Drilling by County</h2>
