<div data-sqsp-text-block-content="">
  <p>The <a href="http://bakkenshale.com/drilling">Bakken-Three Forks rig count</a>&nbsp;continued to gain this week with Baker Hughes reporting 37 rigs running across our coverage area by midday Friday.</p><p>In recent Bakken news, Oasis Petroleum announced it has signed an agreement to purchase the drilling rights to approximately 55,000 net acres in the Williston Basin from SM Energy Company.</p><p><a href="http://bakkenshale.com/bsp-news/news/oasis-petroleum-buys-55000-acres-in-the-bakken">Read more: Oasis Purchases More Bakken Acreage</a></p><p><em>A total of <strong>567</strong>&nbsp;oil and gas rigs were running across the United States this week, a gain of 12 over last week.&nbsp;<strong>117</strong>&nbsp;rigs targeted natural gas (three more than the previous week) and <strong>450</strong>&nbsp;were targeting oil in the U.S. (nine more than the previous week). The remainder were drilling service wells (e.g. disposal wells, injection wells, etc.)&nbsp;</em><em><strong>37</strong>&nbsp;of the rigs active in the U.S. were running in North Dakota.</em></p><h2>Bakken Oil &amp; Gas Rigs</h2><p>Bakken oil rigs were at 37 this week with Bloomberg reporting WTI oil prices dropping $4.57 to $44.13 on Friday afternoon. WTI-Brent also fell, with Bloomberg reporting a per barrel price of $45.60.&nbsp;The Bakken has zero natural active gas rig in the area this week with futures trading at $2.78/mmbtu by midday.</p><p>McKenzie County leads production in the region with 14 rigs running this week. View the full list below under the <em>Bakken Drilling by County</em>&nbsp;section.</p><p>Activity continues to be dominated by horizontal drilling:</p><ul><li>36 rigs are drilling horizontal wells</li><li>1 rigs are drilling directional wells</li><li>0 rigs are drilling vertical wells</li></ul><h2>Bakken Drilling by County</h2>
