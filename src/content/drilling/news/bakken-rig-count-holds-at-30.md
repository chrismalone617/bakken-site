  <p>The <a href="http://bakkenshale.com/drilling">Bakken-Three Forks rig count</a>&nbsp;remained flat this week with Baker Hughes reporting 30 rigs running across our coverage area by midday Friday.</p><p>In recent Bakken news,&nbsp;SM Energy Company announced that they are exploring the sale of certain Bakken assets in the Williston Basin.&nbsp;</p><p><a href="http://bakkenshale.com/bsp-news/news/sm-energy-to-sell-54500-bakken-acres">Read more:&nbsp;</a><a href="http://bakkenshale.com/bsp-news/news/sm-energy-to-sell-54500-bakken-acres">SM Sells 54,500 Bakken Acres</a></p><p><em>A total of <strong>522</strong>&nbsp;oil and gas rigs were running across the United States this week, an increase of one from last week of two.&nbsp;<strong>94</strong>&nbsp;rigs targeted natural gas (two less than the previous week) and <strong>428</strong>&nbsp;were targeting oil in the U.S. (three more than the previous week). The remainder were drilling service wells (e.g. disposal wells, injection wells, etc.)</em><span>&nbsp;30</span><em>&nbsp;of the rigs active in the U.S. were running in North Dakota.</em></p><h2>Bakken Oil &amp; Gas Rigs</h2><p>Bakken oil rigs were at 30 this week with Bloomberg reporting WTI oil prices gaining $1.57 to $49.81 on Friday afternoon. WTI-Brent was also up with Bloomberg reporting $51.84 The Bakken has zero natural active gas rig in the area this week with futures trading at $3.19/mmbtu by midday.</p><p>Dunn County leads production in the region with 11 rigs running this week. View the full list below under the <em>Bakken Drilling by County</em>&nbsp;section.</p><p>Activity continues to be dominated by horizontal drilling:</p><ul><li>29 rigs are drilling horizontal wells</li><li>1 rigs are drilling directional wells</li><li>0 rigs are drilling vertical wells</li></ul><h2>Bakken Drilling by County</h2>
