  <p>The <a href="/drilling">Bakken-Three Forks rig count</a>&nbsp;drops by two this week with Baker Hughes reporting 31 rigs running across our coverage area by midday Friday..</p><p>In recent Bakken news,&nbsp;Whiting Petroleum has announced its intention to sell its Bakken midstream assets to a subsidiary of Tesoro Logistics.</p><p><a href="/bsp-news/news/whiting-petroleum-sells-bakken-assets">R</a><a href="/bsp-news/news/whiting-petroleum-sells-bakken-assets">ead more: Whiting to Sell Bakken Assets</a></p><p><em>A total of <strong>596</strong>&nbsp;oil and gas rigs were running across the United States this week, a gain of four over last week.&nbsp;<strong>119</strong>&nbsp;rigs targeted natural gas (one more than the previous week) and <strong>477</strong>&nbsp;were targeting oil in the U.S. (three more than the previous week). The remainder were drilling service wells (e.g. disposal wells, injection wells, etc.)</em><span>&nbsp;</span><em><strong>31</strong>&nbsp;of the rigs active in the U.S. were running in North Dakota.</em></p><h2>Bakken Oil &amp; Gas Rigs</h2><p>Bakken oil rigs were at 31 this week with Bloomberg reporting WTI oil prices increased $4.25 to $<span>51.68</span> on Friday afternoon. Prices for WTI-Brent also rose to a per barrel price of $54.46.&nbsp;The Bakken has zero natural active gas rig in the area this week with futures trading at $3.46/mmbtu by midday.</p><p>McKenzie County leads production in the region with 13 rigs running this week.
