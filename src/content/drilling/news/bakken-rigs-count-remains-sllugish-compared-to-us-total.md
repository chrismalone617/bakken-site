<div data-sqsp-text-block-content="">
  <p>The <a href="http://bakkenshale.com/drilling">Bakken-Three Forks rig count</a>&nbsp;continued to shrink this week as U.S. counts increase by 20.&nbsp;Baker Hughes reports 34 rigs running across our coverage area by midday Friday.</p><p><em>A total of <strong>587</strong>&nbsp;oil and gas rigs were running across the United States this week, a gain of 20 over last week.&nbsp;<strong>116</strong>&nbsp;rigs targeted natural gas (one more than the previous week) and <strong>471</strong>&nbsp;were targeting oil in the U.S. (19 more than the previous week). The remainder were drilling service wells (e.g. disposal wells, injection wells, etc.)</em><span>&nbsp;</span><em><strong>34</strong>&nbsp;of the rigs active in the U.S. were running in North Dakota.</em></p><h2>Bakken Oil &amp; Gas Rigs</h2><p>Bakken oil rigs were at 34 this week with Bloomberg reporting WTI oil prices increased $2.23 to $<span>45.69</span> on Friday afternoon. Prices for WTI-Brent also rose to a per barrel price of $46.86.&nbsp;The Bakken has zero natural active gas rig in the area this week with futures trading at $2.85/mmbtu by midday.</p><p>McKenzie County leads production in the region with 14 rigs running this week. View the full list below under the <em>Bakken Drilling by County</em>&nbsp;section.</p><p>Activity continues to be dominated by horizontal drilling:</p><ul><li>34 rigs are drilling horizontal wells</li><li>0 rigs are drilling directional wells</li><li>0 rigs are drilling vertical wells</li></ul><h2>Bakken Drilling by County</h2>
