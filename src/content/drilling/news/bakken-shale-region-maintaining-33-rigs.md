<div data-sqsp-text-block-content="">
  <p>The <a href="/drilling">Bakken-Three Forks rig count</a>&nbsp;continued to fall this week with Baker Hughes reporting 33 rigs running across our coverage area by midday Wednesday..</p><p>In recent Bakken news,&nbsp;North Dakota's Department of Mineral Resources announced that the state's crude-oil production dropped in September to the lowest level in more than two years.</p><p><a href="/bsp-news/news/north-dakota-production-drops">Read more: Helms predicts oil to go lower for longer</a></p><p><em>A total of <strong>592</strong>&nbsp;oil and gas rigs were running across the United States this week, a gain of five over last week.&nbsp;<strong>118</strong>&nbsp;rigs targeted natural gas (two more than the previous week) and <strong>474</strong>&nbsp;were targeting oil in the U.S. (three more than the previous week). The remainder were drilling service wells (e.g. disposal wells, injection wells, etc.)</em><span>&nbsp;</span><em><strong>33</strong>&nbsp;of the rigs active in the U.S. were running in North Dakota.</em></p><h2>Bakken Oil &amp; Gas Rigs</h2><p>Bakken oil rigs were at 33 this week with Bloomberg reporting WTI oil prices increased $1.74 to $47.43 on Wednesday afternoon. Prices for WTI-Brent also rose to a per barrel price of $48.42.&nbsp;The Bakken has zero natural active gas rig in the area this week with futures trading at $3.04/mmbtu by midday.</p><p>McKenzie County leads production in the region but dropped one to 13 rigs running this week. View the full list below under the <em>Bakken Drilling by County</em>&nbsp;section.</p><p>Activity continues to be dominated by horizontal drilling:</p><ul><li>33 rigs are drilling horizontal wells</li><li>0 rigs are drilling directional wells</li><li>0 rigs are drilling vertical wells</li></ul><h2>Bakken Drilling by County</h2>
