  <p>The <a href="/drilling">Bakken-Three Forks rig count</a>&nbsp;remained flat this week,&nbsp;with 24 rigs running across our coverage area by midday Friday.</p><p><em>A total of <strong>423</strong>&nbsp;oil and gas rigs were running across the United States this week, an increase of 10 over last week.&nbsp;<strong>85</strong>&nbsp;were targeting natural gas (one more than the previous week) and <strong>337</strong>&nbsp;were targeting oil in the U.S. (nine more than the previous week). The remainder were drilling service wells (e.g. disposal wells, injection wells, etc.)&nbsp;<strong>24</strong> of the rigs active in the U.S. were running in North Dakota.</em></p><h2>Bakken Oil &amp; Gas Rigs</h2><p>Bakken oil rigs were at 24 this week with Bloomberg reporting WTI oil prices at $47.98 on Friday afternoon. WTI-Brent decreasing this week to $49.17.&nbsp;The Bakken has zero natural active gas rig in the area this week with futures trading to $2.62/mmbtu by midday.</p><p>McKenzie County leads development with 11 rigs running this week, far outpacing other counties. View the full list below under the <em>Bakken Drilling by County</em>&nbsp;section.</p><p>Activity continues to be dominated by horizontal drilling:</p><ul><li>24 rigs are drilling horizontal wells</li><li>0 rigs are drilling directional wells</li><li>0 rigs are drilling vertical wells</li></ul><p><a href="http://jobs.bakkenshale.com/">Be sure to visit our Bakken Job Listings to search openings and come back weekly for updates or sign up for alerts - Daily or Weekly Email Alerts</a></p><h2>Bakken Drilling by County</h2>
