<div data-sqsp-text-block-content="">
  <p>The <a href="http://bakkenshale.com/drilling">Bakken-Three Forks rig count</a>&nbsp;gained one this week with Baker Hughes reporting 28 rigs running across our coverage area by midday Friday.</p><p>In recent Bakken news,&nbsp;Energy Transfer Partners, L.P. announced it will sell 36.75% of the Bakken Pipeline Project to Marathon and Enbridge.&nbsp;</p><p><a href="http://bakkenshale.com/bsp-news/news/energy-transfer-partners-sell-bakken-assets">Read more: D</a><a href="http://bakkenshale.com/bsp-news/news/energy-transfer-partners-sell-bakken-assets">alrymple Issues State of Emergency</a></p><p><em>A total of <strong>495</strong>&nbsp;oil and gas rigs were running across the United States this week, an increase of eight from last week of two. &nbsp;<strong>88</strong>&nbsp;rigs targeted natural gas (seven more than the previous week) and <strong>407</strong>&nbsp;were targeting oil in the U.S. (one more than the previous week). The remainder were drilling service wells (e.g. disposal wells, injection wells, etc.)</em><span>&nbsp;</span><em><strong>28</strong>&nbsp;of the rigs active in the U.S. were running in North Dakota.</em></p><h2>Bakken Oil &amp; Gas Rigs</h2><p>Bakken oil rigs were at 28 this week with Bloomberg reporting WTI oil prices dipping to $44.44 on Friday afternoon. WTI-Brent also fell backwards with Bloomberg reporting $46.83.&nbsp;The Bakken has zero natural active gas rig in the area this week with futures trading at $2.79/mmbtu by midday.</p><p>McKenzie County leads development with 12 rigs running this week. View the full list below under the<em>Bakken Drilling by County</em>&nbsp;section.</p><p>Activity continues to be dominated by horizontal drilling:</p><ul><li>28 rigs are drilling horizontal wells</li><li>0 rigs are drilling directional wells</li><li>0 rigs are drilling vertical wells</li></ul><h2>Bakken Drilling by County</h2>
