<div data-sqsp-text-block-content="">
  <p>The <a href="/drilling">Bakken-Three Forks rig count</a>&nbsp;dropped back down this week with Baker Hughes reporting 27 rigs running across our coverage area by midday Friday.</p><p>In recent Bakken news, Lynn Helms, the director of the N.D. Department of Mineral Resources, spoke to a U.S. House Energy and Commerce Subcommittee last week lending his voice to the growing opposition to the proposed regulations by the Environmental Protection Agency.<span>&nbsp;</span></p><p><a href="/bsp-news/news/lynn-helms-urges-lawmakers-over-po">Read more: L</a><a href="/bsp-news/news/lynn-helms-urges-lawmakers-over-po">ynn Helms Testifies About Clean Air Act</a></p><p><em>A total of <strong>446</strong>&nbsp;oil and gas rigs were running across the United States this week, up seven over last week.&nbsp;<strong>89</strong>&nbsp;were targeting natural gas (one more than the previous week) and <strong>357</strong>&nbsp;were targeting oil in the U.S. (six more than the previous week). The remainder were drilling service wells (e.g. disposal wells, injection wells, etc.)</em><span>&nbsp;</span><em><strong>27</strong>&nbsp;of the rigs active in the U.S. were running in North Dakota.</em></p><h2>Bakken Oil &amp; Gas Rigs</h2><p>Bakken oil rigs were at 27 this week with Bloomberg reporting WTI oil prices increasing to $<span>45.95</span>&nbsp;on Friday afternoon. WTI-Brent was also up to ending the week at $47.61.&nbsp;The Bakken has zero natural active gas rig in the area this week with futures trading at $2.76/mmbtu by midday.</p><p>McKenzie County leads development with 12 rigs running this week, far outpacing other counties. View the full list below under the <em>Bakken Drilling by County</em>&nbsp;section.</p><p>Activity continues to be dominated by horizontal drilling:</p><ul><li>27 rigs are drilling horizontal wells</li><li>0 rigs are drilling directional wells</li><li>0 rigs are drilling vertical wells</li></ul><h2>Bakken Drilling by County</h2>
