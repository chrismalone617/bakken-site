<div data-sqsp-text-block-content="">
  <p>The <a href="http://bakkenshale.com/drilling">Bakken-Three Forks rig count</a>&nbsp;fell back this week with Baker Hughes reporting 35 rigs running across our coverage area by midday Friday.</p><p>In recent Bakken news, The Hess Corporation announced its 2016 third quarter results, which included a drop in Bakken production.</p><p><a href="https://bakkenshale.com/bsp-news/news/hess-adds-bakken-rigs">R</a><a href="https://bakkenshale.com/bsp-news/news/hess-adds-bakken-rigs">ead more: Hess Corp Adds bakken Rigs</a></p><p><em>A total of <strong>567</strong>&nbsp;oil and gas rigs were running across the United States this week, a gain of zero over last week.&nbsp;<strong>115</strong>&nbsp;rigs targeted natural gas (two less than the previous week) and <strong>452</strong>&nbsp;were targeting oil in the U.S. (two more than the previous week). The remainder were drilling service wells (e.g. disposal wells, injection wells, etc.)</em><span>&nbsp;</span><em>&nbsp;<strong>35</strong>&nbsp;of the rigs active in the U.S. were running in North Dakota.</em></p><h2>Bakken Oil &amp; Gas Rigs</h2><p>Bakken oil rigs were at 35 this week with Bloomberg reporting WTI oil prices dropping $.67 to $43.46 on Friday afternoon. WTI-Brent also fell, with Bloomberg reporting a per barrel price of $44.79.&nbsp;The Bakken has zero natural active gas rig in the area this week with futures trading at $2.62/mmbtu by midday.</p><p>McKenzie County leads production in the region with 15 rigs running this week. View the full list below under the <em>Bakken Drilling by County</em>&nbsp;section.</p><p>Activity continues to be dominated by horizontal drilling:</p><ul><li>35 rigs are drilling horizontal wells</li><li>0 rigs are drilling directional wells</li><li>0 rigs are drilling vertical wells</li></ul><h2>Bakken Drilling by County</h2>
