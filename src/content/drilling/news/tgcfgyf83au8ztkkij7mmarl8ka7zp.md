<div data-sqsp-text-block-content="">
  <p>The <a href="http://bakkenshale.com/drilling">Bakken-Three Forks rig count</a>&nbsp;slowed this week, with Baker Hughes reporting 52 rigs running through our coverage area by Friday midday.&nbsp;&nbsp;</p><p><em>A total of <strong>940</strong>&nbsp;oil and gas rigs were running across the United States this week, a decrease of one over last week.&nbsp;<strong>184</strong>&nbsp;rigs targeted natural gas (one more than the previous week) and <strong>756</strong>&nbsp;were targeting oil in the U.S. (two less than the previous week). The remainder were drilling service wells (e.g. disposal wells, injection wells, etc.)&nbsp;<strong>52 </strong>of the rigs active in the U.S. were running in North Dakota.</em></p><h2>Bakken Oil &amp; Gas Rigs</h2><p>Bakken oil rigs were at 52 this week with Bloomberg reporting WTI oil prices at $46.04 on Friday afternoon.&nbsp;The Bakken has zero natural active gas rig in the area this week with futures trading at $3.04/mmbtu by midday.</p><p>McKenzie County leads production in the region with 21 rigs running this week. View the full list below under the <em>Bakken Drilling by County</em>&nbsp;section.</p><p>Activity continues to be dominated by horizontal drilling:</p><ul><li>52 rigs are drilling horizontal wells</li><li>0 rigs are drilling directional wells</li><li>0 rigs are drilling vertical wells</li></ul><h2>Rig Count by County</h2>

  <h2>What is the Rig Count?</h2><p>The Bakken Shale Rig Count is an index of the total number of oil &amp; gas drilling rigs running across Montana and North Dakota. The rigs referred to in this article are for ALL drilling reported by Baker Hughes and not solely wells targeting the Bakken formation.
