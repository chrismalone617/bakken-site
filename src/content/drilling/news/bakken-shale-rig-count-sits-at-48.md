  <p>The <a href="http://bakkenshale.com/drilling">Bakken-Three Forks rig count</a>&nbsp;was unchanged this week, with 48 rigs running through our coverage area by Friday midday.</p><p><em>A total of <strong>907</strong>&nbsp;oil and gas rigs were running across the United States this week, nine more than last week.&nbsp;<strong>169</strong>&nbsp;rigs were targeting natural gas (0 change) and <strong>738</strong>&nbsp;were targeting oil in the U.S. (+9). The remainder were drilling service wells (e.g. disposal wells, injection wells, etc.) 48</em><em><strong>&nbsp;</strong>of the rigs active in the U.S. were running in North Dakota.</em></p><h2>Bakken Oil &amp; Gas Rigs</h2><p>48 rigs targeted oil in the Bakken this week with Bloomberg reporting WTI oil prices at $56.74 on Friday afternoon.&nbsp;The Bakken has zero natural active gas rig in the area this week with commodity futures trading at $3.21/mmbtu by midday.</p><p><a href="http://www.mineralrightsforum.com/group/bakken-shale-nd-mt">Own mineral rights in the Bakken?
