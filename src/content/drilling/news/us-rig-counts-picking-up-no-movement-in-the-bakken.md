  <p>The <a href="http://bakkenshale.com/drilling">Bakken-Three Forks rig count</a>&nbsp;has stalled as rigs counts increase around the country. The region remained flat this week with Baker Hughes reporting 27 rigs running across our coverage area by midday Friday.</p><p>In recent Bakken news, the North Dakota’s attorney general filed a lawsuit against the Environmental Protection Agency (EPA), citing concerns that the new rules are “arbitrary, capricious, an abuse of discretion and not in accordance with law."</p><p><a href="http://bakkenshale.com/bsp-news/news/north-dakota-sues-the-epa">Read more:&nbsp;</a><a href="http://bakkenshale.com/bsp-news/news/north-dakota-sues-the-epa">North Dakota Sues EPA</a></p><p><em>A total of <strong>459</strong>&nbsp;oil and gas rigs were running across the United States this week, up 13 over last week.&nbsp;<strong>88</strong>&nbsp;were targeting natural gas (one less than the previous week) and <strong>371</strong>&nbsp;were targeting oil in the U.S. (14 more than the previous week). The remainder were drilling service wells (e.g. disposal wells, injection wells, etc.)</em><span>&nbsp;</span><em><strong>27</strong>&nbsp;of the rigs active in the U.S. were running in North Dakota.</em></p><h2>Bakken Oil &amp; Gas Rigs</h2><p>Bakken oil rigs were at 27 this week with Bloomberg reporting WTI oil prices dipping to $<span>44.19</span>&nbsp;on Friday afternoon. WTI-Brent also ended the week lower with Bloomberg reporting $45.69.&nbsp;The Bakken has zero natural active gas rig in the area this week with futures trading at $2.78/mmbtu by midday.</p><p>McKenzie County leads development with 12 rigs running this week, far outpacing other counties. View the full list below under the <em>Bakken Drilling by County</em>&nbsp;section.</p><p>Activity continues to be dominated by horizontal drilling:</p><ul><li>27 rigs are drilling horizontal wells</li><li>0 rigs are drilling directional wells</li><li>0 rigs are drilling vertical wells</li></ul><h2>Bakken Drilling by County</h2>
