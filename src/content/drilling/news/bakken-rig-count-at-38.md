  <p>The <a href="/drilling">Bakken-Three Forks rig count</a>&nbsp;gained some momentum this week, with Baker Hughes reporting 38 rigs running across our coverage area by midday Friday.</p><p>In recent Bakken news, protests at the construction site of the Dakota Access Pipeline are coming to a close after almost a year of tension and confrontation.</p><p>Read more:&nbsp;<a href="/bsp-news/dakota-access-protest-camps-evacuated">Dakota Access Protest Camps Evacuated</a></p><p><em>A total of <strong>756</strong>&nbsp;oil and gas rigs were running across the United States this week, an increase of one over than last week.&nbsp;<strong>146</strong>&nbsp;rigs targeted natural gas (five less than the previous week) and <strong>609</strong>&nbsp;were targeting oil in the U.S. (seven more than the previous week). The remainder were drilling service wells (e.g. disposal wells, injection wells, etc.)</em>&nbsp;<em><strong>37</strong>&nbsp;of the rigs active in the U.S. were running in North Dakota.</em></p><h2>Bakken Oil &amp; Gas Rigs</h2><p>Bakken oil rigs were at 38 this week with Bloomberg reporting WTI oil prices decreasing to $53.33 on Friday afternoon.&nbsp;The Bakken has zero natural active gas rig in the area this week with futures trading at $2.83/mmbtu by midday.</p><p>McKenzie County leads production in the region with 18 rigs running this week. View the full list below under the <em>Bakken Drilling by County</em>&nbsp;section.</p><p>Activity continues to be dominated by horizontal drilling:</p><ul><li>38 rigs are drilling horizontal wells</li><li>0 rigs are drilling directional wells</li><li>0 rigs are drilling vertical wells</li></ul><h2>Rig Count by County</h2>
