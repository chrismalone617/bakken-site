<div data-sqsp-text-block-content="">
  <p>The <a href="/drilling">Bakken-Three Forks rig count</a>&nbsp;didn't move this week, with Baker Hughes reporting 46 rigs running through our coverage area by Friday midday.</p><p><em>A total of <strong>939</strong>&nbsp;oil and gas rigs were running across the United States this week.&nbsp;<strong>187</strong>&nbsp;rigs were targeting natural gas (+5) and 752 were targeting oil (+10) in the U.S.. The remainder were drilling service wells (e.g. disposal wells, injection wells, etc.)</em>&nbsp; <em>45<strong>&nbsp;</strong>of the rigs active in the U.S. were running in North Dakota.</em></p><h2>Bakken Oil &amp; Gas Rigs</h2><p>46 rigs targeted oil in the Bakken this week with Bloomberg reporting WTI oil prices at $64.30 on Friday afternoon.&nbsp;The Bakken has zero natural active gas rig in the area this week with commodity futures trading at $3.20/mmbtu by midday.</p><p><a href="http://www.mineralrightsforum.com/group/bakken-shale-nd-mt">Own mineral rights in the Bakken? Join the discussion here.&nbsp;</a></p><p>McKenzie County leads activity in the region with 19 rigs running this week.
