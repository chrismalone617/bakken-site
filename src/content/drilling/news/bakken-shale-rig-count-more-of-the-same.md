<div data-sqsp-text-block-content="">
  <p>The <a href="/drilling">Bakken-Three Forks rig count</a>&nbsp;fell by one this week, with Baker Hughes reporting 49 rigs running through our coverage area by Friday midday.</p><p>This week's activity represents more of the same, as the rig count for the region has hovered in the high 40's for months.&nbsp;</p><p>The rig count rose significantly in early 2017, but operators have struggled to bring more rigs back online after hitting a high of 54 in August.&nbsp;</p><p><a href="http://www.mineralrightsforum.com/group/bakken-shale-nd-mt">Own mineral rights in the Bakken? Join the discussion here.&nbsp;</a></p><p><em>A total of <strong>975</strong>&nbsp;oil and gas rigs were running across the United States this week.&nbsp;<strong>177</strong>&nbsp;rigs were targeting natural gas (-7) and <strong>798</strong>&nbsp;were targeting oil (+7) in the U.S.&nbsp;The remainder were drilling service wells (e.g. disposal wells, injection wells, etc.)</em>&nbsp;<em><strong>48</strong>&nbsp;of the rigs active in the U.S. were running in N.D.</em></p><h2>Bakken Oil &amp; Gas Rigs</h2><p>49 rigs targeted oil in the Bakken this week with Bloomberg reporting WTI at $61.68 on Friday afternoon.&nbsp;The Bakken has zero natural active gas rig in the area this week with commodity futures trading at $2.56/mmbtu by midday.</p><p>McKenzie County leads activity in the region with 23 rigs running this week. View the full list below under the <em>Bakken Drilling by County</em>&nbsp;section.</p><p>Activity continues to be dominated by horizontal drilling:</p><ul><li>49 rigs are drilling horizontal wells</li><li>0 rigs are drilling directional wells</li><li>0 rigs are drilling vertical wells</li></ul><h2>Rig Count by County</h2>

  <h2>What is the Rig Count?</h2><p>The Bakken Shale Rig Count is an index of the total number of oil &amp; gas drilling rigs running across Montana and North Dakota. The rigs referred to in this article are for ALL drilling reported by Baker Hughes and not solely wells targeting the Bakken formation. All land rigs and onshore rig data shown here are based upon industry estimates provided by the Baker Hughes Rig Count.</p>
