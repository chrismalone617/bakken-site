  <p>The <a href="/drilling">Bakken-Three Forks rig count</a>&nbsp;gained by one this week with Baker Hughes reporting 28 rigs running across our coverage area by midday Friday.</p><p><em>A total of <strong>510</strong>&nbsp;oil and gas rigs were running across the United States this week, a decrease of 1 from last week of two.&nbsp;<strong>92</strong>&nbsp;rigs targeted natural gas (three more than the previous week) and <strong>418</strong>&nbsp;were targeting oil in the U.S. (two more than the previous week). The remainder were drilling service wells (e.g. disposal wells, injection wells, etc.)</em><span>&nbsp;</span><em>28 of the rigs active in the U.S. were running in North Dakota.</em></p><h2>Bakken Oil &amp; Gas Rigs</h2><p>Bakken oil rigs were at 28 this week with Bloomberg reporting WTI oil prices gaining $1.45 to $<span>44.48</span> on Friday afternoon. WTI-Brent was also up with Bloomberg reporting $<span>45.89</span>.&nbsp;The Bakken has zero natural active gas rig in the area this week with futures trading at $2.96/mmbtu by midday.</p><p>McKenzie County leads development with 10 rigs running this week. View the full list below under the<em>Bakken Drilling by County</em>&nbsp;section.</p><p>Activity continues to be dominated by horizontal drilling:</p><ul><li>27 rigs are drilling horizontal wells</li><li>1 rigs are drilling directional wells</li><li>0 rigs are drilling vertical wells</li></ul><h2>Bakken Drilling by County</h2>
