The remainder were drilling service wells (e.g. disposal wells, injection wells, etc.)</em><span>&nbsp;</span><em><strong>28</strong>&nbsp;of the rigs active in the U.S. were running in North Dakota.</em></p><h2>Bakken Oil &amp; Gas Rigs</h2><p>Bakken oil rigs were at 28 this week with Bloomberg reporting WTI oil prices declining to $<span>45.24</span> on Friday afternoon. WTI-Brent fell back below $40 a barrel ending the week at $46.63.&nbsp;The Bakken has zero natural active gas rig in the area this week with futures trading at $2.80/mmbtu by midday.</p><p>McKenzie County leads development with 12 rigs running this week, far outpacing other counties.
