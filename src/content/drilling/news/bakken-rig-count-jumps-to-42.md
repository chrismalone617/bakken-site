<div data-sqsp-text-block-content="">
  <p>The <a href="http://bakkenshale.com/drilling">Bakken-Three Forks rig count</a>&nbsp;jumped by 4 this week, with Baker Hughes reporting 42 rigs running across our coverage area by midday Friday.</p><p>In recent Bakken news, Northern executives announced a 2017 capital budget of $102.2 million. The company will drill 12 new wells in the Bakken Shale during 2017 and expects total annual production to equal or exceed 2016.</p><p>Read more:&nbsp;<a href="https://bakkenshale.com/bsp-news/news/northern-oil-gas-will-add-bakken-wells">Northern Oil &amp; Gas Will Add Bakken Wells</a></p><p><em>A total of <strong>789</strong>&nbsp;oil and gas rigs were running across the United States this week, an increase of 21 over than last week.&nbsp;<strong>157</strong>&nbsp;rigs targeted natural gas (six more than the previous week) and <strong>631</strong>&nbsp;were targeting oil in the U.S. (14 more than the previous week). The remainder were drilling service wells (e.g. disposal wells, injection wells, etc.)</em>&nbsp;&nbsp;<em><strong>42</strong>&nbsp;of the rigs active in the U.S. were running in North Dakota.</em></p><h2>Bakken Oil &amp; Gas Rigs</h2><p>Bakken oil rigs were at 42 this week with Bloomberg reporting WTI oil prices rising to $48.78 on Friday afternoon.&nbsp;The Bakken has zero natural active gas rig in the area this week with futures trading at $2.95/mmbtu by midday.</p><p>McKenzie County leads production in the region with 17 rigs running this week. View the full list below under the <em>Bakken Drilling by County</em>&nbsp;section.</p><p>Activity continues to be dominated by horizontal drilling:</p><ul><li>41 rigs are drilling horizontal wells</li><li>0 rigs are drilling directional wells</li><li>1 rigs are drilling vertical wells</li></ul><h2>Rig Count by County</h2>
