  <p>The <a href="/drilling">Bakken-Three Forks rig count</a>&nbsp;gained three this week, with Baker Hughes reporting 35 rigs running across our coverage area by midday Friday.</p><p>In recent Bakken news,&nbsp;Hess Corporation has announced it will increase E&amp;P spending for 2017, including adding more Bakken rigs.&nbsp;</p><p><a href="/bsp-news/news/hess-increases-2017-ep-budget-to-225-billion">Read more: Hess Corp to Add Bakken Rigs</a></p><p><em>A total of <strong>693</strong>&nbsp;oil and gas rigs were running across the United States this week, 35 over last week.&nbsp;<strong>142</strong>&nbsp;rigs targeted natural gas (six more than the previous week) and <strong>551</strong>&nbsp;were targeting oil in the U.S. (29 more than the previous week). The remainder were drilling service wells (e.g. disposal wells, injection wells, etc.)</em><span>&nbsp;</span>&nbsp;<em><strong>35</strong>&nbsp;of the rigs active in the U.S. were running in North Dakota.</em></p><h2>Bakken Oil &amp; Gas Rigs</h2><p>Bakken oil rigs were at 35 this week with Bloomberg reporting WTI oil prices increasing slightly to $<span>53.22</span>&nbsp;on Friday afternoon. Prices for WTI-Brent dipped per barrel to $55.49.&nbsp;The Bakken has zero natural active gas rig in the area this week with futures trading at $3.20/mmbtu by midday.</p><p>McKenzie County leads production in the region with 15 rigs running this week. View the full list below under the <em>Bakken Drilling by County</em>&nbsp;section.</p><p>Activity continues to be dominated by horizontal drilling:</p><ul><li>35 rigs are drilling horizontal wells</li><li>0 rigs are drilling directional wells</li><li>0 rigs are drilling vertical wells</li></ul><h2>Rig Count by County</h2>
