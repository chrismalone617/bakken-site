  <p>The <a href="http://bakkenshale.com/drilling">Bakken-Three Forks rig count</a>&nbsp;increased by one this week with Baker Hughes reporting 29 rigs running across our coverage area by midday Friday.</p><p>In recent Bakken news,&nbsp;Pioneer Resources report strong second quarter results including exceptional performance from their Bakken assets.</p><p><a href="http://bakkenshale.com/bsp-news/news/continental-waits-60-dollar-crude">Related: PioneerWaiting for $60 Crude</a></p><p><em>A total of <strong>479</strong>&nbsp;oil and gas rigs were running across the United States this week, and increase from last week of17.&nbsp;<strong>83</strong>&nbsp;were targeting natural gas (two more than the previous week) and <strong>396</strong>&nbsp;were targeting oil in the U.S. (15 more than the previous week). The remainder were drilling service wells (e.g. disposal wells, injection wells, etc.)</em><span>&nbsp;</span><em><strong>29</strong>&nbsp;of the rigs active in the U.S. were running in North Dakota.</em></p><h2>Bakken Oil &amp; Gas Rigs</h2><p>Bakken oil rigs were at 29 this week with Bloomberg reporting WTI oil prices at $<span>44.49</span>&nbsp;on Friday afternoon.
