  <p>The <a href="http://bakkenshale.com/drilling">Bakken-Three Forks rig count</a>&nbsp;continued its climb this week, with Baker Hughes reporting 37 rigs running across our coverage area by midday Friday.</p><p><em>A total of <strong>701</strong>&nbsp;oil and gas rigs were running across the United States this week, an increase of eight over last week.&nbsp;<strong>145</strong>&nbsp;rigs targeted natural gas (three more than the previous week) and <strong>555</strong>&nbsp;were targeting oil in the U.S. (five more than the previous week). The remainder were drilling service wells (e.g. disposal wells, injection wells, etc.)</em><span>&nbsp;</span><em><strong>36</strong>&nbsp;of the rigs active in the U.S. were running in North Dakota.</em></p><h2>Bakken Oil &amp; Gas Rigs</h2><p>Bakken oil rigs were at 37 this week with Bloomberg reporting WTI oil prices dipping to $53.17 on Friday afternoon. Prices for WTI-Brent increased to per barrel of $55.52.&nbsp;The Bakken has zero natural active gas rig in the area this week with futures trading at $3.39/mmbtu by midday.</p><p>McKenzie County leads production in the region with 16 rigs running this week. View the full list below under the <em>Bakken Drilling by County</em>&nbsp;section.</p><p>Activity continues to be dominated by horizontal drilling:</p><ul><li>37 rigs are drilling horizontal wells</li><li>0 rigs are drilling directional wells</li><li>0 rigs are drilling vertical wells</li></ul><h2>Rig Count by County</h2>
