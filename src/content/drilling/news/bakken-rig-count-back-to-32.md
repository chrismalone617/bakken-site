  <p>The <a href="http://bakkenshale.com/drilling">Bakken-Three Forks rig count</a>&nbsp;falls back this week, with Baker Hughes reporting 32 rigs running across our coverage area by midday Friday.</p><p>In recent Bakken news,&nbsp;North Dakota lawmakers face a massive loss in revenue as they settle into week two of the 2017 legislative session.</p><p><a href="https://bakkenshale.com/bsp-news/news/north-dakota-2-billion-revenue-loss">Read more: North Dakota Faces Revenue Loss</a></p><p><em>A total of <strong>658</strong>&nbsp;oil and gas rigs were running across the United States this week, a loss of six over last week.&nbsp;<strong>136</strong>&nbsp;rigs targeted natural gas (one more than the previous week) and <strong>522</strong>&nbsp;were targeting oil in the U.S. (seven less than the previous week). The remainder were drilling service wells (e.g. disposal wells, injection wells, etc.)</em><span>&nbsp;</span>&nbsp;<em><strong>32</strong>&nbsp;of the rigs active in the U.S. were running in North Dakota.</em></p><h2>Bakken Oil &amp; Gas Rigs</h2><p>Bakken oil rigs were at 32 this week with Bloomberg reporting WTI oil prices dipping slightly to $<span>52.55</span> on Friday afternoon. Prices for WTI-Brent also fell per barrel to $55.67.&nbsp;The Bakken has zero natural active gas rig in the area this week with futures trading at $3.46/mmbtu by midday.</p><p>McKenzie County leads production in the region with 14 rigs running this week. View the full list below under the <em>Bakken Drilling by County</em>&nbsp;section.</p><p>Activity continues to be dominated by horizontal drilling:</p><ul><li>32 rigs are drilling horizontal wells</li><li>0 rigs are drilling directional wells</li><li>0 rigs are drilling vertical wells</li></ul><h2>Rig Count by County</h2>
