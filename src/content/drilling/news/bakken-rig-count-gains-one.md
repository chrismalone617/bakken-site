  <p>The <a href="http://bakkenshale.com/drilling">Bakken-Three Forks rig count</a>&nbsp;gained one this week, with Baker Hughes reporting 33 rigs running across our coverage area by midday Friday.</p><p>In recent Bakken news,&nbsp;E&amp;P companies are moving forward with DUC reduction programs, according to the North Dakota Industrial Commission.&nbsp;</p><p><a href="https://bakkenshale.com/bsp-news/news/bakken-reducing-ducs">Read more at : Bakken Operators Reduce DUCs</a></p><p><em>A total of <strong>657</strong>&nbsp;oil and gas rigs were running across the United States this week, a gain of five over last week.&nbsp;<strong>132</strong>&nbsp;rigs targeted natural gas (three more than the previous week) and <strong>525</strong>&nbsp;were targeting oil in the U.S. (two more than the previous week). The remainder were drilling service wells (e.g. disposal wells, injection wells, etc.)</em>&nbsp;<em><strong>33</strong>&nbsp;of the rigs active in the U.S. were running in North Dakota.</em></p><h2>Bakken Oil &amp; Gas Rigs</h2><p>Bakken oil rigs were at 33 this week with Bloomberg reporting WTI oil prices gained slightly to $53.72 on Friday afternoon. Prices for WTI-Brent increased per barrel to $55.82.&nbsp;The Bakken has zero natural active gas rig in the area this week with futures trading at $3.72/mmbtu by midday.</p><p>McKenzie County leads production in the region with 15 rigs running this week. View the full list below under the <em>Bakken Drilling by County</em>&nbsp;section.</p><p>Activity continues to be dominated by horizontal drilling:</p><ul><li>33 rigs are drilling horizontal wells</li><li>0 rigs are drilling directional wells</li><li>0 rigs are drilling vertical wells</li></ul><h2>Rig Count by County</h2>
