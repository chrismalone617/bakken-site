<div data-sqsp-text-block-content="">
  <p><a href="http://bakkenshale.com/drilling">Bakken-Three Forks rig count</a>&nbsp;was unmoved last week, with Baker Hughes reporting 53 rigs running through our coverage area by Friday midday.</p><p>Rig counts across the United States are also down.&nbsp;</p><p>In recent Bakken Shale news, Continental Resources reports its Bakken team doubled their rate of return during the second quarter while achieving impressive efficiency gains.</p><p><a href="https://bakkenshale.com/bsp-news/continental-touts-bakken-innovation">Continental Touts Bakken Innovation</a></p><p><em>A total of <strong>949</strong>&nbsp;oil and gas rigs were running across the United States this week, eight more than last week.&nbsp;<strong>181</strong>&nbsp;rigs targeted natural gas (eight less than the previous week) and <strong>768</strong>&nbsp;were targeting oil in the U.S. (three more than the previous week). The remainder were drilling service wells (e.g. disposal wells, injection wells, etc.)</em>&nbsp; <em>&nbsp;<strong>53 </strong>of the rigs active in the U.S. were running in North Dakota.</em></p><h2>Bakken Oil &amp; Gas Rigs</h2><p>Bakken oil rigs were at 53 this week with Bloomberg reporting WTI oil prices at $48.82 on Friday afternoon.&nbsp;The Bakken has zero natural active gas rig in the area this week with futures trading at $2.98/mmbtu by midday.</p><p>McKenzie County leads activity in the region with 21 rigs running this week. View the full list below under the <em>Bakken Drilling by County</em>&nbsp;section.</p><p>Activity continues to be dominated by horizontal drilling:</p><ul><li>52 rigs are drilling horizontal wells</li><li>1 rigs are drilling directional wells</li><li>0 rigs are drilling vertical wells</li></ul><h2>Rig Count by County</h2>
