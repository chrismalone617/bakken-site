<div data-sqsp-text-block-content="">
  <p>For the fourth week in a row, the <a href="http://bakkenshale.com/drilling">Bakken-Three Forks rig count</a>&nbsp;remains stalled as Baker Hughes reporting 30 rigs running across our coverage area by midday Friday.</p><p>In recent Bakken news, oil production for the region dipped below 1 million barrels a day for the first time since 2014.&nbsp;</p><p><a href="http://bakkenshale.com/bsp-news/news/p9k43al0d3bp5kf9l7lmmdtq6jw1j6">Read more: Bakken Dips Below 1 Million Barrels </a></p><p><em>A total of <strong>551</strong>&nbsp;oil and gas rigs were running across the United States this week, a gain of 14 &nbsp;from last week.&nbsp;<strong>108</strong>&nbsp;rigs targeted natural gas (three more than the previous week) and <strong>443</strong>&nbsp;were targeting oil in the U.S. (11 more than the previous week). The remainder were drilling service wells (e.g. disposal wells, injection wells, etc.)</em><span>&nbsp;</span><em><strong>30</strong>&nbsp;of the rigs active in the U.S. were running in North Dakota.</em></p><h2>Bakken Oil &amp; Gas Rigs</h2><p>Bakken oil rigs were at 30 this week with Bloomberg reporting WTI oil prices gaining $.50 to $50.85 on Friday afternoon. WTI-Brent dipped with Bloomberg reporting $51.78 The Bakken has zero natural active gas rig in the area this week with futures trading at $2.96/mmbtu by midday.</p><p>Dunn County &nbsp;continues to lead production in the region with 11 rigs running this week. View the full list below under the <em>Bakken Drilling by County</em>&nbsp;section.</p><p>Activity continues to be dominated by horizontal drilling:</p><ul><li>29 rigs are drilling horizontal wells</li><li>1 rigs are drilling directional wells</li><li>0 rigs are drilling vertical wells</li></ul><h2>Bakken Drilling by County</h2>
