<div data-sqsp-text-block-content="">
  <p>The <a href="http://bakkenshale.com/drilling">Bakken-Three Forks rig count</a>&nbsp;didn't budge this week, with Baker Hughes reporting 42 rigs running across our coverage area by midday Friday.</p><p>In recent Bakken Shale news, Whiting Petroleum plans to spend $580 million is for activity in the Williston Basin, including running five rigs in the region.</p><p>Read more:&nbsp;<a href="https://bakkenshale.com/bsp-news/news/whiting-petroleum-allocates-580-million-for-bakken">Whiting Petroleum Allocates $580 Million for Bakken</a></p><p><em>A total of <strong>839</strong>&nbsp;oil and gas rigs were running across the United States this week, an increase of 15 over than last week.&nbsp;<strong>165</strong>&nbsp;rigs targeted natural gas (five more than the previous week) and <strong>672</strong>&nbsp;were targeting oil in the U.S. (10 more than the previous week). The remainder were drilling service wells (e.g. disposal wells, injection wells, etc.)</em>&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;<em><strong>42</strong>&nbsp;of the rigs active in the U.S. were running in North Dakota.</em></p><h2>Bakken Oil &amp; Gas Rigs</h2><p>Bakken oil rigs were at 42 this week with Bloomberg reporting WTI oil prices rising to $52.24 on Friday afternoon.&nbsp;The Bakken has zero natural active gas rig in the area this week with futures trading at $3.26/mmbtu by midday.</p><p>McKenzie County leads production in the region with 20 rigs running this week. View the full list below under the <em>Bakken Drilling by County</em>&nbsp;section.</p><p>Activity continues to be dominated by horizontal drilling:</p><ul><li>41 rigs are drilling horizontal wells</li><li>0 rigs are drilling directional wells</li><li>1 rigs are drilling vertical wells</li></ul><h2>Rig Count by County</h2>
