  <p>The <a href="http://bakkenshale.com/drilling">Bakken-Three Forks rig count</a>&nbsp;remains the same for the third week in a row this week, with Baker Hughes reporting 52 rigs running through our coverage area by Friday midday.&nbsp;&nbsp;</p><p>In recent shale news, the University of Chicago’s Energy Policy Institute is reporting the economic benefits of fracking on local economies outweigh undesirable side effects.</p><p>Read more:&nbsp;<a href="https://bakkenshale.com/bsp-news/news/fracking-boosts-local-economies">Fracking Benefits Local Economies</a></p><p><em>A total of <strong>952</strong>&nbsp;oil and gas rigs were running across the United States this week, a decrease of one over last week.&nbsp;<strong>189</strong>&nbsp;rigs targeted natural gas (five more than the previous week) and<strong>763</strong>&nbsp;were targeting oil in the U.S. (seven more than the previous week). The remainder were drilling service wells (e.g. disposal wells, injection wells, etc.)&nbsp;<strong>52 </strong>of the rigs active in the U.S. were running in North Dakota.</em></p><h2>Bakken Oil &amp; Gas Rigs</h2><p>Bakken oil rigs were at 52 this week with Bloomberg reporting WTI oil prices at $44.23 on Friday afternoon.&nbsp;The Bakken has zero natural active gas rig in the area this week with futures trading at $2.86/mmbtu by midday.</p><p>McKenzie County leads production in the region with 21 rigs running this week. View the full list below under the <em>Bakken Drilling by County</em>&nbsp;section.</p><p>Activity continues to be dominated by horizontal drilling:</p><ul><li>52 rigs are drilling horizontal wells</li><li>0 rigs are drilling directional wells</li><li>0 rigs are drilling vertical wells</li></ul><h2>Rig Count by County</h2>
