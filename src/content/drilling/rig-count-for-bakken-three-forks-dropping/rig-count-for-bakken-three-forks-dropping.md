<div data-sqsp-text-block-content="">
  <p>The <a href="http://bakkenshale.com/drilling">Bakken-Three Forks rig count</a>&nbsp;fell back this week, with Baker Hughes reporting 52 rigs running through our coverage area by Friday midday.</p><p>In recent Bakken Shale news,&nbsp;RimRock Oil and Gas gains a foothold in the Bakken with purchase of Williston assets from Whiting.</p><p><a href="https://bakkenshale.com/bsp-news/news/whiting-sells-bakken-assets-500-million">Whiting Sells Bakken Assets for $500 Million</a></p><p><em>A total of <strong>944</strong>&nbsp;oil and gas rigs were running across the United States this week, four more over last two weeks.&nbsp;<strong>187</strong>&nbsp;rigs targeted natural gas ( an increase of seven) and <strong>756</strong>&nbsp;were targeting oil in the U.S. (decreased by three). The remainder were drilling service wells (e.g. disposal wells, injection wells, etc.)</em><em>&nbsp;<strong>52 </strong>of the rigs active in the U.S. were running in North Dakota.</em></p><h2>Bakken Oil &amp; Gas Rigs</h2><p>52 rigs targeted oil in the Bakken this week with Bloomberg reporting WTI oil prices at $49.61 on Friday afternoon.&nbsp;The Bakken has zero natural active gas rig in the area this week with futures trading at $3.08/mmbtu by midday.</p><p>McKenzie County leads activity in the region with 22 rigs running this week. View the full list below under the <em>Bakken Drilling by County</em>&nbsp;section.</p><p>Activity continues to be dominated by horizontal drilling:</p><ul><li>51 rigs are drilling horizontal wells</li><li>1 rigs are drilling directional wells</li><li>0 rigs are drilling vertical wells</li></ul><h2>Rig Count by County</h2>
