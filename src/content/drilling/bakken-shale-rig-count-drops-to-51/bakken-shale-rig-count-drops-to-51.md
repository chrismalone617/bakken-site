<div data-sqsp-text-block-content="">
  <p>The <a href="http://bakkenshale.com/drilling">Bakken-Three Forks rig count</a>&nbsp;dropped by two, with Baker Hughes reporting 51 rigs running through our coverage area by Friday midday.</p><p><em>A total of <strong>946</strong>&nbsp;oil and gas rigs were running across the United States this week, three less than last week.&nbsp;<strong>182</strong>&nbsp;rigs targeted natural gas (one more than the previous week) and <strong>763</strong>&nbsp;were targeting oil in the U.S. (five less than the previous week). The remainder were drilling service wells (e.g. disposal wells, injection wells, etc.)</em>&nbsp;&nbsp;<em>&nbsp;<strong>51 </strong>of the rigs active in the U.S. were running in North Dakota.</em></p><h2>Bakken Oil &amp; Gas Rigs</h2><p>51 rigs targetted oil in the Bakke this week with Bloomberg reporting WTI oil prices at $48.82 on Friday afternoon.&nbsp;The Bakken has zero natural active gas rig in the area this week with futures trading at $2.89/mmbtu by midday.</p><p>McKenzie County leads activity in the region with 22 rigs running this week. View the full list below under the <em>Bakken Drilling by County</em>&nbsp;section.</p><p>Activity continues to be dominated by horizontal drilling:</p><ul><li>49 rigs are drilling horizontal wells</li><li>2 rigs are drilling directional wells</li><li>0 rigs are drilling vertical wells</li></ul><h2>Rig Count by County</h2>

The rigs referred to in this article are for ALL drilling reported by Baker Hughes and not solely wells targeting the Bakken formation.
