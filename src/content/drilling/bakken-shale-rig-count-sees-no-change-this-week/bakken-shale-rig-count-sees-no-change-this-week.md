  <p>&nbsp;<a href="/drilling">Bakken-Three Forks rig count</a>&nbsp;is unmoved this week up, with Baker Hughes reporting 54 rigs running through our coverage area by Friday midday.&nbsp;&nbsp;</p><p>In recent Bakke Shale news,&nbsp;North Dakota is in a hot dispute with oil and gas companies and industry leaders over improper deductions on royalties owed to the state.</p><p><a href="/bsp-news/news/north-dakota-owed-millions-in-royalties">North Dakota Owed Millions in Royalties</a></p><p><em>A total of <strong>958</strong>&nbsp;oil and gas rigs were running across the United States this week, eight more than last week.&nbsp;<strong>192</strong>&nbsp;rigs targeted natural gas (six more than the previous week) and <strong>766</strong>&nbsp;were targeting oil in the U.S. (two more than the previous week). The remainder were drilling service wells (e.g. disposal wells, injection wells, etc.)&nbsp;<strong>54 </strong>of the rigs active in the U.S. were running in North Dakota.</em></p><h2>Bakken Oil &amp; Gas Rigs</h2><p>Bakken oil rigs were at 54 this week with Bloomberg reporting WTI oil prices at $49.71 on Friday afternoon.&nbsp;The Bakken has zero natural active gas rig in the area this week with futures trading at $2.94/mmbtu by midday.</p><p>McKenzie County leads production in the region with 19 rigs running this week. View the full list below under the <em>Bakken Drilling by County</em>&nbsp;section.</p><p>Activity continues to be dominated by horizontal drilling:</p><ul><li>53 rigs are drilling horizontal wells</li><li>1 rigs are drilling directional wells</li><li>0 rigs are drilling vertical wells</li></ul><h2>Rig Count by County</h2>
