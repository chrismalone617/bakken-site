  <p>The <a href="/drilling">Bakken-Three Forks rig count</a>&nbsp;lost ground this week, falling by two to 49 rigs running through our coverage area by Friday midday.</p><p><em>A total of <strong>909</strong>&nbsp;oil and gas rigs were running across the United States this week, four less than last week.&nbsp;<strong>172</strong>&nbsp;rigs were targeting natural gas (- 5) and <strong>737</strong>&nbsp;were targeting oil in the U.S. (+1). The remainder were drilling service wells (e.g. disposal wells, injection wells, etc.)</em><em>&nbsp;49<strong>&nbsp;</strong>of the rigs active in the U.S. were running in North Dakota.</em></p><h2>Bakken Oil &amp; Gas Rigs</h2><p>49 rigs targeted oil in the Bakken this week with Bloomberg reporting WTI oil prices at $53.90 on Friday afternoon.&nbsp;The Bakken has zero natural active gas rig in the area this week with commodity futures trading at $2.96/mmbtu by midday.</p><p><a href="http://www.mineralrightsforum.com/group/bakken-shale-nd-mt">Own mineral rights in the Bakken? Join the discussion here.&nbsp;</a></p><p>McKenzie County leads activity in the region with 21 rigs running this week. View the full list below under the <em>Bakken Drilling by County</em>&nbsp;section.</p><p>Activity continues to be dominated by horizontal drilling:</p><ul><li>48 rigs are drilling horizontal wells</li><li>1 rigs are drilling directional wells</li><li>0 rigs are drilling vertical wells</li></ul><h2>Rig Count by County</h2>
