<div data-sqsp-text-block-content="">
  <p>The <a href="http://bakkenshale.com/drilling/">Bakken-Three Forks rig count</a>&nbsp;remained flat this week as Baker Hughes announces there are 22 rigs running across our coverage area by midday Friday.</p><p>In recent Bakken news, North Dakota officials remain optimistic about the state's health. Even though the state has seen a revenue shortfall, Gov Dalrymple says their economy remains strong.&nbsp;Even though the state has seen a revenue shortfall, Governor Jack Dalrymple <a href="http://bakkenshale.com/bsp-news/news/w0kio7kj3e4ksmzzak9iwi66pzae2c">Read more here.</a></p><p><em>A total of <strong>407</strong>&nbsp;oil and gas rigs were running across the United States this week, an increase of four over last week.&nbsp;<strong>82</strong>&nbsp;were targeting natural gas (five less than the previous week) and <strong>325</strong>&nbsp;were targeting oil in the U.S. (nine more than the previous week). The remainder were drilling service wells (e.g. disposal wells, injection wells, etc.)&nbsp;22 </em><em>of the rigs active in the U.S. were running in North Dakota.</em></p><h2>Bakken Oil &amp; Gas Rigs</h2><p>Bakken oil rigs fell to 22 this week with Bloomberg reporting WTI oil prices slipping &nbsp;to $<span>48.82</span>&nbsp;on Friday afternoon. WTI-Brent also gained some, increasing this week to $49.34.&nbsp;The Bakken has zero natural active gas rig in the area this week with futures trading to $2.41/mmbtu by midday.</p><p>McKenzie County continues to lead development with 11 rigs running this week, far outpacing other counties. View the full list below under the <em>Bakken Drilling by County</em>&nbsp;section.</p><p>Activity continues to be dominated by horizontal drilling:</p><ul><li>22 rigs are drilling horizontal wells</li><li>0 rigs are drilling directional wells</li><li>0 rigs are drilling vertical wells</li></ul><p><a href="http://static1.squarespace.com/static/56ccbdb3a3360c75e6843e94/573f41e26dc6951d90c43bbf/573f42026dc6951d90c43df3/1463763458812/?format=original">Be sure to visit our Bakken Job Listings to search openings and come back weekly for updates or sign up for alerts - Daily or Weekly Email Alerts</a></p><h2>Bakken Drilling by County</h2>
