  <p>The <a href="/drilling">Bakken-Three Forks rig count</a>&nbsp;was unchanged this week, with Baker Hughes reporting 45 rigs running through our coverage area by Friday midday.</p><p>In recent industry news, oil and gas prices are continuing to rise and crude oil production set another high this week.</p><ul><li>Crude Oil: $66.24 (+$2.87)</li><li>Natural Gas: $3.57 (+$.38)</li><li>Crude Production: 9.878 million bpd (up from 9.750 million bpd)</li></ul><p><a href="http://www.mineralrightsforum.com/group/bakken-shale-nd-mt">Own mineral rights in the Bakken?
