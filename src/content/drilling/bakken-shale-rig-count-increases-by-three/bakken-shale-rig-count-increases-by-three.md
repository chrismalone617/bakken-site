  <p>The <a href="/drilling">Bakken-Three Forks rig count</a>&nbsp;jumped this week by three, with Baker Hughes reporting 49 rigs running through our coverage area by Friday midday.&nbsp;&nbsp;</p><p>In recent Bakken News, the U.S. Census Bureau is reporting that every county in the Bakken Shale region experienced a decrease in population between 2015 and 2016.</p><p>Read more:&nbsp;<a href="/bsp-news/news/bakken-counties-losing-people">Bakken Shale Counties Losing People</a></p><p><em>A total of <strong>933</strong>&nbsp;oil and gas rigs were running across the United States this week, an increase of six over last week.&nbsp;<strong>186</strong>&nbsp;rigs targeted natural gas (one more than the previous week) and <strong>747</strong>&nbsp;were targeting oil in the U.S. (six more than the previous week). The remainder were drilling service wells (e.g. disposal wells, injection wells, etc.)&nbsp;<strong>49</strong>&nbsp;of the rigs active in the U.S. were running in North Dakota.</em></p><h2>Bakken Oil &amp; Gas Rigs</h2><p>Bakken oil rigs were at 49 this week with Bloomberg reporting WTI oil prices dipped to $44.74 on Friday afternoon.&nbsp;The Bakken has zero natural active gas rig in the area this week with futures trading at $3.04/mmbtu by midday.</p><p>McKenzie County leads production in the region with 21 rigs running this week. View the full list below under the <em>Bakken Drilling by County</em>&nbsp;section.</p><p>Activity continues to be dominated by horizontal drilling:</p><ul><li>49 rigs are drilling horizontal wells</li><li>0 rigs are drilling directional wells</li><li>0 rigs are drilling vertical wells</li></ul><h2>Rig Count by County</h2>
