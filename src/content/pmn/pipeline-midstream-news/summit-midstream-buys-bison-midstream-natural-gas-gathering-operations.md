  <p>
Summit Midstream has agreed to buy Bison Midstream, LLC, for $250 million.</p><p>Bison Midstream owns a natural gas gathering system that includes 300 miles of low and high pressure gathering lines, as well as six compressor stations (5,950 hp) in Burke and Montrail counties.</p><p>New compression is being added and capacity should grow from 20 mmcfd to 30 mmcfd by year-end 2013. A total of $40 million is being spent to improve the system in 2013 ($29 million remaining).</p><p>The Bison system is supported by producer commitments from over 675,000 acres and fee based agreements are in place for &gt;$155 million in revenue through 2020. Additional agreements are currently being negotiated.</p><blockquote><p>Steve Newby, CEO commented, "We are very excited about the acquisition of Bison, our first drop down transaction from Summit Investments"</p></blockquote><p>The system includes 229 miles of polyethylene pipe for low-pressure gathering and 70 miles of high pressure steel pipe.</p><p>If I deciphered the information on the company's website correctly: Bison Midstream was owned by Summit Investments, who is owned by members of Summit Midstream's management, Energy Capital Partners, and GE Energy Financial Services.</p><p>The deal was announced in conjunction with a $210 million acquisition in the Marcellus Shale.</p><p>Read the full press release at <a href="http://www.summitmidstream.com/docs/smlp%20acquisition%20-%20bison%20mountaineer%20(6%205%2013)%20final%20docx.pdf" target="_blank">summitmidstream.com</a></p>
