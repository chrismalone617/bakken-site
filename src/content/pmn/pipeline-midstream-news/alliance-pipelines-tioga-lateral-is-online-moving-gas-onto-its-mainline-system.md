<div data-sqsp-text-block-content="">
  <p>Alliance Pipeline's 80-mile, 12-inch Tioga Lateral pipeline came into service in September and is moving Bakken gas onto the Alliance mainline.</p><p>The pipeline was constructed over a period of a little less than a year at a cost of $170 million.</p><p>The pipeline adds much needed high btu, wet gas pipeline capacity out of the basin. The lateral has capacity to move ~126 mmcfd from Hess' Tioga Processing Plant to the company's mainline system near the Canadian border.</p><p><strong>Read more:</strong><a href="/bsp-news/news/tioga-pipeline-gets-regulatory-approval-2013-in-service">Tioga Pipeline Gets Regulatory Approval</a></p><p>The system was originally supported by an agreement with Hess to move 61.5 mmcfd for ten years.</p><p>The Alliance system has capacity to move 1.6 Bcf/d of high btu gas out of Western Canada to Chicago.</p><p>The Tioga lateral is the second pipeline from the Bakken that ties into the system. As much as ~250 mmcfd can now move into the system through the Tioga Lateral and Pecan Rose Pipeline.</p><p>Read the full press release at <a target="_blank" href="http://www.alliancepipeline.com/Media/PressRelease/Pages/Alliance%20Pipeline%20completes%20natural%20gas%20pipeline%20project%20in%20western%20North%20Dakota.aspx">alliancepipeline.com</a></p>
