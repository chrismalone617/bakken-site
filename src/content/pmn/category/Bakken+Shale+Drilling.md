<div data-sqsp-text-block-content="">
  <p>The North Dakota rig count reached an all time record on November 19, 2011, when the ND Oil &amp; Gas Division announced there were <strong>204 active rigs</strong> working in the state.
The overwhelming majority of rigs are targeting the Bakken and Three Forks formations. With oil prices holding strong and gas prices dropping through the fall, I expect we'll potentially shoot through this record when a few gas directed rigs head our way.</p>
