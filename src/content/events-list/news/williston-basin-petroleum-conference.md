  <p>The North Dakota Petroleum Council, North Dakota Department of Mineral Resources, and the Government of Saskatchewan – Ministry of the Economy, look forward to hosting you at our 2016 Williston Basin Petroleum Conference.</p><h3>Who should attend?</h3><p>Anyone with an interest in the oil and gas business in the Williston Basin should attend. Topics will range from North American and world energy supply and demand, to highly technical Bakken talks, to managing community impacts.</p><h3>Presenters</h3><p>Bakken CEOs and industry leaders from across North America will discuss emerging Bakken technologies and development impacts. View the full list of <a href="https://www.wbpcnd.org/presenters.aspx">presenters</a>.</p>
