  <p>If you are doing business in the Rockies, DUG Rockies is the conference for you. With an expanded geographic focus for 2016, this comprehensive event offers the latest market intelligence on upstream developments and midstream activity in plays throughout the Rocky Mountains and Northern Great Plains- including the Bakken, Niobrara, Three Forks, Codell, Wasatch, Fort Union and other formations.</p><p>Formerly DUG Bakken and Niobrara, DUG Rockies is part of Hart Energy’s Developing Unconventionals (DUG) conference series, which attracts the industry's top leaders.
