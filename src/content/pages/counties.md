    <h2><strong>Bakken Shale County Coverage</strong></h2>
    <p>The Bakken covers much of Western North Dakota and parts of Montana. The play benefits from being in a more rural area of the country where fewer people are impacted by industry operations. Many leases cover large tracts of land, which can be several hundred and up to thousands of acres. This makes operations and leasing much more simple than it is in urban shale areas like the Barnett or Marcellus.</p>
