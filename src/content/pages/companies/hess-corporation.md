  <h1>Hess Bakken Shale Map</h1>

  <p>Hess is one of the largest operators in the Bakken Shale. The company was well positioned with acreage that was already held by production when the Bakken boom began expanding into North Dakota in the mid-2000s. Hess has approximately 800,000 net acres targeting the North Dakota Bakken Shale. The company cut its development program back in 2013, but operated 16 rigs throughout 2012. The company plans to continue expanding its infrastructure assets and has a goal of reaching 120,000 b/d of production.</p>

  <p>Production from the Bakken has grown from less than 10,000 b/d in 2008 to more than 50,000 b/d in 2012. All while drilling times have fallen. The company's number of days from spud to total depth (TD) has fallen from 37 days to less than 32 days since mid-2011. Drilling and completion costs have also fallen from $13 million to less than $10 million during the same time frame.</p>

  <h2>Hess Corporation Bakken Shale Quarterly Results</h2>

  <h3>April 2016</h3>
  <p>Net production from the Bakken increased to 111,000 boepd from 108,000 boepd in the prior-year quarter due to higher natural gas liquids and natural gas production in 2016. The Corporation operated four rigs in the quarter and brought 31 gross operated wells on production. Drilling and completion costs averaged $5.1 million per operated well, down 25 percent from the year-ago quarter.</p>

  <h3>January 2016</h3>
  <p>For the fourth quarter of 2015, the Hess Corporation (NYSE: HES) reported an adjusted net loss of $396 million or $1.40 per common share. Net production from the Bakken increased approximately 7 percent to 109,000 boepd from the prior-year quarter. The Corporation brought 34 gross operated wells on production in the fourth quarter of 2015. Drilling and completion costs per operated well averaged $5.1 million, down 28 percent from the year-ago quarter.</p>

  <h3>November 2015</h3>
  <p>Net production from the Bakken increased approximately 31 percent to 113,000 boepd from the prior-year quarter. The Corporation brought 48 gross operated wells on production in the third quarter of 2015. Drilling and completion costs per operated well averaged $5.3 million, down 26 percent from the year-ago quarter. During the quarter, the Corporation operated 7 rigs.</p>

  <h3>July 2015</h3>
  <p>Net production from the Bakken increased approximately 49 percent to 119,000 boepd from the prior-year quarter. The Corporation brought 67 gross operated wells on production in the second quarter of 2015. Drilling and completion costs per operated well averaged $5.6 million, down 24 percent from the year-ago quarter. During the second quarter, the Corporation operated 8 rigs.</p>

  <h3>Fourth Quarter 2014</h3>
  <p>Net production from the Bakken increased approximately 50 percent to 102,000 boepd from the prior year quarter. The Corporation brought 96 gross operated wells on production in the fourth quarter of 2014. Drilling and completion costs per operated well averaged $7.1 million, down from $7.6 million in the year-ago quarter.</p>

  <h3>November 2, 2012</h3>
  <p>Net production from the Bakken averaged 62,000 boe/d in the third quarter, an increase of 94% over the year ago. Hess shipped approximately 37,000 barrels per day of crude oil from its Tioga rail-loading facility to higher value markets to improve price realizations. The company reduced average drilling complete cost to $9.5 million in the third quarter, down from $13.4 million in the first quarter — a 29% reduction driven primarily by the switch to sliding sleeve completion designs.</p>
