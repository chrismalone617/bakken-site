  <h1>Continental Resources</h1>

  <p>Continental Resources is one of the largest operators in the Bakken Shale. The company was an early entrant in the Elm Coulee Field in Montana and began exploring in North Dakota in the early 2000s. The company currently holds or has agreements in place for 1.1 million acres prospective for the Bakken Shale. As of the latter half of 2012, more than 60% of the company's production was attributable to the Bakken.</p>

  <p>Continental Resources is one of the top ten oil producers in the U.S. and is the largest leaseholder in the Bakken Shale of North Dakota and Montana. The company was founded in 1967 by Harold Hamm and is based in Oklahoma City, OK. The company's headquarters moved from Enid to Oklahoma City in 2012. Other major assets include the Red River units in North Dakota and South Dakota, and the Anadarko Woodford in Western Oklahoma.</p>

  <h2>Continental's Quarterly Bakken Results</h2>

  <h3>May 2016</h3>
  <p>Continental's Bakken production averaged 139,602 Boe per day in first quarter 2016, a slight increase over fourth quarter 2015. The Company had no stimulation crews in the Bakken during first quarter 2016, but brought online 10 net (12 gross) wells that had been drilled and completed in 2015, but not actively produced until first quarter 2016. Continental also participated in 5 net (42 gross) non-operated Bakken wells during the quarter.</p>
  <p>Enhanced slickwater and hybrid completions continue to improve Bakken well performance. Continental now has 118 30-stage, 2-mile enhanced-completion wells in Williams and McKenzie counties with at least 180 days of production history. These wells are showing 45%-to-60% higher 180-day production rates and 35%-to-45% higher EURs when compared to direct offsets with historical standard designs.</p>

  <h3>February 2016</h3>
  <p>Continental's Bakken production averaged 136,400 Boe per day in fourth quarter 2015, a slight increase over third quarter 2015. The Company completed and initiated first sales for 22 net (105 gross) operated and non-operated Bakken wells during fourth quarter 2015. In 2015, the Bakken team doubled capital efficiency and cut finding costs in half.</p>

  <h3>November 2015</h3>
  <p>In the Northern Region during third quarter 2015, the Bakken drilling team set multiple new Continental performance records. The Company reduced average drilling time for spud-to-total-depth (TD) by 15%. The average spud-to-TD time in third quarter 2015 was 15.0 days for a two-mile lateral.</p>

  <h3>August 2015</h3>
  <p>Continental's Bakken production averaged 140,988 Boe per day in the second quarter of 2015, an increase of 4% compared with first quarter 2015. The Company completed 56 net (159 gross) operated and non-operated Middle Bakken and Three Forks wells during second quarter 2015.</p>

  <h3>May 2015</h3>
  <p>Continental's Bakken production averaged 135,538 Boe per day in the first quarter of 2015. The Company completed 66 net (199 gross) operated and non-operated Middle Bakken and Three Forks wells during first quarter 2015.</p>

  <h3>Fourth Quarter 2014</h3>
  <p>Continental's Bakken production averaged 130,783 Boe per day in the fourth quarter of 2014. For full-year 2014, Bakken production averaged 114,715 Boe per day. The Company completed 72 net (234 gross) operated and non-operated Bakken wells during fourth quarter 2014.</p>

  <h3>November 8, 2012</h3>
  <p>Continental's Bakken production in the third quarter of 2012 was 62,453 boe/d or 81 percent higher than the same time 2011. During the quarter, CLR participated in 137 gross (46 net) wells in the Bakken. Continental is the leading leaseholder in the Bakken, with 984,040 net acres at September 30, 2012.</p>
