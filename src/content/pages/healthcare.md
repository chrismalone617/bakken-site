  <h3><em>Directory of Hospitals and Urgent Care Clinics</em></h3>
  <p class="healthcare-intro">The Bakken oil &amp; gas boom is bringing a flood of industry personnel to the ND/MT area, increasing the need for healthcare services in response to illness or injury. Oilfield injuries range from major trucking accidents requiring emergency medical care to minor incidents often covered by workers' compensation. Bakken area healthcare providers include the hospitals and urgent care clinics listed below.
