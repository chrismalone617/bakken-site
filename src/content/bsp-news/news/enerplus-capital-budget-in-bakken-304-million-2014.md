  <p>Approximately 40% of Enerplus's $760 million capital budget for 2014 will be dedicated to the Bakken and Three Forks. That's about $304 million.</p><p>The company expects to grow production by more than 30% in North Dakota in 2014.</p><h2>Enerplus 2013 Capital Expenditures in Bakken</h2><p><strong>Read more</strong>: <a href="http://bakkenshale.com/bsp-news/news/enerplus-sets-new-bakken-production-record-in-q1?rq=Enerplus%20Sets%20New%20Bakken%20Production%20Record%20in%20Q1">Enerplus Sets New Bakken Production Record in Q1</a></p><p>~$308 million of capital spending was in North Dakota, with the majority invested at Fort Berthold.&nbsp;Approximately 70% of company spending in 2013 was directed to crude oil assets.</p>
</div>






















</div></div><div data-block-type="31" data-sqsp-block="quote"><div>

Our focus was on driving improvements in capital efficiencies through a reduction in drilling costs and improvement in productivity.<span>”</span>
  </blockquote>
  
</figure>

<div data-sqsp-text-block-content="">

The cost of this addition was $19.74 per boe including future development capital.</p><p>Total 2P reserves increased by more than 17% year-over-year, driven by additions in the Marcellus and Bakken/Three Forks properties.</p><h2>Enerplus Bakken Initial Production Rates and Total Production</h2>
