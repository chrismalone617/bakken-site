<div data-sqsp-text-block-content="">
  <p>Pioneer Resources report strong second quarter results including exceptional performance from their Bakken assets.</p><p><a href="http://bakkenshale.com/companies/continental-resources/">Related: Continental Resources in the Bakken</a></p><p>Pioneer released second quarter results this week that highlighted a $521 million loss along with a strong operational performance.&nbsp;</p><p>Continental's Bakken operations had an average production rate of 125,028, 10% less that Q1. Other Bakken highlights include:&nbsp;</p><ul><li>Four drilling rigs running in the play</li><li>Completed three net wells</li><li>165 gross operated DUCs</li><li>Well cost of $6.2 million, down from $6.8 million in 2015</li><li>Plans to complete eight wells in second half of 2016</li></ul>
