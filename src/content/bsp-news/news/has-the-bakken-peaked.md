<div data-sqsp-text-block-content="">
  <p>The Energy Information Administration ( EIA) is predicting that oil production in the Bakken will begin to decline in June.</p><p>Shale producers have been reducing rigs, cutting budgets and laying off workers for months to compensate for low crude prices. Over the past year, the Bakken rig count has dropped by more than half, but until now production has remained at record levels, with the EIA reported 1.2 million barrels a day in February.</p><p>Related: <a href="/bsp-news/news/record-production-bakken?rq=Record%20Production%20for%20Bakken">Record Production for Bakken</a></p><p>In the EIA’s monthly productivity report for May, the agency reports that oil and gas production has probably peaked and they expect a 31,000 Mbbl/d drop in oil and 30,000 MMcf/d f drop for natural gas throughout June.</p>
