<div data-sqsp-text-block-content="">
  <p>EOG Resources announced results from the first quarter of 2013 and impressive well results in the Bakken and Three Forks were included.</p><p>The first test of the second bench of the Three Forks yielded promising results in the company's Antelope Extension acreage in McKenzie County, ND. The Riverview 03-3130H was completed to sales at 3,150 b/d of oil. Additional tests are planned in the same area.</p><blockquote><p>In addition, modifications in completion techniques and production enhancements combined to make EOG's rate-of-return results from the Bakken the highest in company history.</p></blockquote><p>The Bakken helped fuel company-wide crude oil production growth of 33% year-over-year and recent completions are yielding projected returns as good as any play in the company's portfolio.</p><h2>EOG Tests 160-Acre Spacing At Parshall</h2><p>Initial production rates from 160-acre spacing test in the Parshall Field look promising. Highlighted wells include:</p><ul><li>The Wayzetta 136-2127H was completed at an initial production rate of 1,910 Bopd</li><li>The Fertile 53-3024H began sales at 1,725 Bopd</li><li>The Van Hook 20-0107H and 127-0107H were completed to sales at rates of 2,375 and 2,170 Bopd</li></ul><blockquote><p>If crude oil prices remain in the current range, EOG plans to increase the level of its North Dakota drilling activity in 2014.</p></blockquote>
