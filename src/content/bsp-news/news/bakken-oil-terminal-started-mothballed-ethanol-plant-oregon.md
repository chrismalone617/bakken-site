<div data-sqsp-text-block-content="">
  <p>An Oregon ethanol plant that went bankrupt within a year of opening is now being used to store and ship Bakken crude oil.
The plant was built at a cost of $200 million and was supported by $36 million in green loans and tax credits. The plant was purchased by Global Partners of Massachusetts in February for $94.2 million.</p><p>Global Partners is using the facility for crude oil transloading and could restart the ethanol plant if market conditions improve. </p><p>The facility is in the Port of St. Helens' Port Westward industrial park near Clatskanie, OR. The facility began accepting crude oil by rail deliveries late in 2012.</p><p>It's the first major crude oil terminal to begin operations in the area in decades.</p>
