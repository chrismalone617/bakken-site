<div data-sqsp-text-block-content="">
  <p>According to the oil and gas research and consulting firm Wood Mackenzie,&nbsp;$15 billion will be spent on drilling and completion in the Bakken in 2014, second only to the <a target="_blank" href="http://eaglefordshale.com/">Eagle Ford Shale</a>. This year, oil production is expected to reach the 1 million b/d mark, and grow markedly beyond that figure over the next 5 - 6 years.</p><p><strong>Read more</strong>: <a href="http://bakkenshale.com/bsp-news/news/bakken-production-sets-another-record-10000-plus-nd-wells-producing?rq=Bakken%20Production%20Sets%20Another%20Record%20-%20More%20Than%2010%2C000%20ND%20Wells%20Producing">Bakken Production Sets Another Record - More Than 10,000 ND Wells Producing</a></p>
</div>






















</div></div><div data-block-type="31" data-sqsp-block="quote"><div>





















<figure>
  <blockquote data-animation-role="quote" data-animation-override="">
    <span>“</span>We expect Bakken/Three Forks oil production to average 1.1 million barrels a day (b/d) in 2014, growing to 1.7 million b/d in 2020,” says Jonathan Garrett, upstream research analyst for Wood Mackenzie.<span>”</span>
  </blockquote>
  
</figure>
</div></div><div data-block-type="2" data-sqsp-block="text"><div>

<div data-sqsp-text-block-content="">
  <p>The firm's analysis shows the Bakken and Three Forks plays hold close to $118 billion in value. Despite infrastructure concerns, estimates indicate operators will recover more than 20 billion bbls of oil reserves throughout the life of the play.</p><h2>Continental Remains Top Bakken Operator</h2><p>In 2014, Continental Resources remains the top Bakken operator, with more than 1.2 million acres according to Wood Mackenzie. The value of the company's proved reserves in the Bakken are ~ $14.5 billion.</p><p><strong>Read more</strong>: <a href="http://bakkenshale.com/bsp-news/news/continentals-proved-reserves-in-bakken-valued-at-14-5-billion-2013">Continentals Proved Reserves in the Bakken Valued at $14.5 Billion</a></p><p>In 2013, Continental conducted multiple tests of the Three Forks . Wood Mackenzie indicates the company has the most advanced delineation program in the deeper Three Forks benches.</p><h2>Wood Mackenzie Analysis of Bakken Three Forks Sub-Plays</h2><p>Current figures show the highest initial production rates are on the Nesson Anticline sub-play, with 30-day IP rates of 1000 boe/d. Estimated ultimate recovery (EUR) rates are the highest in the Fort Berthold sub-play at nearly 700,000 barrels of oil equivalent (boe).</p><p>In the Southern fringe sub-play, wells targeting the Three Forks formation outperformed Bakken wells. Similar results for Three Forks wells were also found in the North Williston and Williams Perimeter sub-plays.</p><p>Wood Mackenzie also indicates in their report that infrastructure constraints will not impact the pace of the play's development. Currently, 73% of crude leaves the Williston Basin by rail.</p><p>Read more at <a target="_blank" href="http://public.woodmac.com/public/media-centre/content/11997283">woodmac.com</a></p>
