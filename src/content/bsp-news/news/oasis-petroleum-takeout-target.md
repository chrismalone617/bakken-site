  <p>Location, location, location. It's a good time to have an interest in the Bakken and Three Forks.</p><p>A recent article by Bloomberg indicates Oasis Petroleum's <a href="/bsp-news/news/oasis-petroleums-bakken-deal-grows-acreage-position-50-1-5-billion">recent Bakken acquisition </a>makes the company even more attractive to buyers. Oasis acquired 161,000 acres through four separate transactions and now controls ~492,000 net acres prospective for Bakken drilling.</p><p>A few highlights from the article include:</p><ul><li>European companies like Statoil and Repsol might be interested in expanding their shale footprint in the U.S.</li><li>Oasis offers "core acreage in very good areas."</li><li>The company's large Bakken position signals a "commitment to be a pure-play", which is attractive to buyers</li><li>Larger drilling inventory makes the company more attractive</li><li>Oasis is trading ~70% cheaper than other U.S. explorers based on EBITDA multiples</li></ul><p>Read more in the article published at <a target="_blank" href="http://www.bloomberg.com/news/2013-09-19/oasis-expanding-in-bakken-seen-luring-buyers-real-m-a.html">bloomberg.com</a></p>
