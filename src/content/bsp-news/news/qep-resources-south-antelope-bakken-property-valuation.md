<div data-sqsp-text-block-content="">
  <p>QEP Resources acquired its South Antelope Properties in the fall of 2012 for ~$1.4 billion.</p><p>Since the acquisition, QEP's value estimate for those properties has increased to $2.8 billion based on probable reserve estimates.</p><p><strong>Read more</strong>: <a href="http://bakkenshale.com/bsp-news/news/qep-helis-bakken-deal">QEP - Helis Bakken Deal Agreed for 27,600 Acres for $1.4 Billion</a></p><h2>QEP Bakken South Antelope Properties</h2><p>Since the acquisition of its South Antelope properties, QEP has lowered its development costs and increased production.</p><p>Current gross completed well costs have decreased by more than $1 million from estimated costs at the time of acquisition.</p><p>In spite of delays due to downstream and weather-related issues, current South Antelope oil production grew in 2013.</p>

  <h2>QEP Production and Proved Reserves for 2013</h2><p>QEP total equivalent production in 2013 was 309 bcfe and oil production was 10.2 million bbl. Natural gas and NGL production was 218.9 bcf and 4.8 million bbl respectively.</p><p>2013 year end total proved reserves were 2.55 tcf of natural gas, 148.6 million bbl of crude oil and 102.6 million bbl of NGL. That's a 37% increase of total proved reserves at year end 2013 compared to 2012.</p><p>At the end of 2013, QEP's Williston Basin proved reserves were estimated at 797.5 bcfe or ~140 million boe.</p><h2>Highlights</h2><ul><li>QEP Resources South Antelope property valued at $2.8 billion</li><li>QEP total equivalent production in 2013 was 309 bcfe and oil production was 10.2 million bbl</li><li>Proved crude oil reserves at 149 million barrels. Up 25% from prior year</li><li>37% increase in total proved reserves</li><li>Williston Basin contributed more than 140 million boe in reserves</li></ul><p><strong>* Extensions and Discoveries:</strong>&nbsp;As to any period, the increases to proved reserves from all sources other than the acquisition of proved properties or revisions of previous estimates.</p><p>Read more at <a target="_blank" href="http://phx.corporate-ir.net/phoenix.zhtml?c=237732&amp;p=irol-pressArticle&amp;ID=1896511&amp;highlight=">QEP.com</a></p>
