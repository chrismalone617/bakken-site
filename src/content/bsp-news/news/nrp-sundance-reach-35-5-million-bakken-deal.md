<div data-sqsp-text-block-content="">
  <p>Natural Resource Partners (NRP) has agreed to acquire non-operated working interests in the Bakken from Sundance Energy for $35.5 million.&nbsp;</p>
</div>






















</div></div><div data-block-type="31" data-sqsp-block="quote"><div>





















<figure>
  <blockquote data-animation-role="quote" data-animation-override="">
    <span>“</span>This is NRP’s second acquisition of producing, non-operated working interests in the Bakken/Three Forks play, which will grow and further diversify NRP’s revenue going forward.<span>”</span>
  </blockquote>
  <figcaption>— Nick Carter, NRP's COO.</figcaption>
</figure>
</div></div><div data-block-type="2" data-sqsp-block="text"><div>

<div data-sqsp-text-block-content="">
  <p>The deal includes 77 producing wells and associated acreage in Dunn, McKenzie, and Mountrail counties. All leases are held by production. NRP plans to participate in future development on the properties.</p><p>Read the full release at <a target="_blank" href="http://phx.corporate-ir.net/phoenix.zhtml?c=135162&amp;p=irol-newsArticle&amp;ID=1870519&amp;highlight=">nrplp.com</a></p>
