<div data-sqsp-text-block-content="">
  <p>ConocoPhillips' 2014 budget includes spending $16.7 billion, with 55% of the total allocated to North America.</p><p>Within North America, Conoco expects continued growth from the Eagle Ford, Bakken, and Permian plays.</p><p>Approximately $4.3 billion will be spent on development drilling in the Lower 48 states. The budget includes<strong> increased investment</strong> in the drilling programs in the Eagle Ford, Bakken and Permian.</p>
</div>






















</div></div><div data-block-type="31" data-sqsp-block="quote"><div>





















<figure>
  <blockquote data-animation-role="quote" data-animation-override="">
    <span>“</span>2014 is an important year for ConocoPhillips,” said Ryan Lance, chairman and chief executive officer. “Since becoming an independent E&amp;P company, we have set out to deliver a unique value proposition of 3 to 5 percent volume and margin growth with a compelling dividend.<span>”</span>
  </blockquote>
  
</figure>
</div></div><div data-block-type="2" data-sqsp-block="text"><div>

<div data-sqsp-text-block-content="">
  <p>Conoco has a five-year plan to spend $5 billion in the Bakken to grow production to more than 50,000 boe/d by 2017. The company has over 626,000 acres prospective for the Bakken and estimates a drilling inventory of more than 1,400 wells.</p><p>Read more at <a target="_blank" href="http://www.conocophillips.com/newsroom/pages/news-releases.aspx?docid=1996734">conocophillips.com</a></p>
