<div data-sqsp-text-block-content="">
  <p>As the free-fall in crude prices continues, major Bakken operators are expressing their caution by slashing their budgets for 2015.</p><p>Marathon Oil is the latest giant to announce that its projected budget will <a target="_blank" href="http://fuelfix.com/blog/2014/12/17/marathon-oil-announces-20-percent-budget-cut/">curb exploration spending </a>by a whopping 20% for 2015. Though the company still forecasts spending in upwards of $4.4 billion, the decrease is another sign that the spiraling oil prices are casting a dark shadow over the incredible growth that is taking place in the shale regions.</p>
</div>






















</div></div><div data-block-type="31" data-sqsp-block="quote"><div>





















<figure>
  <blockquote data-animation-role="quote" data-animation-override="">
    <span>“</span>We remain confident in our investment opportunities in the three U.S. resource plays,” Marathon Oil President and Chief Executive Officer Lee Tillman said in a statement. “Our 2015 capital program is not opportunity constrained but will reflect sound discipline in managing cash flows in the current price environment.<span>”</span>
  </blockquote>
  
</figure>
</div></div><div data-block-type="2" data-sqsp-block="text"><div>

<div data-sqsp-text-block-content="">
  <p>Related: <a target="_blank" href="http://eaglefordshale.com/news/eagle-ford-and-bakken-drilling-permits-fall-30/">Eagle Ford and Bakken Drilling Permits Fall 30%</a></p><p>This news comes as oil plunged Thursday to an incredible $54.11, prices not seen since 2009. And Marathon is not the only E&amp;P company to back off on growth plans. ConocoPhillips is also shaving 20% off for 2015, by deferring investment in unconventional plays. The company, however, is continuing to affirm its commitment to Bakken region, which continues to be a major source of growth for the company. Bakken’s largest operator, Continental Resources, also seems to be getting skittish as it announced Wednesday it will reduce spending in 2015 by $600 million and delay new rig starts while they wait out the current situation.</p><p><strong><a target="_blank" href="http://www.reuters.com/article/2014/12/10/capex-crude-idUSL3N0TU3SN20141210">For more on 2015 budgets visit Reuters.com</a></strong></p>
