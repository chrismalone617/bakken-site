<div data-sqsp-text-block-content="">
  <p>North Dakota lawmakers are working to eliminate the reporting requirement on certain oil spills.</p><p><a href="/bsp-news/news/two-saltwater-leaks-in-north-dakota?rq=north%20dakota%20spill">Related: Oil Spill Threatens Wetlands</a></p><p>Late last month, the North Dakota House legislators voted in favor of House Bill 1151, which eliminates a reporting requirement for oil spills under 10 barrels. The 82-11 vote was a clear victory for companies who say the measure will save them time and money.&nbsp;</p><p>Removing reporting requirements for large spills will also ease the administrative burden of the state. <a target="_blank" href="http://bakken.com/news/id/257290/north-dakota-house-passes-new-rule-for-reporting-oil-spills/">One analyst </a>estimates that 80 employees receive an email every time there is a spill.&nbsp;</p><p>If the bill is passed, companies will no longer need to report large spills of natural gas, crude oil or the water water used in production. They define &nbsp;are less than 10 barrels, or 420 gallons.</p>
</div>
