  <p>Continental Resources announces first quarter results that highlights increases in production and reduced costs.</p><p>Related:&nbsp;<a href="http://bakkenshale.com/bsp-news/news/harold-hamm-gains-more-bakken-acreage?rq=Harold%20Hamm%20Gains%20More%20Bakken%20Acreage">Harold Hamm Gains More Bakken Acreage</a></p><p>In a press release on May 6th, Continental reported a net loss of $132.0 million for the first quarter of 2015 and a production increase to 206,829 boed.</p><p>Most impressive is the company’s drilling and completion costs , which fell by 15% since 2014 year end. The company expects to see service cost reductions of up to 20% by mid-year and further savings from drilling and completion efficiencies. One example of these efficiencies is the new company record for drilling in the Bakken as they report drilling the two-mile lateral portion of a well in three days, nearly four days faster than its average time to drill a lateral.</p><p><strong>Bakken Highlights for First Quarter</strong></p><ul><li>Production averaged 135,538 Boe per day (39% increase over first quarter 2014)</li><li>Completed 66 net wells</li><li>Operated an average of 13 rigs in the Bakken, down from 19 rigs at year-end 2014</li><li>Significantly reduced its completion crew count</li><li>115 gross operated Bakken wells drilled and waiting on first production (122 at year-end 2014)</li></ul><p><strong>Continental’s plans for Bakken moving forward</strong></p><ul><li>The Company plans to average 10 operated rigs through the remainder of 2015, based on current market conditions.</li><li>The Company expects to have approximately 90 gross operated Bakken wells drilled and waiting on first production at year-end 2015.</li><li>Approximately 60% of the wells in the 2015 program will be drilled on 660-foot to 880-foot inter-well spacing in the Middle Bakken and Three Forks reservoirs.</li><li>The Company's 2015 Bakken drilling program is targeting an average EUR of approximately 800,000 Boe per well.</li></ul><p>Red more at <a target="_blank" href="http://investors.clr.com/phoenix.zhtml?c=197380&amp;p=irol-newsArticle&amp;ID=2044975">clr.com</a></p>
