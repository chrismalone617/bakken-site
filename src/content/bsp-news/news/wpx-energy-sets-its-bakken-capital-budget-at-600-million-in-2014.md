<div data-sqsp-text-block-content="">
  <p>WPX Energy will spend between $1.420-$1.523 billion in 2014, with $580-600 million directed to Bakken and Three Forks targets in the Williston Basin.</p><p>WPX is planning to add a rig to bring its operated total to five in 2014.</p><p>The company expects the added rig will allow the company to drill 62 gross operated wells, or 25% more than the ~50 wells the company drilled in 2013.</p><p><strong>Also read:</strong><a href="http://bakkenshale.com/bsp-news/news/halcon-holds-production-guidance-lowers-2014-capital-budget">Halcon Lowers Its 2014 Capital Budget &amp; Holds Production Guidance</a></p>
</div>

  </blockquote>
  
</figure>
</div></div><div data-block-type="2" data-sqsp-block="text"><div>

<div data-sqsp-text-block-content="">
  <p>The emphasis in liquids producing areas will drive production growth in 2014. WPX expects daily oil production from the Williston and San Juan to increase ~50% from 16,000 b/d in the first quarter to more than 24,000 b/d by year-end.</p><p>In total, 85% of the company's capital budget is split between liquids producing plays in the Piceance, San Juan, and Williston basins.</p><p>WPX's capital budget:</p><ul><li>$580-600 million in the Williston Basin (oil)</li><li>$475-495 million in the Piceance Basin (gas &amp; ngls)</li><li>$155-180 million in the San Juan Basin (oil)</li><li>$20-30 million in the Appalachia region</li><li>$10-15 million in legacy areas like the Powder River Basin</li><li>$100-115 million on land and exploration</li></ul><p>Read the full press release at <a target="_blank" href="http://www.wpxenergy.com/news-media/press-releases/2014/210_wpx-energy-provides-2014-capital-plan-and-guidance.aspx">wpxenergy.com</a></p>
