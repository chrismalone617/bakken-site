<div data-sqsp-text-block-content="">
  <p>There were $1.8 billion in Bakken deals in the third quarter and that led all shale plays in terms of dollar value. There were $16.4 billion worth of deals in the third quarter and $5.4 were attributable to assets in shale plays.</p><p>The <a target="_blank" href="http://eaglefordshale.com/">Eagle Ford</a> in South Texas led with seven deals compared to the Bakken's three, but trailed the Bakken in total deal value by $100 million.</p><p>PWC only tracks deals with a value of more than $50 million. We actually covered four deals in the quarter:</p><ul><li><a href="/bsp-news/news/oasis-petroleums-bakken-deal-grows-acreage-position-50-1-5-billion">Oasis Acquires 160,000 Plus Bakken Acres - $1.5 Billion</a></li><li><a href="/bsp-news/news/whiting-petroleum-acquires-bakken-acreage-mt-nd">Whiting Petroleum Acquires Bakken Acreage in Montana and North Dakota - $260 million</a></li><li><a href="/bsp-news/news/samson-oil-gas-selling-bakken-assets">Samson Sells Bakken Assets in Montana - $13.5 million</a></li><li><a href="/bsp-news/news/halcon-resolute-bakken-acreage-williams-county">Halcon Buys Resolute Energy's Williams County Bakken Acreage - $75 million</a></li></ul><p>Read the full oil and gas divestitures report at <a target="_blank" href="http://www.pwc.com/us/en/press-releases/2013/divestitures-continue-to-drive-us-oil-and-gas.jhtml">pwc.com</a></p>
