  <p>Whiting Petroleum announces solid first quarter results for Bakken region.</p><p>In its Q1 press release, Whiting reported production totaling 15.0 million barrels of oil equivalent (MMBOE), 88% crude oil/natural gas liquids (NGLs). Production averaged 166,930 barrels of oil equivalent per day (BOE/d), a 3% increase over the fourth quarter 2014.</p><p>Whiting’s budget remains at $2 billion with capital expenditures expected to decline sharply in the second half of 2015.</p>
</div>

    <span>“</span>James J. Volker, Whiting’s Chairman, President and CEO, commented, “While we are reducing rig count and well cost, production was strong in Q1 2015. We had solid results in the Bakken/Three Forks and Niobrara. Our total rig count will average 11 rigs in the second half of 2015. Nine of these rigs will operate in the Bakken/Three Forks.<span>”</span>
