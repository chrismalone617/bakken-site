<div data-sqsp-text-block-content="">
  <p>Bakken Shale oil producers are under pressure to scale back 2015 drilling plans due to falling oil prices, according to <a target="_blank" href="http://www.bloomberg.com/news/2014-10-13/bakken-drillers-poised-to-curb-exploratory-spending.html">Bloomberg</a>.&nbsp;On October 13th,&nbsp;Houston-based Plains Marketing L.P. listed Williston Basin sweet crude at $67.69, which is a ~$4 drop from last week's price.</p><p>Crude oil prices have been on the decline worldwide. Surging production from U.S. shale plays are one of the reasons behind the worldwide oil price drop. Other factors include a weakening global demand for oil and an increase in Libyan oil drilling over the summer, according to the <a target="_blank" href="http://www.eia.gov/todayinenergy/detail.cfm?id=18311">Energy Information Administration (EIA)</a>.</p><p>North Dakota’s Department of Mineral Resources (DMR) Director Lynn Helms updated lawmakers on the status of oil &amp; gas development in the state last week. Helms said two factors could negatively impact oil production – lower oil prices and new flaring regulations.</p><p><strong>Read more</strong>: <a href="/bsp-news/news/bakken-development-threats-on-the-horizon?rq=Bakken%20Development%20Threats%20on%20the%20Horizon">Bakken Development Threats on the Horizon</a></p><p>Lower prices doesn't mean production growth will stop in North Dakota for the immediate future. In April of 2014, consultancy <a target="_blank" href="http://public.woodmac.com/public/media-centre/content/11997283">Wood Mackenzie</a>, predicted the play will hit 1.7-million b/d by 2020. Currently, oil production in North Dakota is ~1.1-million b/d.</p><p>Growth in the Bakken region has been marked by a sharp up tick in infill drilling and pad drilling to increase efficiencies and the recovery of hydrocarbons. In January of 2010, just a few years after the boom began, there were &nbsp;about 4,600 producing wells in North Dakota. Now, there are more than 11,000 producing wells in the state.</p><p>If oil prices remain low or stay flat, operators will scale back drilling across the Bakken.&nbsp;Look for operators to focus mostly on the sweet spots in core areas located in <a href="/counties/mckenzie-county-nd">McKenzie</a>, <a href="/counties/williams-county-nd">Williams</a>, <a href="/counties/mountrail-county-nd">Mountrail</a> and <a href="/counties/dunn-county-nd">Dunn</a> Counties.</p>
