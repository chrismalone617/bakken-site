  <p>Every year, the <em>Oil and Gas Investor</em> honors the companies, deals and individuals who excelled in the North American energy industry for the prior year.</p><p>Honorees for 2014 OGI Excellence Awards will be acknowledged April 6th at Hart’s Energy Capital Conference in Austin. The event draws hundreds of executives from E&amp;P companies, midstream operators, financial institutions and private-equity firms.</p><p><strong>Best Discovery: Harold Hamm</strong> Harold Hamm, chairman and CEO of <em>Continental Resources,</em> will be honored for the year's Best Discovery for development of the Springer Shale oil play in south-central Oklahoma. Results released in September indicate that the play will yield top-notch initial output rates and economic returns.</p><p>More on Continental:&nbsp;<a href="http://bakkenshale.com/bsp-news/news/harold-hamm-gains-more-bakken-acreage?rq=Harold%20Hamm%20Gains%20More%20Bakken%20Acreage"><strong>Harold</strong> Hamm Gains More Bakken Acreage </a></p><p><strong>M&amp;A Deal of the Year </strong>Jim Volker, chairman, CEO and president of <em>Whiting Petroleum</em><em>Corp.,</em> wins the award for M&amp;A Deal of the Year for purchasing Kodiak Oil &amp; Gas in a $6 billion all-stock deal. This deal &nbsp;propelled Whiting to the top-tier position in the Bakken Shale in North Dakota.</p><p>Read more:&nbsp;<a href="http://bakkenshale.com/bsp-news/news/whiting-petroleum-acquires-kodiak-oil-gas-3-8-billion">Whiting Petroleum Acquires Kodiak Oil &amp; Gas - $3.8 Billion</a></p><p><strong>Excellence Award Recipients:</strong></p><ul><li><em>Executive of the Year:</em> Chuck Davidson, Noble Energy executive chairman</li><li><em>Financing of the Year:</em> Eclipse Resources Corp.</li><li><em>Best Field Rejuvenation</em>: Shell Oil Co.</li><li><em>Corporate Citizen of the Year: </em>ConocoPhillips</li></ul><p>Fore more go to <a target="_blank" href="http://www.oilandgasinvestor.com/2014-oil-and-gas-investor-excellence-awards-789276">oilandgasinvestor.com</a></p>
