  <p>
Enerplus grew U.S. oil production to more than 20,000 boe/d in the second quarter. Production from the Bakken was the primary driver, with volumes growing to 15,000 boe/d from the company's Fort Berthold assets.</p><p>Enerplus invested $78 million drilling 4.7 net wells and bringing 6.1 net wells to production during the second quarter. Service costs and well costs continue to fall. Enerplus has realized savings of 10% compared to its plan at the beginning of 2013.</p><p>The company's Fort Berthold assets are the primary focus of development. Enerplus has drilled 92 wells to date (74 Bakken &amp; 18 Three Forks) and estimates its acreage holds:</p><ul><li>6 year drilling inventory or 130 undrilled locations</li><li>Downspacing potential at 160 acres per well that could add another 150 undrilled locations</li><li>Potential in the second, third, and fourth benches of the Three Forks.</li></ul><p>Read the full Q2 press release at <a href="http://www.enerplus.com/investor/news/news-releases.cfm?newsReleaseAction=view&amp;releaseId=371" target="_blank">enerplus.com</a></p>
