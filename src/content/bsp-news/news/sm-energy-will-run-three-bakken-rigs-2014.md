SM Energy plans to run three rigs spending $275 million on operated activity and another $75 million on non-operated properties.</p><p>Activity will primarily target the Bear Den, Raven, and Gooseneck areas in North Dakota.</p><p>SM Energy expects to complete 45 gross, operated wells in 2014.</p><p>Tony Best, CEO commented, "Our 2014 growth will be anchored by our core development programs in the Eagle Ford and Bakken-Three Forks, with additional investments being made in our emerging oil programs in the Permian Shales and the Powder River Basin."</p><p>Read more at <a href="http://phx.corporate-ir.net/phoenix.zhtml?c=90687&amp;p=irol-newsArticle&amp;ID=1885894" target="_blank">sm-energy.com</a></p>
