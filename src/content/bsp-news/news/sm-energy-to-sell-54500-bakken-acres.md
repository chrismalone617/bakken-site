<div data-sqsp-text-block-content="">
  <p>SM Energy Company announced today that they are exploring the sale of certain Bakken assets in the Williston Basin.&nbsp;</p><p><a href="http://bakkenshale.com/bsp-news/news/north-dakota-oil-production-at-lowest-level-since-2014">Related: ND Production Lowest Level Since 2014</a></p><p>SM Energy has hired Petrie Partners to explore the sale as part of a strategy to change focus to its 'Tier 1' assets in the Permian Basin and the Eagle Ford Shale in Texas.&nbsp;The sale will include approximately 54,500 net acres consisting of the Raven/Bear Den acreage and effectively all lease-holdings in the basin outside of the Company’s Divide County program in North Dakota.</p>
