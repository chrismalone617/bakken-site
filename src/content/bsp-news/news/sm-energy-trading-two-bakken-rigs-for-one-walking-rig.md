  <p>
SM Energy plans to drop from running four Bakken rigs to three later in the year. Two rig contracts are up and the company will be trading two for one.</p><p>The rig coming in will be a walking rig capable of drilling multi-well pads.</p><p>Bakken production was up 3% on the quarter and 18% from this time last year to 12,200 boe/d. The focus of drilling is in the Raven, Bear Den, and Gooseneck prospects in Divide, McKenzie, and Williams counties in North Dakota.</p><p>SM Energy completed 11 gross wells on its operated acreage during the quarter.</p><p>Read the full press release at <a href="http://phx.corporate-ir.net/phoenix.zhtml?c=90687&amp;p=irol-newsArticle&amp;ID=1813362" target="_blank">sm-energy.com</a></p>
