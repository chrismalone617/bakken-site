<div data-sqsp-text-block-content="">
  <p>Samson Oil and Gas Limited has announced it will sell assets in the Bakken worth $15 million.</p><p><a href="http://bakkenshale.com/bsp-news/news/bakken-bankruptcies-on-the-rise">Related: &nbsp;Bakken Bankruptcies on the Rise</a></p><p>Australian-based, Samson Oil &amp; Gas Ltd. plans to sell its interest in the North Stockyard field in North Dakota in order to reduce its debt.&nbsp;The deal is scheduled to close on August 31st, 2016.</p><p>Angelus Private Equity Group is purchasing the field,&nbsp;located in the Williston Basin in Williams County, N.D., which includes the Mississippian Mission Canyon (Bluell), Mississippian-Devonian Bakken and Devonian Three Forks formations.</p><p>Highlights from the North Stockyard sale include:&nbsp;</p><ol><li>A reduction of our debt level</li><li>Providing cash liquidity that can be invested in the Foreman Butte project which, as illustrated by our recent reserve report has considerable growth potential, with more than $50 million of proved developed non-producing and proved undeveloped reserves.</li><li>Maintaining compliance with our debt covenants.</li><li>Maintaining the excellent working relationship that we have with our lender.</li></ol><p>Last year, Samson shifted its focus to the Foreman Butte project, which involves wells in both Montana and North Dakota.&nbsp;</p><p><a href="http://www.samsonoilandgas.com/irm/PDF/2717/SaleofNorthStockyardandAmendmentofDebtFacility">Read more at samsoilandgas.com</a></p><p>&nbsp;</p><p>&nbsp;</p><p>&nbsp;</p>
