  <p>
SM Energy grew Bakken production volumes from 13,700 boe/d in the second quarter to 14,900 boe/d in the third quarter.</p><p>The company completed 13 gross wells and is producing 35% more than it did at this time last year.</p><p>SM has three rigs running, with a focus in the Gooseneck, and Raven/Bear Den areas. SM Energy has 162,000 net acres prospective for the Bakken and Three Forks, but is focused on development of 81,000 net acres.</p><p>The company made two comments related to testing in the play:</p><ul><li>Downspacing results will be considered in planning at year-end</li><li>Evaluating alternate completion designs being used in the play - Current design utilizes a 26-stage sliding sleeve completion and 80,000 barrels of fluid.</li></ul><p>Read the company's full third quarter press release at <a href="http://phx.corporate-ir.net/phoenix.zhtml?c=90687&amp;p=irol-newsArticle&amp;ID=1869916" target="_blank">sm-energy.com</a></p>
