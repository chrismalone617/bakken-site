  <p>We in full development mode in the Bakken, but don't expect smooth sailing from here on out. That was was the basic message from the Department of Mineral Resources Director Lynn Helms at the North Dakota Petroleum Council's annual meeting. A few of threats to development include:</p><ul><li>Federal regulations such as new rules around hydraulic fracturing</li><li>A lack of light-sweet crude refining capacity</li></ul><p>The commission is also working to help reduce natural gas flaring.</p>
