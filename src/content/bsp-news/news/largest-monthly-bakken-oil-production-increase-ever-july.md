<div data-sqsp-text-block-content="">
  <p>Bakken oil production reported in July is both a new record and a <em>broken record</em> in North Dakota.</p><p>Oil production in North Dakota grew by almost 53,000 b/d in a single month. The <strong>increase from June to July represents the largest monthly increase in production in the state's history</strong>.</p><p>Preliminary estimates peg state production at 874,460 b/d and 970 mmcfd (both records). The Bakken and Three Forks contributed 810,795 b/d.</p><p>The increase was primarily driven by an increase from 102 well completions in June to 251 well completions in July. Less than 100 completions are needed to sustain production.</p><p>Production has only increased more than 40,000 b/d in a single month twice and the biggest increase before now was ~41,000 b/d in February.</p>
