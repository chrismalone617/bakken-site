<div data-sqsp-text-block-content="">
  <p>Kodiak Oil &amp; Gas has signed a purchase and sale agreement with Liberty Resources for 42,000 net acres in the Williston Basin for $660 million.</p><p>The acquisition includes acreage prospective for the Bakken and Three Forks formations in McKenzie and Williams counties.</p><p>In total, a portion of 35 (1,280-acre) spacing units are included in the deal.</p><ul><li>14,000 net acres adjacent to the Polar Area</li><li>25,000 net acres west of the Koala &amp; Smokey areas</li></ul><p>Production from the assets averaged 5,700 b/d in May of 2013. Kodiak will also assume a rig contract that has 14 months remaining.</p><p>After closing, Kodiak will control 196,000 net acres in the Bakken.</p>
