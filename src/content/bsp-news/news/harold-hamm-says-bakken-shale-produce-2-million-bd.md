  <p>Harold Hamm followed reports from the NDIC that production had surpassed 900,000 b/d with a prediction that the Bakken will ultimately produce 2 million b/d.</p><p>Read more: <a href="http://bakkenshale.com/bsp-news/news/north-dakota-reports-record-production-volumes-august-2013?rq=North%20Dakota%20Reports%20Record%20Production%20Volumes%20in%20August%202013">North Dakota Reports Record Production Volumes in August 2013</a></p><p>His company, Continental Resources, controls over 1 million acres and produces approximately 80,000 boe/d from the Bakken.</p><p>A few other notable comments included:</p><ul><li>We're 75% of the way to fully optimizing the Bakken</li><li>He believes our borders should be opened to oil exports</li><li>He doesn't expect an oil price crash. Oil is simply harder to find and produce than natural gas</li><li>Oil prices will remain in the $90-100 bbl range</li><li>The chances Keystone XL will be built are dwindling</li></ul><p>Mr. Hamm was speaking at the Bloomberg Oil &amp; Gas Conference held October 17, 2013 in Houston.</p>
