<div data-sqsp-text-block-content="">
  <p>Oasis Petroleum set its 2014 capital budget at $1,425 million, with 96% of expenditures ($1,367 million) earmarked for drilling and completion of its operated and non-operated wells.</p><p>The company saw production growth jump ~50% in 2013 from its' 2012 figures, and expects continued growth in 2014.</p><h2>Oasis Anticipates Growing Production in 2014</h2><p>Oasis plans on increasing production to 46,000 boed/d - 50,000 boe/d by the end of the year.</p><p>If the company reaches it's midpoint estimate of 48,000 boe/d, then that will be ~42% higher than total production of 33,904 boe/d in 2013.</p><h2>Oasis Petroleum Divestiture of Bakken Acreage</h2><p>A portion of Oasis's capital budget for 2014 will come from a $333 million sale in January of its' non-operated Sanish properties and a few non-operated leases adjacent to the Sanish.</p><p>Whiting Petroleum is believed to be the operator of the Sanish properties, and would be a <em>logical</em> buyer, although, Oasis has not disclosed any information about the purchaser.</p>
</div>






















</div></div><div data-block-type="31" data-sqsp-block="quote"><div>

The transaction is consistent with our growth strategy as we de-lever the balance sheet and put the capital into our high return operated projects.<span>”</span>
  </blockquote>
  
</figure>
</div></div><div data-block-type="2" data-sqsp-block="text"><div>

<div data-sqsp-text-block-content="">
  <p>Properties ear-marked in the sale are 8,354 net acres and 28.2 net producing Bakken and Three Forks wells.&nbsp; Production from the properties was 2,691 boe/d during Q4 2013.</p><p>In 2013, Oasis grew its Bakken acreage to 515,314 total net acres from 335,383 net acres in 2012. With ample running room, non-operated and non-core properties become a fit for divesting. The company generated cash that can be used immediately and saved future development expenses that it can directed toward operated wells and acreage.</p><h2>Oasis at a Glance in 2014</h2><ul><li>$1,425 million capital budget ($1,367 million for drilling and completing wells)</li><li>42% production increase to 46,000 boed/d - 50,000 boe/d expected by the end of 2014</li><li>$333 million sale of 8,354 net acres in January 2014</li><li>Increase total operated rigs from 14 - 16 by second half of 2014</li><li>Complete approximately 205 gross (147.8 net) operated and 7.7 net non-operated wells</li></ul>
