<div data-sqsp-text-block-content="">
  <p>Halcon Resources lowered its capital budget for 2014 and is keeping its production guidance the same (38,000-42,000 boe/d).</p><p>Approximately $125 million will be spent on leasehold, infrastructure, and seismic. Halcon also plans to divest $300-400 million in properties in 2014.</p><p>Halcon originally planned to spend more than $1 billion, but has lowered its budget 14% to $950 million.</p>
</div>
