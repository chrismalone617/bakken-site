  <p>Northern Oil and Gas will ramp up activity in the Bakken Shale in 2017.</p><p>Related:&nbsp;<a href="/bsp-news/news/continental">Continental Plans 26% Growth for Bakken</a></p><p>In a recent press release, Northern executives announced a 2017 capital budget of $102.2 million. The company will drill 12 new wells in the Bakken Shale during 2017 and expects total annual production to equal or exceed 2016.</p><h2>Fourth Quarter and Full Year Highlights</h2><ul><li>Q4 Net loss was $12.3 million ($172.3 million in Q4 2015)</li><li>2016 net loss of $293.5 million ($975.4 million in 2015)</li><li>Average 90-day initial production rate increased 47% in 2016</li><li>Completed $8.9 million property acquisition in the Q4&nbsp;</li><li>2016 capital expenditures totaled $75.6 million (41.3% decrease over 2015)</li><li>Q4 production totaled 1,259,274 barrels of oil equivalent ("Boe")&nbsp;</li><li>End of year: $212.5 million of liquidity</li></ul>
</div>

    <span>“</span>We continue to see an increase in deal flow kind of coming in smaller package sizes like that, so we’ll continue to stay aggressively in the market doing that. With the liquidity, I think you’ll see us pretty much ramp up with respect to the activity, I guess, in the Bakken. When you take a look at our large acreage position of over 154,000 net acres, it’s a good acreage position located in great parts of the basin.<span>”</span>
