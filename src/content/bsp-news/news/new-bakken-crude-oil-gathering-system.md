<div data-sqsp-text-block-content="">
  <p>Enable Midstream Partners announced last week that it has completed its first crude oil and produced water gathering system in North Dakota.</p><p>The project began in August of 2013 to service XTO Energy Inc, a major Bakken producer.&nbsp;The Bear Den Crude &amp; Produced Water Gathering System system is located in Dunn Co, N.D. and now that it is fully operational, will a maximum throughput of 19,500 barrels of oil per day.</p><p><a target="_blank" href="http://www.mineralrightsforum.com/group/bakken-shale-dunn-county-nd">Related: News &amp; discussion with mineral owners in Dunn Co, N.D.</a></p>

<div data-sqsp-text-block-content="">
  <p>In the oil and gas industry, a gathering system includes both the flowline networks and the process facilities. It is through this system that oil and/or gas is transported and controlled from the well site to a main storage facility, processing plant, or shipping point.</p><p><a target="_blank" href="http://www.mineralweb.com/owners-guide/oil-and-gas-basics-for-mineral-owners/">Read more about oil &amp; gas 101</a></p><p>Enable Midstream serves major markets by developing and operating natural gas and crude oil infrastructure. The company’s operations include:</p><ul><li>11,000 miles of gathering pipelines</li><li>12 processing plants that process ~2.1 billion cubic feet per day</li><li>~ 7,900 miles of interstate pipelines with 8.4 billion cubic feet per day of transport capacity</li><li>~ 86.5 billion cubic feet of natural gas storage capacity</li><li>~ 2300 intrastate pipelines</li></ul><p>Read ore at <a target="_blank" href="http://enablemidstream.investorhq.businesswire.com/press-release/company-news/enable-midstream-announces-completion-bakken-crude-oil-gathering-system">enablemidstream.com</a></p>
