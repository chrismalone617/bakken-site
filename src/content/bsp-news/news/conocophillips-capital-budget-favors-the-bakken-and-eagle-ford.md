<div data-sqsp-text-block-content="">
  <p>ConocoPhillips released plans to spend $15.8 billion in 2013. Of that, more $4 billion will be spent across the company's U.S. assets in the <a href="http://bakkenshale.com/">Bakken</a>, Barnett, Eagle Ford, Niobrara, and Permian Basin areas. Targets in all of those areas are liquids-rich.</p><p>If 2012 is a good indication of what to expect in 2013, Conoco will likely spend 15-20% of the $4 billion allocated for the U.S. in the Bakken. In 2012, the company spent approximately $600 million developing the play.</p><p>Conoco's believes its 600,000 + acres in the Bakken will yield:</p><ul><li>1,300 gross proved and probably drilling locations</li><li>400 million barrels of resources from just 40% of its acreage</li><li>Production of almost 40,000 boe/d in 2016</li></ul><p>Read the full press release at <a target="_blank" href="http://www.conocophillips.com/EN/newsroom/news_releases/2012NewsReleases/Pages/12-07-2012.aspx">conocophillips.com</a></p>
