<div data-sqsp-text-block-content="">
  <p>Marathon Oil averaged 25 days from spud to spud in the first quarter and that time improved to 22 days in the second quarter. The company's spud to total depth for each well fell to 15 days.</p><p>Marathon hit total depth on 22 wells and brought 16 wells to production. That compares to 18 wells drilled and 22 wells brought to production in the first quarter.</p><p>The company's Bakken production grew 5% from 37,000 boe/d to 39,000 boe/d in the second quarter.</p>
