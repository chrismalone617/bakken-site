  <p>Kodiak Oil &amp; Gas' Bakken production is up more than 7% to ~36,500 boe/d since July 23rd.</p><p>Kodiak is the eight largest producer in the Bakken and the increase should be no surprise. The company acquired <a href="/bsp-news/news/kodiak-buying-bakken-assets-from-liberty-resources-for-660-million">assets from Liberty Resources for $660 million</a> and increased spending in 2013 shortly thereafter.</p><p>Read more at <a target="_blank" href="http://www.reuters.com/article/2013/09/11/kodiak-production-idUSL2N0H711C20130911">reuters.com</a></p>
