<div data-sqsp-text-block-content="">
  <p>
Marathon Oil is drilling a horizontal well in the Tyler formation in Slope County this month.</p><p>The formation has produced from vertical wells for years and is comparable to the Bakken from a geology perspective. Both plays include a limestone formation sandwiched by shales.</p><p>Marathon has a four well development plan approved and believes the wells could yield an average of 400,000 boe each. That's comparable to Bakken production rates.</p><p>It's very early, but watch for additional announcements. It might be a while before we get well results, but Marathon and possibly others will expand their testing plans if the wells prove successful.</p>
