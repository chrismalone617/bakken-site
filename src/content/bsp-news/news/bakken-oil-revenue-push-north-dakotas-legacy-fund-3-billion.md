<div data-sqsp-text-block-content="">
  <p>North Dakota first proposed the Legacy Fund in 2008 and legislators approved its creation in 2010. The legislature expected deposits might reach $600 million dollars by the summer of 2013. <strong>Instead, the fund is sitting on $1.3 billion</strong> and could very well surpass $3 billion by the time lawmakers determine the best use of the funds.</p><p>30% of annual oil extraction and production taxes are directed to the fund and spending is restricted through 2017. Even then, spending has to be approved by a two-thirds vote and no more than 15% of the fund can be spent in any two year budget cycle.</p>
