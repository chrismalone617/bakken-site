<div data-sqsp-text-block-content="">
  <p>
North Dakota oil production is on pace to eclipse 1 million b/d by year-end. The state reported production of ~932,000 b/d in September and is expected to add between 20,000 and 30,000 b/d each month going forward.</p><p>ND natural gas production set a new record at 1.06 bcf/d.</p><p>The state should also eclipse 10,000 producing wells before year-end. The real risk to any of the above trends is snow and cold weather.</p><p>Other takeaways include:</p><ul><li>207 wells were completed in September - up from 153 in August</li><li>One well is getting completed for every 1.5 drilled</li><li>Time from spud to first production held at ~100 days</li><li>&gt;95% of drilling targets the Bakken and Three Forks</li><li>Natural gas prices near Watford City are trading near $3/mcf</li></ul><p>Read the full director's cut at <a href="https://www.dmr.nd.gov/oilgas/directorscut/directorscut-2013-11-15.pdf" target="_blank">dmr.nd.gov</a></p>
